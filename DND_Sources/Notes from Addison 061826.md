##### \[\[Doll of the Fallen Soldier]] (Lvl 5 Upgrade)

* HP = 6d8 + CON mod + STR mod + Proficiency Bonus.,
* Doll has the Animated Armorstat block.,
* Your Doll has 3 charges per day that recharge over a long rest - instinctual understanding that the summoning magic will not be impossible but may have chaotic consequences if you attempt to spend a " 4th charge".,
* Attacks against you have disadvantage while the Doll is within 5 feet of you. The Doll enters initiative when summoned and takes it's first turn immediately.,
* The Doll has full movement while carrying you.,
* All effects end if the Doll reaches 0HP, but the Doll possesses Orcish Endurance.,



##### Abilities (Priorities) of the Doll:

* ###### Get up: 

1 charge - Triggers automatically when you reach 0HP the second time - Doll enlarges and uses its full movement and the dash action to pick you up and carry you the shortest route away from danger.,

* ###### Get the Fey-k Out: 

2 charges - Doll uses your Fey Step ability (Fey-Touched) to fey-step you or an ally the Doll can touch to an area you can see within range of the spell.,

* ###### Fight Me: 

1-2 charges - Doll enlarges and casts Compel Duel on a single enemy. Can expend a 2nd charge to cast it on a second enemy.,

* ###### I. said. Get. UP.: 

Uses all remaining charges (2 minimum) - Doll heals you for 2d8 +  prof. bonus + STR + CON mod, and uses Second Wind to enlarge and either move you or take one action, including Compel Duel and Fey Step,

##### \[\[Shapeshifter's Bane]] - +1 Magical Greatsword

* Requires attunement by a Battlemaster Fighter - Soul Bound to Kit Aluri,
* Once per day, You can use your bonus action or burn a superiority die to grant the greatsword the properties and appearance of another melee or thrown weapon in your inventory or one that you have previously wielded.  The greatsword cannot shift into shields or ammunition-based weapons.,
* The greatsword can shift into other magical weapons in your inventory or that you are currently attuned to, but magical affects do not stack.,
* On a hit while the weapon is in a different form, the weapon may either do base greatsword damage OR damage type and die of the shifted weapon.,
* When Duel-wielding with this weapon shifted into to a one-handed weapon, attacks require a STR saving throw (DC20-STR modifier).,
* You can use bonus action to re-summon the greatsword back to you as long as you can see it.

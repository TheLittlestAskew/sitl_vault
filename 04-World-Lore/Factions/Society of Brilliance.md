# Society of Brilliance

> **Type:** Loose order of intelligent Underdark monsters who investigate faerzress/chaos anomalies and prefer diplomacy.

## Overview

A group of unusually intelligent, well-spoken Underdark creatures who travel the depths studying the worsening chaotic energy (faerzress corruption). First named to the party by the dying [[Jorlan Duskryn]] (S14); first met on-screen in [[Session 17 — The Big Fish Eat the Little Fish|Session 18]] in the person of [[Sloopidoop]]. They prefer diplomacy and bargaining over force, and operate transactionally.

## Known / Reported Members

- **[[Sloopidoop]]** — Kuo-toa scholar-priest; the party's first contact (S18). Worships "knowledge" / the Sea Mother, not Lolth.
- **[[Wazalax]]** — Mind flayer *(name unresolved: "Wazalax"/"Grazilax"/"Rasa Lax"; possibly Jorlan's "Grazilaxx," S14)*. Named by Sloopidoop (S18); not seen.
- Per Jorlan's S14 intel: also **a derro, a troglodyte, and a kuo-toa** among the membership.

## Goals & Lore

- **Primary goal (S18):** Studying the origin of the intensifying chaotic energies that drive Underdark madness. Sloopidoop suspects a **demonic** origin; the party cautiously offered a competing **fey-dimension / death-imbalance** theory (tied to archfey patron [[Silofirst]]).
- **The chaos is worsening (S18)** — explicitly intensifying, tying the campaign's madness mechanic to a faction-level mystery.

## Relationship with the Party

- **New — transactional ally (S18):** Mutual-aid bargain struck with [[Sloopidoop]] — escort him to [[Sloobludop]] and help defend the village against the chaos, in exchange for knowledge and aid (including loot identification). Sloopidoop gave a village-access bead as a token of trust.
- Vehemently anti-[[Lolth]] (at least per Sloopidoop), a possible point of alignment with the party.

# The Bone Teeth

> A field of jagged rock and stalagmites rising out of [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]], with a whirlpool at its mouth. The party's first sea hazard — and where [[Nanny Plunk]] was waiting.

## Description

- A **field of jagged rock and stalagmite formations rising out of the water**, north of [[The Sheltered Cove]], with a **current and a whirlpool** dragging vessels into the shallows.
- Named "the bone teeth" in play for the way the formations break the surface.
- Beyond it the water opens out into **calmer, more open lake**.

## Notable Features

- **The whirlpool.** A random terrain encounter (d10 = 7, d4 = 1) that pulled both of the party's hulls off course and into the shallow teeth.
- **Shallow rock.** Both vessels ran aground and took hull damage.
- ⚠️ One formation is described by Addison as **"a stalactite out of the water."** Stalactites descend from ceilings; this one rises from the lake. The error is the DM's, not the transcriber's, so it is **left verbatim** per the verbatim rule. Do not "correct" it in future passes.

# Menzoberranzan
> **Type:** Underdark Location — the iconic drow metropolis
> **Status:** Not yet visited. **~26 days east-northeast (S21) — and explicitly ruled out.** [[Binks Stonevein]]: *"We're avoiding the drow city bad place."*

## Notes
- (S21) **~26 days east-northeast** — the farthest of the five destinations Addison laid out, and the only one the party rejected outright.
- (S21) ⚠️ **Drow encounters are now a coin flip.** Addison rates it roughly even odds whether any drow the party meets are [[Bregan D'aerthe]] or **Menzoberranzan loyalists**, and reminded them that a drow guard and a priestess **escaped [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Velkynvelve]] alive.** The party has no idea what the spy network already knows about them.
- (S19) [[Artaxle]] departed toward Menzoberranzan alone in a flashback scene (4 days before the present-day events), carrying false intel and hoping to join [[Bregan D'aerthe]]. Later in S19, relayed secondhand via [[Indigo]]'s thieves' cant: seen heading northeast, "ragged but" retaining his fervor — still alive, still en route. Not yet visited on-screen.

# Darklake Approach Pool

> **Type:** Underground pool clearing on the approach to [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]], with a submerged chuul lair beneath it.
> **Status:** Visited [[Session 17 — The Big Fish Eat the Little Fish|Session 18]]; party departed for [[Sloobludop]].

## Description

A high-ceilinged, bioluminescent clearing in the canal-like tunnels at the threshold of the [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]] region, built around a still, deep underground **pool**. The pool is shallow (~5 ft) at the rim and drops sharply to **>15 ft** in the middle. Stalagmites, mushroom caps, and rocky shellfish-mushrooms ring the water; steam rises and air flows northward. Side tunnels lead **left** (explored by [[Topsy]] & [[Turvy]]) and **right/east** ([[Sloopidoop]]'s entrance).

### The Chuul's Submerged Lair

A small air-cave reached via [[Sloopidoop]]'s whirlpool at the bottom of the pool (accessible only with his water-breathing/gills magic). Littered with kuo-toa, insectoid, and fish remains and rusted gear — the [[Chuul]]'s hunting hoard.

## Notable Features

- Gouges and grooves in the rock near the bank — sign of the chuul's lair (read by [[Blarg]], Perception 19).
- The drop-off made it perfect ambush terrain: the chuul grappled [[Kit Aluri]] and tried to drag her into the depths to drown her.

## Events

- [[Session 17 — The Big Fish Eat the Little Fish]]: The party rested, fished (baiting with [[Glabbagool]]) and bathed here. A [[Chuul]] ambushed them and was whittled down, then **devoured by [[Derendil]]**. [[Sloopidoop]] arrived, warned that Derendil "is no elf," and after the party subdued a frenzied Derendil, led them down through the whirlpool to the **submerged lair** to recover his acolyte's body and the chuul's hoard (5 gems, Lock of Trickery, Rope of Mending, Spell Scroll of Sand, a kuo-toa trident, scale armor, and a fishing net). Party departed for [[Sloobludop]].

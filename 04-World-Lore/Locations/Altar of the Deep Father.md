# Altar of the Deep Father

> **Type:** Cult ritual site within [[Sloobludop]], on the shore of [[The Darklake]]
> **Status:** Site of the S20 betrayal-ambush and the ritual that raised [[Demogorgon]]. Referenced (unseen) in S19 as the destination of the arrest party ("to the altar of the Deep Father").

## Description

The sacrificial altar of [[Bloppblippodd]]'s Deep Father cult: a manta-ray-shaped hide stretched between support poles, with a dead splayed manta pinned at its center and two dead octopuses artfully arrayed across the top, painted red and blue. A reeking golden chalice-basin collects blood beneath the idol; a broad blood-stained stone altar stands before it, with a sacrificial depression and grate 20 feet away. The Darklake laps beneath the whole structure. Remnants of past sacrifices litter the site; some kuo-toa offer their own blood.

## Notable Features

- **The golden chalice-basin** — collecting sacrificial blood at the idol; not looted (S20).
- **The sacrifice post** — where the duergar prisoner [[Hemeth]] was bound and gagged, queued for sacrifice (S20).
- **The altar's stone base** — cracked by [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s Shillelagh-buffed war pick follow-through in round 1 (S20).
- **Flanking hovels** — two rickety multi-story buildings on either side of the square; [[Aeolus]] crashed through one roof (failed Misty Step landing), [[Roxy]] demolished the other by stampede (S20).
- Guarded (pre-battle) by six kuo-toa altar-workers, a whip, and two monitors (S20).

## Events

- [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon]]: The party is herded here as false sacrifices; [[Ploop]] springs his betrayal-ambush by striking his daughter [[Bloppblippodd]] mid-ritual. Three rounds of mass combat follow: the Destructive Wave massacre of the Deep Father commoners, the freeing of [[Hemeth]], the ixitxachitl swarm boiling out of the lake — and the ritual **succeeds anyway**. [[Demogorgon]] rises from the Darklake as Blop dies by her father's hand. Combat "effectively ended" unresolved at hard stop.

# Gracklstugh
> **Type:** Underdark Location — duergar trade hub, seat of duergar power
> **Status:** Not yet visited. **~20 days southerly across [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]] (S21)** — and reaching it means going **back around behind [[Demogorgon]]**. Ruled out for now.

## Notes
- (S21, [[Binks Stonevein]]'s History 13) **The duergar trade seat.** A **massive** trade hub whose **forge is powered by a red dragon**. Duergar are still evil, but *less so* than the drow; the **slave trade is prominent** there.
- (S21) **~20 days' travel, southerly across the lake.** The route's fatal problem: it runs **back around behind Demogorgon.** Not seriously considered once that was said.
- Positioned by Addison as farther than [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Neverlight Grove]] in the party's mental map of options.

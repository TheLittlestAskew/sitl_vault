# Neverlight Grove
> **Type:** Underdark Location — myconid settlement
> **Status:** Not yet visited. **Deferred, not abandoned (S21)** — [[Amanita de'Champignon]] pitched it as the trip *after* [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Mantol-Derith]].

## Notes
- [[Stool]]'s home. [[Sarith Kzekarit]]'s stated destination (S02) — he told [[Kit Aluri]] to find him there, and that "They're coming."
- The subject of [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s recurring **corruption dreams** since S01: a sprawling underground mushroom city of bioluminescence and mycetes, with a dark, oily corruption creeping across it.
- (S21) **Farther than [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Blingdenstone]] but nearer than [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Gracklstugh]], and more westerly. Not on [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]]** — reaching it means skirting the lake around.
- (S21) **[[Amanita de'Champignon]] still feels the mycelial network pulling north** and pitched the trip as a follow-on: *"I could take you all to Neverlight Grove after. We just have to skirt the lake around. It'd be cool. We could meet more mushrooms. You could get to know all the fungi. Be an awesome trip. What do you think?"* **Deferred, not dropped.**

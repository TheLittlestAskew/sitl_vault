# The Sheltered Cove

> [[Indigo]]'s cache. A small, dry, one-way-in cove roughly an hour's Water Walk north up the coast from the ruins of [[Sloobludop]]. The party's first long rest after [[Demogorgon]].

## Description

- A **small, protected cove** on the northern shore of [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]], about **an hour's travel north** of [[Sloobludop]] over water — past shipwrecks, crags and stalagmites.
- Contains a **shallow dry cave**. **No other egress** — one way in, one way out. [[Kit Aluri]] counted the exits out of habit, found only the one, and decided that was acceptable.
- [[Indigo]] knew it already; it holds his **cache**.
- The party barricaded the cave mouth with zurkhwood.

## Notable Features

- **Indigo's cache / flotsam:** roughly **six barrels and crates** (two broken), recovered on [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s Investigation 14 with advantage. Contents: **food, rope, line, ties, floating O-rings, ship replacement parts, zurkhwood, a kelp-like fungus and lichen.**
- Most of the salvage was consumed building the party's two vessels; the rest barricaded the cave.
- **Fishing:** [[Indigo]] took the first watch and fished.

## Events

- [[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger]]:
  - **The party's long rest** after escaping the destruction of Sloobludop. [[Indigo]] took first watch, [[Amanita de'Champignon]] second.
  - **[[Indigo]] introduced himself properly for the first time** — *"My name is Indigo Montoya de Charmed"* — delivered the six-fingered-man speech, called [[Demogorgon]] "a beluga" and immediately apologised to belugas, complimented [[Roxy]], and lectured [[Blarg]] on goblinoid tribes.
  - **[[Kit Aluri]] told the party who Indigo is to her** — wordlessly, through [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s rapport spores, showing the memory of making the same blind prayer for him that she'd just made for [[Binks Stonevein]].
  - **[[Darby Stonefeather]] conceived her dislike of Indigo here** and hid it behind a natural 20 Deception.
  - **Addison retconned in the [[Sloobludop]] archpriest's treasure** at this point in the session — 500 cp, 2,000 sp, 150 gp, 27 pp, a ~1,000 gp strand of pearls, 2 potions of healing, a potion of water breathing, and a spell scroll of *light*.
  - **The destination debate.** Travel times laid out for [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Menzoberranzan]] (~26 days), [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Blingdenstone]] (~20), [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Gracklstugh]] (~20), [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Neverlight Grove]] and [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Mantol-Derith]] (10–15 days by water). **[[Binks Stonevein]]'s dirty-20 History** produced the full Mantol-Derith briefing; the party chose it, primarily to spread the warning about Demogorgon.
  - **The fleet was built here.** [[Darby Stonefeather]] (dirty-20 Animal Handling, advantage from Amanita) reshaped [[Roxy]] into a longboat — 50 hull HP (25/side), AC 14, shields as gunwales. [[Binks Stonevein]] and [[Blarg]] (Survival 18) and [[Kit Aluri]] and [[Indigo]] (Survival 13+) scavenged and patched a four-person rowboat — 25 HP, AC 12.
  - **[[Darby Stonefeather]]'s mace** was examined (Nature 15) and upgraded to 1d6 fire.
  - **Party supplies at departure:** ~3 days of water each, 4–5 days of food, for **11 bodies**.

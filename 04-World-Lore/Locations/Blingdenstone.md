# Blingdenstone
> **Type:** Underdark Location — svirfneblin (deep gnome) city
> **Status:** Not yet visited. **~20 days' travel (S21).** Deferred in favour of [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Mantol-Derith]].

## Notes
- Svirfneblin city. **Its destruction by the drow is established history.**
- (S05) [[Binks Stonevein]] is knowledgeable about Blingdenstone and its **wererat community**; he knows the route there.
- (S21) **~20 days' travel** from the party's position — the second-nearest of the destinations Addison laid out, after [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Mantol-Derith]] by water.
- (S21) **[[Topsy]] and [[Turvy]] expect a cool reception** as wererats. They haven't been since they were very young. [[Binks Stonevein]] reports there *is* a wererat population in town but attitudes toward them are "fairly back and forth." Their long-standing reluctance to return (open since S01–S03) now has a stated shape.
- (S21) **[[Jimjar]]'s likely destination** after he and [[Hemeth]] split off overland northeast from [[Sloobludop]] — the party's best guess at where to find him again.

# Mantol-Derith
> **Type:** Neutral trade enclave — a centuries-old outpost carved into a pillar running from the cavern ceiling down to the waterline
> **Status:** 🎯 **The party's current destination (S21)** — **10–15 days across [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]]** by water. Not yet visited.

## Description

*All of the following comes from [[Binks Stonevein]]'s **dirty-20 History check** in S21 — the session's full lore briefing. [[Kit Aluri]] rolled a 7 and got nothing; Addison: "maybe the politics of Mantol-Derith were not really your cup of tea."*

- A **centuries-old neutral trade outpost**, carved into a **pillar that runs from the cavern ceiling down to the waterline**, with **waterfalls** running down it.
- **~140 people.** It never grew into a city and never was meant to.
- **Chilly and damp.** Brightly lit by **continual flame** spells and **light-reflecting crystals** set into the walls.
- Walkways and small **plazas paved with crushed crystal**.

## Governance & Trade

- Governed by an **informal council of four chief negotiators**, one per enclave: **drow, duergar, human, and [[svirfneblin]]**.
- Trade is in **rare and exotic goods**; each enclave also **trades information for goods**.
- ⚠️ **Surface dealings are dominated by the [[Zhentarim]]**, with a **"black network"** operating beneath that umbrella.
- Trade here is described as **rare** — this is not a bustling market but a discreet one.

## Notes

- (S19) [[Kit Aluri]] confirmed a personal contact of hers with loose [[Bregan D'aerthe]] ties, last known to be operating out of the Darklake/Mantol-Derith region (revealed via a private history/intimidation roll in a flashback scene).
- (S19) [[Indigo]], a Triton ally Kit reunites with at [[Sloobludop]], is a prior contact of hers from Mantol-Derith.
- **(S21) Chosen as the destination**, primarily to **spread the warning that [[Demogorgon]] is loose in the Darklake** — [[Binks Stonevein]]'s "goal number one." [[Amanita de'Champignon]] also wants to trade the [[Sloobludop]] strand of pearls here.
- ⚠️ **Open question:** whether the party's new outward-facing objective — warning strangers — survives contact with a place governed by four rival negotiators and a Zhentarim black network. Kit's failed History roll means she walks in without the political map.
- ⚠️ **Travel constraint:** the party departs with **~3 days of water and 4–5 days of food for 11 bodies** across two damaged hulls, facing a 10–15 day crossing. Foraging is possible at normal pace only.

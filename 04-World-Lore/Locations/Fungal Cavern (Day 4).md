# Fungal Cavern (Day 4)

> **Region:** Underdark tunnels, en route from [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Velkynvelve]] to [[Sloobludop]]
> **First Visited:** [[Session 15 — The Long Road to Sloobludop|Session 15]]
> **Discovered By:** [[Amanita de'Champignon]] (encounter roll of 4)

---

## Description

A large cavern filled with fungi and mushrooms of all sizes and types, encountered on Day 4 of the party's 7-day journey from Velkynvelve to Sloobludop. Groundwater trickles through the cavern, consistent with the general trend of increasing moisture as the party descends toward the Darklake region.

## Flora Identified

| Fungus | Properties | Danger Level | Action Taken |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Timmask]] | Explosive | High | Amanita identified and guided party to avoid |
| Tongue of Madness | Poisonous if eaten; not damaging on contact | Moderate | Noted; not harvested |
| Nightlight | General Underdark fungus | Low | Noted |
| [[Waterorb]] | Water-containing fungus | None | Party may have refilled waterskins |

## Party Activity

- [[Amanita de'Champignon]] performed Nature check (26 per DDB) to identify dangerous fungi and guide the party safely through.
- [[Amanita de'Champignon]] searched for Nilhogg's Nose specifically (Investigation 6 with [[Blarg]] Help) — not found in this cavern.
- 24-hour Bless from [[Binks Stonevein]]'s shrine desecration had worn off by this point.
- Party traversed without incident.

## Session 16 — Night Camp ([[Session 16 — Zone of Truth|Session 16]])

- Party camped overnight at the cavern in [[Roxanne|Roxy]]'s newly discovered **Zurkhwood shelter form**.
- **Watch 1 ([[Binks Stonevein]] + [[Topsy]]):** distant rumbling/groaning, mud-like flow trickling across the path, and ordinary-looking fungi flashing purple — **[[Violet Fungus|violet fungi]]** identified (false appearance; can attack within range; too distant to threaten).
- **Watch 2 ([[Kit Aluri]] + [[Turvy]]):** a wild **[[Carrion Crawler]]** (~7–8 ft body, no harness) scurried along a far wall, feeding. Party stayed low; it passed.
- Site of the horn ritual (Roxy partially stored), Binks's arachnid dream, [[Derendil]]'s sleep-hunting episode, and the **Zone of Truth** confrontation.
- Party exited the cavern over Days 5–6: the far north end slopes downward with pooling water — the threshold of [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]].

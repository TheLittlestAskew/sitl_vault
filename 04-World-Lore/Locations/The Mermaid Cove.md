# The Mermaid Cove

> A starlit water cove walled in rock, one way in, choked in unnatural magical fog. **It belongs to [[Indigo]]'s people** — merfolk and other Tritons — and [[Nanny Plunk]] had taken it from them and was converting them into hags. The entire twenty-second session happens here, and it ends mid-initiative.

⚠️ **Naming note.** "The Mermaid Cove" is **Addison's only name for it**, given in the session's closing line — *"that hysteria echoes throughout the Mermaid Cove as we close the curtain on the session."* No in-fiction character names the place.

## Description

- A **starlit water cove**, walled in rock, reached **around a bend** past [[The Bone Teeth]] and the Shoals. **One way in.**
- **Swift, shallow water** at the entrance; the party drifted in aboard [[Roxy]]'s ship-form and the scavenged skiff.
- **Bioluminescent crystal in the sand** and **glowing sporic plankton in the water.**
- [[Nanny Plunk]]'s **rock outcropping** rises roughly **15 feet above the landing shallows** — where she conducted her ritual, and where she died.
- Once the fog collapsed, the cove was visible in full for the first time: **bright blue azure crystal** outcrops and **blue lichen** across the rock, water like glass.

## Notable Features

- **The fog.** Unnatural, magical, thick enough to hide an entire enemy force — Stormcallers, Wave Benders, Skirmishers and two green hags were all inside it. It thickened into a **storm cloud** with ozone and blue arcs as the ritual neared completion. Destroyed by [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s **3rd-level Dispel Magic** (cleared a 19).
  - 🛑 **Its origin is stated two different ways by Addison and was not reconciled.** At **00:25**: *"You are aware that this is a magical fog created by Nanny Plunk."* At **01:06**: *"the fog that you're currently in is not harnessed or channeled or created by Nanny Plunk, although her energy is definitely permeating… she was not the origin."* **The session runs on the later ruling** — it is the merfolk Stormcallers' storm ritual — but both are on the record. **Needs Taylor's call before this is written as settled.**
- **Azure crystal.** Bright blue crystal outcrops revealed when the fog died. [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s **Plant Growth** burst mycelial tendrils out of the nearest azure crystal to pin a fleeing hag — so it supports fungal growth.
- **Blue lichen.** Revealed with the crystal. No properties established. `[Unknown/Ambiguous]`
- **The sand.** 🛑 **Setting foot on it forced [[Topsy]] and [[Turvy]] fully into wererat hybrid form**, in pain and effectively under short-term madness. Whether that is the cove, the ritual, the faerzress or the twins' own corruption is **unstated**.

## Inhabitants

- **[[Indigo]]'s people.** Merfolk **and other Tritons** — *"This cove is not for you. This cove belongs to my people."* He described them as innocents and refused to negotiate for that reason.
- **The High Priest of the merfolk** — found alive but weak by [[Indigo]] mid-fight; never seen on screen.
- **An unnamed merfolk priest** — emerged at the top of the cove as the fog died. ⚠️ **May or may not be the same figure as the High Priest. Addison does not say.** `[Unknown/Ambiguous]`
- **Charmed thralls:** [[Merfolk Stormcaller|Stormcallers]], [[Merfolk Wave Bender|Wave Benders]] and [[Merfolk Skirmisher|Skirmishers]] — all confirmed **charmed victims**, not willing servants.
- **[[Sloopidoop]]** — recovered here alive but very weakened. ⚠️ He is **kuo-toa**, not merfolk; [[Indigo]]'s relay groups him with them, but he is simply present alongside them.

## Events

- [[Session 22 — Blind Faith in a Hunter's Mark]] — **the whole session.**
  - The party rounds the bend hours after [[Sloobludop]] fell and is met by [[Nanny Plunk]] on her outcropping: *"Fate has delivered you unto me. I shall have my revenge and turn you into my own sisters."* [[Indigo]] names her, bickers with her in a "very suave Spanish accent," and only then mentions the cove holds innocents.
  - **The stakes.** There must always be a **triad** of hags. The storm being raised by charmed Stormcallers would have put the entire party into a coma and turned them into hags. **Two rounds** to stop it.
  - **Round one.** [[Stool]] Misty Steps onto the hag's head and empties [[Glabbagool]] over her; [[Aeolus]] summons the **Grinning Cat**; [[Amanita de'Champignon]] walks into the fog and traces a mycelial line to a hidden point; [[Topsy]] and [[Turvy]] lose their forms on the sand; [[Binks Stonevein]]'s **lute** Spiritual Weapon misses and rings off the rock.
  - **Round two.** [[Amanita de'Champignon]] **Dispels the fog** and the cove's real geometry appears. [[Darby Stonefeather]] knowingly kills a charmed Wave Bender and is driven mad by her own mace. The two hags shimmer and **disguise themselves as [[Binks Stonevein]]** — and [[Kit Aluri]] kills [[Nanny Plunk]] through the illusion on the strength of her own **Hunter's Mark**.
  - **Aftermath.** The **psychic faerzress link between [[Nanny Plunk]] and [[Demogorgon]] is cut.** The storm dies, the fog thins to mist, and the surviving merfolk are suddenly stunned and in agonising pain. Two hags flee north; [[Amanita de'Champignon]] pins one with **Plant Growth**, the other escapes **carrying [[Nanny Plunk]]'s soul-essence**. [[Indigo]] and a very weakened [[Sloopidoop]] hold the survivors in a trap of ice shards and glowing water bubbles.
  - 🛑 **The session ends mid-initiative**, with [[Darby Stonefeather]] and [[Kit Aluri]] both under active short-term madness and [[Kit Aluri]] incapacitated by [[Binks Stonevein]]'s *Tasha's Hideous Laughter*. Her hysteria "echoes throughout the Mermaid Cove as we close the curtain."

# The Darklake
> **Type:** Underdark Region — vast under-lake
> **Status:** 🚢 **The party is now ON it (S21),** aboard two damaged hulls, ~10–15 days out from [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Mantol-Derith]]. ⚠️ **[[Demogorgon]] is loose in these waters.**

## Geography (S21)

- A **vast under-lake in a high-ceilinged cavern**; tidally ambiguous — Addison: "basically just like a great lake."
- **Larger and smaller islands** further out; **open water to the west**; jagged rock, coves, whirlpools, and stalagmite fields breaking the surface.
- ⚠️ **[[Indigo]] reports one expanse near the middle with its own weather** — elementally and magically infused. Not yet visited.
- **Travel pace:** normal ≈ **6 miles/day**; fast ≈ **8 miles/day** with Indigo's help, at **−3 to passive Wisdom and no foraging.**
- **Known destinations by water:** [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Mantol-Derith]] 10–15 days. Overland comparisons given the same session: [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Menzoberranzan]] ~26 days east-northeast, [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Blingdenstone]] ~20, [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Gracklstugh]] ~20 southerly (which means going back around behind Demogorgon), [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Neverlight Grove]] farther than Blingdenstone but nearer than Gracklstugh, more westerly, and **not on the lake.**

## Sub-locations

- [[Darklake Approach Pool]] — the bioluminescent clearing on the approach; chuul lair (S17).
- [[Sloobludop]] — kuo-toa town on its banks. **Destroyed S21.**
- [[The Sheltered Cove]] — [[Indigo]]'s cache, ~1 hour north of Sloobludop. Long-rest site (S21).
- [[The Bone Teeth]] — whirlpool and stalagmite field north of the cove. Where [[Nanny Plunk]] was waiting (S21).
- The Shoals — swift shallow water past the whirlpool, before the cove (S22).
- [[The Mermaid Cove]] — 🆕 starlit walled cove around the bend past the bone teeth. **Home to merfolk and Tritons — [[Indigo]]'s people.** Taken over by [[Nanny Plunk]], who died here (S22).

## Notes

- Vast water-dominated region of the Underdark. [[Sloobludop]], the kuo-toa settlement, sits on its banks — the party's current destination.
- **Approach (S15–S16):** The terrain between [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Velkynvelve]] and the Darklake grows steadily wetter — trickling groundwater in the tunnels (S15), then the far north end of the [[Fungal Cavern (Day 4)]] sloping downward with pooling water (S16).
- DM (S16): "You're now officially in the region of The Darklake," ending the session ~1 day from Sloobludop.
- **Approach (S17):** Canal-like tunnels open into the [[Darklake Approach Pool]] — a bioluminescent clearing around a still, deep pool that proved to be a [[Chuul]]'s hunting ground. The party fished, was ambushed, met the kuo-toa scholar-priest [[Sloopidoop]] of the [[Society of Brilliance]], and looted the chuul's submerged lair. [[Sloobludop]] is now "a short day's walk" away.
- Per [[Notes From Addison]] (11/22/25): if [[Shuushar]] survived his fall into the pool at Velkynvelve, he likely went toward Sloobludop.
- 🛑 **S20 — [[Demogorgon]] rose out of it.** The Deep Father cult had been "chumming" the lake for something; the something was a demon lord. He destroyed [[Sloobludop]] in S21 and **remains at large in these waters.**
- **S21 — the party took to the water.** [[Indigo]]'s Water Walk carried ten of them an hour north up the coast to [[The Sheltered Cove]], past shipwrecks, crags and stalagmites. After a long rest they built a fleet — [[Roxy]]'s ship-form (50 hull HP, AC 14) and a scavenged rowboat (25 HP, AC 12) — and set out for [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Mantol-Derith]] to warn the Underdark. They made it as far as [[The Bone Teeth]] before a whirlpool, hull damage, [[Binks Stonevein]] going overboard, and **[[Nanny Plunk]]** on a rock.
- ⭐ **S22 — the lake is inhabited.** Hours later, past the Shoals and back out through the bone teeth, the party rounded a bend into **[[The Mermaid Cove]]** and found **merfolk and other Tritons living on the Darklake's north coast** — [[Indigo]]'s own people. A green hag had taken the cove and was **converting its residents into new hags**, powered by the faerzress and by a **psychic link to [[Demogorgon]]** that the party severed by killing her. This is the first evidence in the campaign that the Darklake has settled populations other than [[Sloobludop]], and the first indication that [[Demogorgon]]'s presence is empowering things at a distance across the whole region.
- ⚠️ **[[Sloopidoop]] was found here, alive but very weakened** — days north of where he was last seen holding off two green hags at the end of S18. How he came to be in the cove is **unstated**.

# Sloobludop

> **Type:** Kuo-toa settlement on the banks of [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]]
> **Status:** **Arrived and entered (S20).** Party delivered here one-way by Sloopidoop's whirlpool (S18); taken captive, then rescued into an allied faction's custody.

## Description

A kuo-toa settlement on the [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]]. First named in S03 as a destination; confirmed as **7 days travel from [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Velkynvelve]]** (S15, corrected from 8). The home village of the Society of Brilliance scholar-priest [[Sloopidoop]] (S17), whose successor as Archpriest is [[Ploop]] (S20). Per [[Notes From Addison]], if [[Shuushar]] survived his fall at Velkynvelve he likely went here — **confirmed correct (S20).**

**S20 — first full look at the settlement:** ramshackle, multi-level stilt buildings and rope bridges over canals; reeks of fish and shellfish. Wealth/status correlates with proximity to the waterline (lower = wealthier, higher = poorer/more derelict) — an inversion the party noted on arrival.

## Notable Locations Within Sloobludop

- **The netting gate (S20):** The settlement's main defensive perimeter — a wall of heavy netting on massive [[Zurkhwood]] posts, studded with sharp bone hooks. Party was escorted through it as captives.
- **Shrine of the Sea Mother (S20):** A nine-foot idol built from carved wood and the head/claws of a giant albino crayfish, draped in offerings of stones, mushrooms, and rotting fish; four kuo-toa guards on constant rotation. [[Kit Aluri]]'s Religion check (21) confirmed [[Blibdoolpoolp|the Sea Mother]] is a **scavenger goddess** who favors discarded/personal/regurgitated offerings. Site of the party's offering ritual, including [[Roxy]]'s dung "fertilizer" offering and an audible, disembodied feminine giggle in response.
- **Archpriest Ploop's home (S20):** [[Ploop]]'s private quarters. Where most of the party spent ~an hour planning the bait/infiltration operation against the Deep Father cult; site of the session's cliffhanger arrest attempt.
- **The hidden tavern / speakeasy (S20):** A rancid, cramped underground haven for non-kuo-toa visitors, accessed via a rigged cord and a crate-covered passage; kept open at personal risk by a Sea Mother-loyalist keeper. Where [[Kit Aluri]] reunited with [[Indigo]], [[Shuushar]], and [[Jimjar]].

## Notable Features

- **Level-5 milestone:** Reaching Sloobludop unlocks Level 5 for the party, taken at each player's narratively-chosen moment (DM ruling, S16). Party is still mostly Level 4 (Kit alone leveled to 5, S18) as of S20.
- **Village-access bead (S17):** [[Sloopidoop]] gave [[Blarg]] a bead from his necklace that grants the party access to the village.
- **Worsening chaos (S17):** The village is affected by the intensifying chaotic energy/faerzress the [[Society of Brilliance]] studies; Sloopidoop's mutual-aid bargain is to help defend it.
- **Religious civil war (S20, new):** The village's Sea Mother faith is split by [[Ploop]]'s daughter [[Aplopod]], who leads a rival "Deep Father" (**Limu Gugun**) cult performing human/humanoid blood sacrifice. See [[Open Threads & Mysteries]].

# Sloobludop

> **Type:** Kuo-toa settlement on the banks of [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]]
> 🛑 **Status: DESTROYED (S21).** [[Demogorgon]] rose from the Darklake at the close of S20 and levelled the town in S21. The party escaped north over water; [[Ploop]] stayed behind to evacuate his people; [[Derendil]] was left raging on the docks; [[Shuushar]]'s fate is explicitly unknown. **A permanent golden shrine to [[Garl Glittergold]] remains somewhere in the ruins, still emanating his power.**
> *Arrived and entered S19 — the destination thread opened in S03. The party spent three sessions here.*

## Description

A kuo-toa settlement on the [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]]. First named in S03 as a destination; confirmed as **7 days travel from [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Velkynvelve]]** (S15, corrected from 8). The home village of the Society of Brilliance scholar-priest [[Sloopidoop]] (S17), whose successor as Archpriest is [[Ploop]] (S19). Per [[Notes From Addison]], if [[Shuushar]] survived his fall at Velkynvelve he likely went here — **confirmed correct (S19).**

**S19 — first full look at the settlement:** ramshackle, multi-level stilt buildings and rope bridges over canals; reeks of fish and shellfish. Wealth/status correlates with proximity to the waterline (lower = wealthier, higher = poorer/more derelict) — an inversion the party noted on arrival.

## Notable Locations Within Sloobludop

- **The netting gate (S19):** The settlement's main defensive perimeter — a wall of heavy netting on massive [[Zurkhwood]] posts, studded with sharp bone hooks. Party was escorted through it as captives.
- **Shrine of the Sea Mother (S19):** A nine-foot idol built from carved wood and the head/claws of a giant albino crayfish, draped in offerings of stones, mushrooms, and rotting fish; four kuo-toa guards on constant rotation. [[Kit Aluri]]'s Religion check (21) confirmed [[Blibdoolpoolp|the Sea Mother]] is a **scavenger goddess** who favors discarded/personal/regurgitated offerings. Site of the party's offering ritual, including [[Roxy]]'s dung "fertilizer" offering and an audible, disembodied feminine giggle in response.
- **Archpriest Ploop's home (S19):** [[Ploop]]'s private quarters. Where most of the party spent ~an hour planning the bait/infiltration operation against the Deep Father cult; site of the session's cliffhanger arrest attempt.
- **The hidden tavern / speakeasy (S19):** A rancid, cramped underground haven for non-kuo-toa visitors, accessed via a rigged cord and a crate-covered passage; kept open at personal risk by a Sea Mother-loyalist keeper. Where [[Kit Aluri]] reunited with [[Indigo]], [[Shuushar]], and [[Jimjar]].

## Notable Features

- **Level-5 milestone:** Reaching Sloobludop unlocks Level 5 for the party, taken at each player's narratively-chosen moment (DM ruling, S16). Party is still mostly Level 4 (Kit alone leveled to 5, S18) as of S19.
- **Village-access bead (S17):** [[Sloopidoop]] gave [[Blarg]] a bead from his necklace that grants the party access to the village.
- **Worsening chaos (S17):** The village is affected by the intensifying chaotic energy/faerzress the [[Society of Brilliance]] studies; Sloopidoop's mutual-aid bargain is to help defend it.
- **Religious civil war (S19, new):** The village's Sea Mother faith is split by [[Ploop]]'s daughter [[Aplopod]], who leads a rival "Deep Father" (**Limu Gugun**) cult performing human/humanoid blood sacrifice. See [[Open Threads & Mysteries]]. **The war ended by annihilation (S21) — there is no longer a town to win.**
- **The canal district and docks (S20–S21):** Where the party fought the altar battle and where the escape began. Ramshackle buildings on stilts, rope and zurkhwood bridges, canals and docks. The north canal / north gate — still netted and webbed — is the route [[Jimjar]] tried to lead the party through and the way he and [[Hemeth]] eventually left overland, northeast.
- 🌟 **The golden shrine to [[Garl Glittergold]] (S21, permanent):** [[Kit Aluri]]'s DC-30 blind prayer transformed [[Binks Stonevein]]'s last stunt double into a gold shrine bearing Garl's form with **Binks's own facial features, posture and robes** — shield raised toward the water and toward [[Demogorgon]], **a middle finger raised on the other hand**, humming in a voice that reminded Binks of Kit. It paused a demon lord. Addison: *"it's still going to emanate Garl's power wherever it ends up, even if it gets buried or swallowed up by water."* **It is the only thing that survives Sloobludop, and nobody knows what grows around a thing like that.**

## Events

- [[Session 03 — Into the Dark]]: First named as a destination (~10 days travel at the time).
- [[Session 15 — The Long Road to Sloobludop]]: Travel distance corrected to 7 days from Velkynvelve.
- [[Session 16 — Zone of Truth]]: Party ends the session ~1 day out, officially in the Darklake region.
- [[Session 17 — The Big Fish Eat the Little Fish]]: Still "a short day's walk" away. [[Sloopidoop]] reveals it as his home village and bargains for the party to escort him there and aid it against the chaos; grants a village-access bead.
- [[Session 19 — We Are Split in Two]]: Party arrives via whirlpool, is nearly captured by a hostile kuo-toa contingent, escorted in, then rescued mid-ambush by [[Indigo]]'s faction under [[Ploop]]. Full tour of the settlement's streets, the Shrine of the Sea Mother, Ploop's home, and the hidden tavern. Learns of the Sea Mother/Deep Father civil war; agrees to act as bait against [[Aplopod]]'s cult.
- [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon]]: The betrayal-ambush at the [[Altar of the Deep Father]]. [[Ploop]] kills his own daughter mid-ritual; the summoning succeeds anyway; **[[Demogorgon]] rises from [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]].**
- [[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger]]: 🛑 **The town is destroyed.**
  - The session opens in the canal district in fog, among the dead, with a tidal wave seconds out. [[Binks Stonevein]] booms an evacuation order in Undercommon through three duplicates (nat-20 spellcasting, 26 Persuasion) and the town starts running; [[Ploop]] reinforces it and **refuses to flee.**
  - [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s Insight 21 rules that **[[Derendil]] is and always was a quaggoth** — he is left behind, raging.
  - **[[Jimjar]] and [[Hemeth]] split off overland** through the north gate, northeast. No reunion plan.
  - **[[Indigo]] releases his held Water Walk** for ten and calls the party to run *into* the tidal wave. They escape north over the lake. Demogorgon's tentacle catches [[Amanita de'Champignon]] and [[Turvy]]; everyone survives the DC 12 madness saves, barely.
  - **[[Binks Stonevein]] hangs back to pray for the kuo-toa dead** — the town's people "were nice to me… they helped me through my travels," and this is "the second town he held in his heart that has been destroyed." His roll fails; **[[Kit Aluri]] notices and prays for him instead**, clearing DC 30 with a 36. The **golden [[Garl Glittergold]] shrine** is created and left behind.
  - Demogorgon moves on the Deep Father shrine. **Sloobludop is lost.**

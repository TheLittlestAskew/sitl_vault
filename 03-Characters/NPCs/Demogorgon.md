# Demogorgon

> **Type:** Demon lord — Prince of Demons. The campaign's first revealed antagonist of demon-lord tier.
> **Status:** Active threat — rose from [[The Darklake]] at the S20 ritual's success; wading toward [[Sloobludop]], arrival "in an indeterminate number of rounds."
> ⚠️ *Filed under `03-Characters/NPCs/` per the S20 handoff's "new active antagonist page" note; whether he should instead live in `07-Flora_Fauna/Creatures/` is Taylor's call — move if preferred.*

## Description / Appearance

- (S20) Two monstrous baboon-like heads on a single **30-plus-foot** torso: red eyes, curved tusks, madness; tentacles burst from the Darklake ahead of him — [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon|S20]]
- (S20) His two heads are named **[[Aameul and Hethradiah]]** *(spelling approved at ≤60% confidence — verify against sources before treating as settled)* — [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon|S20]]

## Identity & Lore (as established in play)

- Only [[Blarg]] (Religion 26) identified him at the rising: **Demogorgon — Prince of Demons, the sibilant beast, master of the spiraling depths.** Kit (15), Darby (18), Amanita (8), and Aeolus all failed the check.
- **"[[Limu Gugun]]" was the kuo-toa's name for him all along** — the "Deep Father" the cult of [[Bloppblippodd]] worshipped and fed. *(Vault spelling "Limu Gugun" retained; official OotA spelling believed to be "Leemooggoogoon" — Taylor's migration ruling pending.)*

## Abilities Observed

- **Gaze/roar (S20):** His roar forced a **DC 13 Charisma save** on every party member; all six passed ([[Kit Aluri]] and [[Amanita de'Champignon]] both burned Heroic Inspiration to do it), and Addison shelved the madness table.
- **Madness as weather (S20):** For a full round before he surfaced, the battlefield signaled his approach — thickening fog, a pervasive manic energy in the water that tried to push Kit into frenzy, ixitxachitls fleeing their own element, kuo-toa dragged under mid-chant.

## Key Events

### S20 Key Events
- Rose from [[The Darklake]] when the Deep Father ritual **succeeded despite the ambush** — the accumulated offerings and fervor had already met the summoning's condition; [[Bloppblippodd]]'s death at her father's hands only punctuated it — [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon|S20]]
- The Deep Father kuo-toa dropped and groveled; the Sea Mother kuo-toa stood in shock. He began wading toward [[Sloobludop]], heedless of everything.
- The party's unanimous response — for the first time in the campaign — was retreat; the land-vs-water escape choice is deferred to S21.

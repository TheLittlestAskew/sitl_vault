# Garl Glittergold

> **[[Binks Stonevein]]'s god.** Trickery, wit, cleverness. Has now answered Binks twice on-screen and left a permanent golden monument in the ruins of [[Sloobludop]] — one that holds a shield in one hand and a middle finger in the other.

## Description

- Gnomish deity of **trickery, wit, cleverness, humour and gems**. [[Binks Stonevein]] is a Cleric of the **Trickery Domain**.
- Per Binks, Garl "likes wittiness, trickery, cleverness" — and rewards being asked for exactly those things.

## Key Events

### S14 ([[Session 14 — Far From the Sun|Session 14]])

- Binks cast **Ceremony (Atonement)** on the Shrine of Lolth at [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Velkynvelve]]. **Half the statue transformed into Garl Glittergold giving [[Lolth]] the middle finger.** Four jet eyes turned to gold; the faerzress shifted purple → gold; the whole party gained a 24-hour Blessed condition.
- Garl appeared to Binks in his stun-vision, praised his courage, and slapped him awake.
- Binks's backstory reveal in the same session: he was captured by drow before, and **followers of Garl freed his settlement.**

### S21 Key Events ([[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger|Session 21]])

- **Answered Binks's public prayer at level-up.** Binks prayed out loud before doing anything else — *"Please, give me wit. Give me cleverness, Garl"* — and rolled a combined **Religion + Insight 31**. Garl's answer: Binks's **stunt doubles tripled to three, made golden and semi-corporeal, and able to speak through him.** Permanent.
- 🏆 **Answered [[Kit Aluri]]'s prayer — from a woman who refused to name a god.** Binks stayed behind at the mist line to pray for the kuo-toa dead and failed (Religion 8). Kit — the party's declared non-believer — noticed (Perception 19), hummed her parents' song, and made a **blind reach** for "whoever would have its eyes on Binks," holding one image: **a shield**. She cleared **DC 30 with a 36**.
- **The golden shrine.** Binks's last remaining duplicate solidified into a **permanent gold shrine to Garl Glittergold, bearing Binks's own facial features, posture and robes** — holding a **shield raised toward [[Demogorgon]]** and **humming in a low sing-song voice that reminded Binks of Kit.** A wave of protection rolled out of the city and **Demogorgon visibly paused.**
- Binks immediately added a **raised middle finger** to the statue's other hand. Addison: *"Okay, all right. There may be consequences for that later."*
- Addison on the shrine's permanence: ***"it's still going to emanate Garl's power wherever it ends up, even if it gets buried or swallowed up by water."*** Sloobludop was destroyed; the shrine is somewhere in the ruins, still radiating.

> ⚠️ **It is not confirmed that Garl is what answered Kit.** She refused to name a deity and reached for "whoever is attached to [Binks]." The *result* bears Garl's iconography — but the voice in the shrine reminded Binks of **Kit**, not of his god. Deliberately left open by Addison; do not assert it.

## Relationships

- **[[Binks Stonevein]]:** His cleric. Deep, active, and increasingly irreverent — Binks has now put a middle finger on two divine monuments, one of them Garl's own.
- **[[Kit Aluri]]:** Prayed to him without knowing it, or without admitting it. The shrine hums in her voice.
- **[[Lolth]]:** Opposed. The S14 shrine desecration transformed her statue into Garl flipping her off; her attention on Binks is confirmed and dormant, not gone.

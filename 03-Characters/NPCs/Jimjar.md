# Jimjar

Deep gnome, formerly a prisoner at [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Velkynvelve]]. A gambler who disappeared overnight in S01 — and turned up nineteen sessions later running an intelligence network in [[Sloobludop]].

## Personality

- Gambling deep gnome; talkative, charismatic
- Different energy than Buppido — less chaotic, more charming
- (S21) **Unbothered by catastrophe.** With a demon lord rising and a town dying, his parting line is a shrug: *"All right, I guess I'll see you when I see you."* Per [[Kit Aluri]]'s account, he "bobbed off into the fog like a man who has never once been where a disaster was."

## Key Events

- **[[Session 01 — Prisoners of the Underdark]]:** Disappeared overnight before [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s arrival (Day 4 of captivity). No explanation given by DM. Only met by [[Amanita de'Champignon]] (who had been there the longest).
- **[[Session 19 — We Are Split in Two|S19]]:** **Found alive** at a hidden tavern inside [[Sloobludop]], disguised in kuo-toa netting, **running an intelligence network** with ties to [[Bregan D'aerthe]] lieutenants in the northeast passages. **Reconciled with [[Kit Aluri]]** over his S01 betrayal — she forgave him without conditions ("survival is survival… no hard feelings"). Greeted [[Indigo]] as "you wet bastard."

### S21 Key Events ([[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger|Session 21]]) — **separated**

- Acted **second in the carried-over initiative order** — Addison: "Jimjar probably notices on their turn, which is right after Binks."
- **Tried to lead the party out the north gate** by land while [[Indigo]] argued for the water.
- **[[Binks Stonevein]]'s persuasion to bring him along failed** (two DCs, one roll, covering both Jimjar and [[Hemeth]]).
- **Took the overland tunnels northeast with [[Hemeth]]**, splitting off from the party permanently for now. Parting line: *"All right, I guess I'll see you when I see you."*
- **No reunion plan.** Addison: *"If you're all heading toward water, you don't know when or if you'll reconnect unless you, like, actively circle back and try."* Likely destination: [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Blingdenstone]].

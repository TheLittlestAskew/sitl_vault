# Glug

Kuo-toa "whip" (enforcer) of the Sea Mother faction; [[Ploop]]'s son and [[Bloppblippodd]]'s brother. His short name is an explicit, deliberate assignment by the DM in-session (not a mishearing).

## Key Events

- **[[Session 19 — We Are Split in Two|S19]]:** Objected loudly to outsiders' involvement in Sloobludop's civil war; overruled by his father. Hostile to the party, unresolved.
- **[[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon|S20]]:** Asserted present at the altar battle — Addison: "engaged this whole time. Though I don't have him on the map." Presence asserted but never witnessed on the map; recorded as such. Surfaced as the answer to Kit's "Does he have any other kids?" after [[Ploop]] killed [[Bloppblippodd]].
- **[[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger|S21]]:** **Tried to pull his father along** while [[Ploop]] lingered over [[Bloppblippodd]]'s body during the evacuation of [[Sloobludop]]. Ploop refused and stayed behind. Glug's own fate is **unknown** — the town was destroyed by [[Demogorgon]]. The only surviving member of Ploop's family the party knows of, and the last thing they saw him do was try to save his father from the same stubbornness that killed his sister.

# Indigo

> **Type:** NPC — Triton Eldritch Knight and **mercenary from the surface ocean**; [[Kit Aluri]]'s prior contact from [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Mantol-Derith]], now **travelling with the party as its 11th member**
> **Status:** Alive. Got the whole party out of a dying [[Sloobludop]] by making them run *into* a tidal wave, then led them to his cache.
> ⚠️ **Surname unverifiable.** He gives his full name in S21 as **"Indigo Montoya de Charmed"** — a clear *Inigo Montoya* homage, since the six-fingered-man speech follows immediately. **"de Charmed" appears in no vault source and is almost certainly mis-transcribed.** No correction proposed; this page carries no surname. **Needs Addison.**
> ⚠️ **Do not fold transcript line 1767 into his dialogue.** *"I built this character to try to practice my Spanish and then I never…"* is **Addison speaking out of character**, not Indigo.
> *How he and Kit know each other is still not detailed on-screen (open thread).*

## Description / Appearance

- (S19) Blue skin, green hair "like weed on the tide" (per Kit's journal); fights with a trident — [[Session 19 — We Are Split in Two|S19]]
- (S20) Wears **scale mail made of bone and teeth** — [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon|S20]]

## Personality

- (S20) Showman-proud of his skills: "Did you see that trident throw? I mean, come on." — [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon|S20]]
- (S20) A craftsman — his hand-made net "took me weeks to make" — [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon|S20]]
- (S20) Decisive in crisis: with a demon lord rising, immediately offered the Water Walk escape across the Darklake — [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon|S20]]
- (S21) **Certain, and right, against every instinct in the room.** Ordered the party to run **into** the tidal wave rather than away from it — *"We need to run into this wave. Not away from it"* — while standing in a spinning ring of water he'd pulled out of the canal. [[Kit Aluri]] followed because "I have fought beside that certainty before." — [[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger|S21]]
- (S21) **Courtly and performative.** Bows. Compliments [[Roxy]] rather than confronting [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s obvious hostility. Apologises to [[Blarg]] for goblinoids he may have killed. Calls [[Demogorgon]] "a beluga" and then retracts it **out of respect for belugas.** — [[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger|S21]]

## Backstory

- (S21) **Full name given for the first time: "Indigo Montoya de Charmed."** ⚠️ Surname unverifiable — see the flag at the top of this page. — [[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger|S21]]
- (S21) **From the surface ocean**, not the Underdark. A **mercenary**; "a bit of a traveler myself." — [[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger|S21]]
- (S21) **He is hunting the man who killed his father** — *"a six fingered, not really a man… pretty sure who killed my father"* — and asked the party outright whether any of them has **six fingers on their left hand.** Species, identity and whereabouts all unknown. A straight-faced *Princess Bride* homage. — [[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger|S21]]
- (S21) **Knows [[Nanny Plunk]] by name** and treats her as a recurring nuisance rather than a new threat — *"Nanny Plunk, we do not have time for your games."* How he knows her is unstated. — [[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger|S21]]
- (S21) **[[Kit Aluri]] once made her blind prayer for him**, the same one she makes for [[Binks Stonevein]] this session — shown to the party through [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s rapport spores. Predates the campaign. — [[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger|S21]]
- (S21) **Knowledgeable about goblinoid tribes** — lectured [[Blarg]] on them after Blarg declared himself "a goblin in spirit." — [[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger|S21]]

## Abilities Observed

- **Water/fog shaping (S20):** Pulled water and steam off a canal with a turn of his trident to thicken the fog and hide himself and Kit.
- **Returning trident (S20):** Thrown (9 damage, pinning kuo-toa K through the net), shimmers to mist, reappears in his hand.
- **Swordfish rapier (S20):** Two kills' worth of work on kuo-toa K; Vex mastery granting advantage on the follow-up. Possibly named "La Guha" — garbled audio, unconfirmed.
- **Hand-made net (S20):** Thrown over two kuo-toa at once; cut open by Kit's misfire, yet kuo-toa B still failed to escape it.
- **Water Walk (S20/S21):** **Once per day.** He *held* the spell rather than spending it, then released it to catch **ten creatures at once** during the escape from [[Sloobludop]] — converting the lake from a wall into a road. The party ran an hour up the coast on it.
- **Shape Water (S21):** Granted [[Kit Aluri]] a swim speed and an air pocket for the rowboat salvage. *(The transcript's "give you like an air cock it with his magic" is almost certainly "an air pocket.")*
- **Stat notes (S21):** **Passive Insight 16, Survival +6, swim speed 30.** Read [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s natural-20 Deception as mere standoffishness.
- **Aquatic athleticism (S21):** Leapt the tidal wave "like a swordfish or a dolphin"; surfaced mid-whirlpool and hauled [[Binks Stonevein]] back aboard on an Athletics 20 with advantage.

## Key Events

- **[[Session 19 — We Are Split in Two|S19]]:** Reunited with Kit at Sloobludop — a prior Mantol-Derith contact, now inside Ploop's faction. Led the ambush that rescued the party from the hostile contingent; took Kit to the hidden tavern; relayed intel on [[Artaxle]] via thieves' cant.

### S20 Key Events
- Flanked the escort with Kit from a fog-thickened alleyway (her Stealth 23 kept them unseen) — [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon|S20]]
- Netted two kuo-toa and pinned one (K) with a thrown trident; finished kuo-toa K in R2 with two rapier strikes ("See? This is how it is done" *[garbled]*)
- Running banter with Kit all fight ("skill issue" / "Did you see that trident throw?"); she showed him the trident she kept — "I picked this up and I thought of you" — and accidentally cut a hole in his hand-made net with her misfired sword-shift
- **Offered the water escape** as the session closed: "We can escape by the water. I can cast water walk on you all, and we can flee across the Darklake." Kit's declared position: if Indigo says run, she runs — "she trusts him implicitly."

### S21 Key Events ([[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger|Session 21]])

- 🏆 **Saved the party.** Insisted they run *into* the tidal wave rather than away from it, and released his held **Water Walk** to catch ten of them. The wave passed under the walking party instead of crushing them shoreward. Addison confirmed this is what actually saved them.
- **Led them an hour north up the coast** — past shipwrecks, crags and stalagmites — to **[[The Sheltered Cove]]**, a dry one-way-in cove holding his cache.
- **Introduced himself properly for the first time:** *"Kit, you never talked about me with your new comrades. My name is Indigo Montoya de Charmed"* — then the six-fingered-man speech.
- **Took first watch** and fished.
- Called [[Demogorgon]] "a beluga," then apologised to belugas. Complimented [[Roxy]]. Lectured [[Blarg]] on goblinoid tribes and apologised for any kin he may have killed. **Earned [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s quiet, hidden dislike** without ever noticing it (her natural 20 Deception vs. his passive Insight 16).
- **Helped [[Kit Aluri]] find and patch the four-person rowboat** (his Survival +6, with advantage), granting her a swim speed and an air pocket via Shape Water.
- **Lent Kit his trident** for her attunement experiment. Its properties are undescribed — Kit: "I don't know what's special about it or if there's something special about it. I just know I want to hold it."
- **Swam alongside the vessels through the bone teeth** and **pulled [[Binks Stonevein]] out of the whirlpool** after his natural 1 (Athletics 20, advantage). "Crisis averted there."
- 🛑 **Named [[Nanny Plunk]]** at the session's hard cut — the last line of the session.
- Reported that one expanse near the middle of [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]] has **its own weather** and is elementally and magically infused.

### S22 Key Events ([[Session 22 — Blind Faith in a Hunter's Mark|Session 22]])
- **[[The Mermaid Cove]] is his people's home.** He recognised [[Nanny Plunk]] on sight, **named her aloud for the first time**, and bickered with her in what Addison plays as a "very suave Spanish accent" — a full *Princess Bride* homage, complete with *"I do not think it means what you think it means."*
- **He buried the lead, again.** Only after the whole performance did he mention what actually mattered: *"This cove is not for you. This cove belongs to my people"* — **there are merfolk here, and other Tritons, and they are innocents** — and advised against parley on that basis. [[Binks Stonevein]]: *"you really buried the lead there."* [[Kit Aluri]]: *"It's like a thing that he does."*
- **His relationship with the hag was commercial, not personal.** [[Aeolus]]'s Insight 18 placed them as long-running **business competitors** rather than nemeses — Addison's word, a "weird… business relationship." ⚠️ **This is the closest the session comes to explaining S21's "It is you," and it does not actually explain it.**
- **He went into the fog alone.** Shouted in dismay in a language nobody understood and darted off — out of the party's hands but still active. [[Kit Aluri]] spent her **reaction** reaching for him through the faerzress and felt a pulse **hidden in stone and deep in the sand**: in danger, but not mortal danger.
- ⭐ **He extracted the leadership.** Relayed telepathically that he had found the **High Priest of the merfolk** and **[[Sloopidoop]]**, both alive but weak; that there must always be a **triad** of hags; and that the storm was being cast by **charmed merfolk forced into it**. *"I needed to save the leaders before they became infected by the witch's magic… Work to slow the surge and keep their attention. I will work on the storm."* **That briefing is the session's central exposition** and gave the party its two-round deadline.
- **Ended the session holding the line.** Emerged at the top of the cove with a very weakened [[Sloopidoop]], the two of them holding a containment trap of ice shards and glowing water bubbles over the surviving charmed merfolk.
- 🛑 **He used force on a party member.** As [[Darby Stonefeather]] — raging and hallucinating — hunted a stunned merfolk, [[Indigo]] **shouted "Stop" in Aquan** and magically held her; she failed the Wisdom save on a 5. Her rage dropped; the hallucinations did not. She had already said *"I don't like him"* twice that session, and registered this before rolling: *"I do not like Indigo very much."* ⚠️ **The S21 friction is now a standing problem, and he has escalated it.**

## Relationships

- **[[Kit Aluri]]:** The campaign's most guarded character names total trust in him out loud (S20), then in S21 shows the party *why* through the rapport spores — she once made the same blind prayer for him that she makes for [[Binks Stonevein]] here. Kit places him "on the same level as you guys." **Backstory of how they met still not detailed on-screen — open thread.**
- **[[Darby Stonefeather]]:** **New friction (S21).** She disliked him on sight after the beluga joke and chose to hide it rather than say it, rolling a natural 20 Deception. He read only standoffishness and complimented her mount. Her closing line of the session: "I don't like Indigo."
- **[[Blarg]]:** Cordial and slightly absurd. Lectured him on goblinoids, apologised for possibly killing his "kin," and was waved off.
- **[[Nanny Plunk]]:** **Knows her by name (S21)** and is unimpressed rather than alarmed. The single biggest open question about him.
- **[[Ploop]] / Sea Mother faction:** Operated within it; led the S19 rescue ambush. [[Sloobludop]] is now destroyed and Ploop's fate is unknown.
- **[[Jimjar]]:** Fellow traveler in Sloobludop's underground; Jimjar greeted him "you wet bastard" (S19). Separated in S21. Their intel networks' overlap is an open question.

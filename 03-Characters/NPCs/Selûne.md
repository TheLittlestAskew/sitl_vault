# Selûne

> **[[Blarg]]'s god.** Named on-record for the first time in S21, retroactively giving a name to the moon thread that has run through the whole campaign.

## Description

- Goddess of **the moon**. Blarg prays to her and to "the moon" for guidance under pressure.
- **First named in S21.** Prior to that the campaign only had "the moon" as an unnamed, recurring divine presence in Blarg's story.

## The Moon Thread (retroactive)

Blarg's connection to the moon predates the name by many sessions, and the vault should now read these as one thread:

- **S05** — the **Moon Flask** with its moon shard, found in the dwarven safe house and given to [[Turvy]] to help control his lycanthropy.
- **S08** — Religion 9 revealed his connection to the moon is **impeded at depth**: *"I can barely feel the moon down here. It's like shouting into a pillow."* Flagged as a possible drag on Moonbeam, Wild Shape and Channel Divinity.
- **S10** — Moonbeam felt "maybe even a little bit weaker" in [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Feydark]].
- **S14** — holding [[Ilvara Mizzrym]]'s **moonstone**, he sensed the moon's phase (waxing, not yet full), its position (past apex, receding), and estimated the time of day. The party's first concrete time-of-day reading underground.
- **S21** — he **names her**, prays to her, and Addison answers by **rounding his darkvision from 120 up to 200 feet** and describing the world in black-and-white moonlight. The impediment established in S08 is, at least for this moment, lifted.

## Key Events

### S21 Key Events ([[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger|Session 21]])

- [[Blarg]] took his **2nd Cleric level** in the opening round, framing it explicitly around **Selûne and the moon**, and reaching for guidance because he was openly frightened of [[Demogorgon]].
- **Religion 26** confirmed how bad the demon lord's presence is.
- Addison's reward for the moment: darkvision **120 → 200 ft**, rendered as moonlight — the strongest his moon connection has ever been in the Underdark.

## Relationships

- **[[Blarg]]:** His deity. The relationship was implicit for twenty sessions and became explicit in S21.
- **[[Turvy]]:** Indirectly — the moon flask channels lunar energy to help him control his lycanthropy, and DM homebrew confirms wererats are still moon-affected.

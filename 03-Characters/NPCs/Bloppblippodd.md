# Bloppblippodd ("Blop")

> **Type:** NPC — kuo-toa Archpriestess of the Deep Father; leader of the cult that split [[Sloobludop]]
> **Status:** **DEAD (S20)** — killed by her own father [[Ploop]]'s scepter, round 3 of the altar battle
> ✅ **Naming migration:** first recorded in S19 under the placeholder **"Aplopod"** (name unconfirmed at the time). The S20 spell-check confirmed **Bloppblippodd** ("Blop Lepad" on air; matches `Characters Associated with the Underdark.md`). She died this session, so the name is permanent in the record. S19 artifacts have been migrated.

## Description / Appearance

- (S20) Emerged from a low, squat hovel by the altar "like a queen coming out of a palace," flanked by her whip [[Clib]] — [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon|S20]]

## Backstory

- (S19) [[Ploop]]'s daughter. Weeks before the party's arrival, she experienced a vision of a new god — **[[Limu Gugun]], "the Deep Father"** — and drew away a large, growing faction performing human/humanoid blood sacrifice and "chumming" the Darklake — [[Session 19 — We Are Split in Two|S19]]

## Personality

- (S20) The party's group Insight read her instantly: **a bloodthirsty sadist with full, perfect confidence** in her faith that her divine vision would raise her to rule her people — [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon|S20]]

## Key Events

### S20 Key Events
- Presided over the sacrifice ritual at the [[Altar of the Deep Father]] — struck by her father [[Ploop]]'s scepter mid-gesture, springing his planned betrayal-ambush — [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon|S20]]
- Shielded herself (Shield of Faith) while chanting [[Limu Gugun]]'s name; then rolled a natural 2 against [[Blarg]]'s Command ("grovel") and spent most of the fight **prone and groveling in the middle of her own ritual**
- Battered by [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s Shillelagh-buffed war pick (19 and 24 damage hits); crit by Ploop's scepter (~30 lightning, natural 20)
- **Died in round 3**: made one final call of "[[Limu Gugun]]!" as her father's killing blow landed — and the ritual **still achieved its terrible success**, raising [[Demogorgon]] from the Darklake
- Was not an only child — her brother [[Glug]] survives her (source of Kit's "Does he have any other kids?" exchange)

## Relationships

- **[[Ploop]]:** Father, rival Archpriest — and her killer. The session's emotional core: filicide that changed nothing.
- **[[Clib]]:** Her whip/enforcer *(name uncertain — "Clip or Clib" on air)*.
- **[[Glug]]:** Brother, a Sea Mother whip loyal to their father.
- **[[Demogorgon]]:** The "Deep Father" her cult fed. Her death punctuated, not prevented, his rising.

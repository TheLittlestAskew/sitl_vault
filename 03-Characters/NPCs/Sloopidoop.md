# Sloopidoop

A kuo-toa scholar-priest of the [[Society of Brilliance]], encountered at the Darklake-approach pool in [[Session 17 — The Big Fish Eat the Little Fish|Session 17]]. The party's first transactional ally of the Society after the pacifist monk [[Shuushar]] (whom he is acquainted with). Delivers his single name — a mononym — without embarrassment despite the table's open laughter.

## Description / Appearance

- Tall, thin kuo-toa in **fishnet robes** with a beaded necklace of bone/stone trinkets and a **staff carved with a flying-fish / crustacean motif** (S17).
- Big, neutral eyes; immediately recognizable as the same species as [[Shuushar the Awakened]] (S17).
- Surprisingly agile — entered the lair with a triple-flip dive (S17).

## Backstory

- (S17) A scholar-priest of the [[Society of Brilliance]] and a colleague of the mind flayer [[Wazalax]] *(name unresolved — also heard "Grazilax" / "Rasa Lax")*. Acquainted with [[Shuushar the Awakened]].
- (S17) Home village is **[[Sloobludop]]**, a kuo-toa settlement on the Darklake — "a short day's walk" away.
- (S17) Worships not [[Lolth]] (he reacts to the suggestion with genuine anger) but, he says, "knowledge" and **the Sea Mother** *(deity-name delivered as a deliberate, untranslatable kuo-toa gurgle — [Unknown/Ambiguous]; the canonical identification is not asserted in-note — no metagaming)*.
- (S17) Had been "merely waiting… for a group like you." A [[Chuul]] killed his **acolyte** and took the acolyte's belongings.

## Personality

- (S17) Serene, scholarly, transactional, unbothered by mockery of his name ("It is a mononym").
- (S17) Vehemently anti-[[Lolth]].
- (S17) Bargains rather than gives — proposes a quid-pro-quo mutual-aid deal, not charity. Per the DM's insight exposition to [[Amanita de'Champignon]], he is also "taking advantage" of the party's power.

## Key Events

### S17 Key Events ([[Session 17 — The Big Fish Eat the Little Fish|Session 17]])
- Walked out of an eastern passage just after [[Derendil]] devoured the chuul, warning: **"that is no elf. That is a quaggoth, and he will kill you."** Declared the quaggoth chaos-deluded into a false memory, with "no cure that I know of besides the peace of death."
- After the party subdued Derendil non-lethally, introduced himself — **Sloopidoop** — and identified himself as a scholar-priest of the [[Society of Brilliance]].
- Granted the party **magical gills** and led them down a whirlpool to the chuul's **submerged lair** to recover his acolyte's body and the creature's hoard; preserved and shrank the acolyte's corpse with water-magic.
- **Identified the recovered loot**: the **Lock of Trickery**, **Rope of Mending**, and **Spell Scroll of Sand** *(spell name flagged — see [[Session 17 — The Big Fish Eat the Little Fish|S17]] Archivist Notes)*.
- Pressed on [[Derendil]]'s story: knows the **High Forest** kingdoms and has **never heard of "Nel Rinden Vain"** nor of the wizard **"Pterostor"** (Intelligence 18) — confirming, to him, that the quaggoth is simply mad.
- Confirmed the **chaotic energies are worsening** and that studying their origin is the Society of Brilliance's primary goal (he suspects demonic origin; the party hinted at a **fey** dimension / archfey patron [[Silofirst]] and a death/undeath imbalance).
- Proposed a **mutual-aid bargain**: escort him to [[Sloobludop]] and help the village against the chaos, in exchange for knowledge and aid. Gave the party a **village-access bead** (to [[Blarg]]) as a show of trust.

## Relationships

- **Party** — First working relationship with the [[Society of Brilliance]]; explicitly transactional ("you help us, we help you"). Respects the party's competence (they killed a chuul he would not have faced alone).
- **[[Darby Stonefeather]]** — Comedic: Darby is openly smitten with him ("weird feelings… in a crushy kind of way").
- **[[Shuushar the Awakened]]** — Acquainted; same species and faith-adjacent.
- **[[Wazalax]]** — Society of Brilliance colleague (a mind flayer).
- **[[Lolth]]** — Vehement rejection; a possible future alignment of interests with the party's anti-Lolth baggage.

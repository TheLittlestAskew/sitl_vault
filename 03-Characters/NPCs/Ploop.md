# Ploop (Ploopploopeen)

> **Type:** NPC — kuo-toa Archpriest of the Sea Mother, leader of [[Sloobludop]]
> **Status:** Alive (as of S20's hard stop, in the altar square with [[Demogorgon]] inbound)
> *Page created during the S20 sync; S19 content sourced from [[Session 19 — We Are Split in Two]].*

## Backstory

- (S19) Current Archpriest of Sloobludop; successor to [[Sloopidoop]], who left "some months" ago on [[Society of Brilliance]] business — [[Session 19 — We Are Split in Two|S19]]
- (S19) Father of [[Bloppblippodd]] (leader of the rival Deep Father cult) and of [[Glug]] (a Sea Mother whip) — the family schism is the source of Sloobludop's civil war — [[Session 19 — We Are Split in Two|S19]]

## Personality

- (S19) Self-deprecating about his own name at introduction: "I am Ploopploopeen. Plin. I can't even say it. … You may call me Ploop." — [[Session 19 — We Are Split in Two|S19]]
- (S20) Capable of ruthless, premeditated resolve: planned and executed a betrayal-ambush at his daughter's ritual, massacred her followers, and killed her with his own scepter — [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon|S20]]

## Key Events

- **[[Session 19 — We Are Split in Two|S19]]:** Rescued the party from a hostile kuo-toa contingent via [[Indigo]]'s ambush; hosted them, explained the Sea Mother/Deep Father schism, and recruited them as false "prisoners"/bait for an ambush at his daughter's ritual. Dispatched scouts (naming [[Nanny Plunk]] as a known danger) to check on [[Sloopidoop]]. Surrendered himself to the arrest party as planned at the cliffhanger.

### S20 Key Events
- Collected from his quarters with his whip and the "prisoners" by a kuo-toa monitor and twelve kuo-toa, and escorted to the [[Altar of the Deep Father]] — [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon|S20]]
- **Sprang the betrayal**: struck his daughter [[Bloppblippodd]] with his scepter mid-ritual, opening the battle
- **Destructive Wave (R1):** massacred the Deep Father kuo-toa commoners in the 30-foot burst — Addison ruled they simply die, no damage rolled; the archpriestess, monitors, and whips saved
- **Crit his own daughter** with a scepter blow (natural 20, ~30 lightning, R2), then **killed her with his final blow in R3** — as her dying invocation completed and the ritual succeeded anyway
- Answered Kit's "Does he have any other kids?": one son, [[Glug]]
- Ended the session alive in the altar square, his ambush a perfect tactical success and a total strategic failure — [[Demogorgon]] risen regardless

## Relationships

- **[[Bloppblippodd]]:** Daughter — killed by his own hand to stop her god. It wasn't enough.
- **[[Glug]]:** Son, a whip in his faction; asserted present at the S20 battle though never shown on the map.
- **The party:** Allies of convenience — they played the bait role as promised and fought on his side through the ambush. With the town facing destruction, the alliance's future is moot beyond escape.
- **[[Sloopidoop]]:** Predecessor as Archpriest; Ploop sent scouts after him (S19) — no result reported yet.

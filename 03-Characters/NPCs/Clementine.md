# Clementine

[[Darby Stonefeather]]'s pet spider. Grew in size in [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Feydark]] (S10). Size/stats TBD by Addison.

## Description / Appearance

- Spider, exact species unstated. Grew slightly in the Feydark when Darby's father-vision gave her a scritch (S10).
- Described by Darby as "sweet darling girl" (S16); "She's so cute."

## Personality

- Affectionate toward Darby; tolerated by the rest of the party.
- S16 reveals awareness and judgment: she dislikes her "master" (The Master of Spiders) and prefers Darby — "she's not as cool as you."

## Abilities Observed

- **Speech (S16, per Binks):** Binks claims she spoke to him in Common during the night episode. Under Zone of Truth he could say "she talked to me" (he believes it) — partially caught on "she tried to bite me" (she was *prepared* but not attempting).
- Help action in combat — distracted [[Ilvara Mizzrym]], granting advantage (S13).

- **[[Session 10 — 021526_Echoes_in_the_Feydark|Session 10]]:** Grew slightly in size during Darby's Feydark father-vision.
- **[[Session 13 — Reckoning at Velkynvelve|Session 13]]:** Used the Help action to distract Ilvara. Darby shielded her from spider gore: "Clementine, don't look."

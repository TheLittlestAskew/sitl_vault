# Nanny Plunk

> **The session-21 cliffhanger.** A green hag who has been waiting, knows the party on sight, and is known by name to [[Indigo]]. No initiative was rolled — the session hard-cuts on her line.

## Description / Appearance

- **Green hag.** Unambiguously so — Addison specifies **no illusion and no disguise**, in contrast to the S18 [[Sea Hag]] who approached the party wearing a "mermaid" glamour.
- **Visibly older** than the hag [[Kit Aluri]] killed in S18.
- Perched on a stalactite-like rock formation rising out of the water among the bone teeth. *(Addison says "stalactite"; stalactites descend from ceilings and this one rises from the lake. Left verbatim — the slip is the DM's, not the transcriber's.)*
- Turns to face the party, registers a **look of surprise**, then a **malevolent smile**.

## Backstory

- **Named by [[Ploop]] in S19** — before she ever appeared — as "particularly mischievous" and dangerous. That was the party's only prior reference to her.
- ⚠️ **Possible, unconfirmed connection to the green hag Kit killed in S18.** Hags traditionally operate in covens of three; [[Sloopidoop]] stayed behind at the end of S18 to hold off **two more** green hags. Neither Addison nor the transcript confirms a link. `[Unknown/Ambiguous]`
- **[[Indigo]] knows her by name and treats her as a known quantity** — not as a new threat but as a recurring nuisance he has dealt with before: *"Nanny Plunk, we do not have time for your games."* How he knows her is unstated.

## Personality

- Theatrical and unhurried. She does not attack, threaten, or bargain — she greets.
- Frames the encounter as **fate**, not coincidence, which implies prior knowledge of the party or of someone in it.

## Key Events

### S21 Key Events ([[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger|Session 21]])

- **First direct appearance.** As the party rounds the bone teeth into calmer, more open water on two damaged hulls, they see her on a rock — no illusion, no ambiguity.
- She turns, is **surprised**, then smiles and says: ***"It is you. It was fates that brought us back together."***
- **[[Indigo]] answers her by name**, wearily: *"Nanny Plunk, we do not have time for your games."*
- **No initiative was rolled. The session ends here.** She is unresolved and active going into S22.

> ⚠️ **Who is "you"?** She looks at the party; [[Kit Aluri]]'s own POV account reads it as directed at *her* ("she looked at me like she'd been waiting"). Not resolved on-screen. `[Unknown/Ambiguous]`
>

It may be Addison narrating a joke or Taylor's OOC line captured inside the DM's segment — which determines whether [[Kit Aluri]] canonically drew a weapon here. **Needs Taylor's ear on the recording.**

## Relationships

- **[[Kit Aluri]]:** The apparent target of "It is you," per Kit's own account. Kit killed a green hag in S18.
- **[[Ploop]]:** Named her to the party in S19 as mischievous and dangerous.
- **[[Sloopidoop]]:** Stayed behind fighting two green hags at the end of S18 — possible but unconfirmed overlap.

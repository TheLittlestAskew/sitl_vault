# Shuushar

Kuo-toa prisoner at [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Velkynvelve]]. Pacifist who sought meditation.

## Personality

- Monk/hermit personality; go-with-the-flow disposition
- Pacifist — did not resist or fight back
- May have had knowledge of surrounding Underdark area

## Key Events

- **[[Session 01 — Prisoners of the Underdark]]:** Knocked off the bridge by [[Ront]] during Ront's rage incident at a guard. Fell into the pool below. A whirlpool appeared briefly when he fell. Fate unknown.
- **[[Session 17 — The Big Fish Eat the Little Fish|Session 17]]:** Referenced — the new kuo-toa NPC [[Sloopidoop]] is **acquainted with Shuushar**, and per the session's framing Shuushar **survived and recently returned to the village** ([[Sloobludop]]) — consistent with Addison's note that he likely swam there. Strengthens "likely alive."
- **[[Session 19 — We Are Split in Two|Session 19]]:** ✅ **Confirmed alive.** Reunited with [[Kit Aluri]] at a hidden non-kuo-toa tavern inside [[Sloobludop]], deep in meditation, aloof and neutral — *"if you live, you live; if you die, you die — I will do what I will do."* Offered passive aid from the water if needed. Kit met his surrender without judgment, recognising an exhaustion she knows.
- **[[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger|Session 21]]:** 🛑 **Status reopened.** He does not appear. [[Sloobludop]] is **destroyed** by [[Demogorgon]], and Addison put his fate explicitly back in play: *"you also don't know what happened to fellow prisoner from Velkynvelve Shuushar, but that's a tale for another time."* Unknown whether he evacuated with [[Ploop]]'s people or went down with the town. `[Unknown/Ambiguous]`

## DM Notes (Addison, 11/22/25)

> "Shuushar fell into the pool during the events that got Ront sacrificed to Lolth's Spiders. If anyone could survive in the water without resurfacing, it would be Shuushar. If he is alive, he likely went toward Sloopludop."

Kuo-toa are amphibious; Shuushar's ability to breathe underwater makes survival plausible. [[Sloobludop]] is a kuo-toa settlement identified in S03 as being ~10 days travel from the party's position.

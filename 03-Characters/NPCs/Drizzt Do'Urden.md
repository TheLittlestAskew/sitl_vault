# Drizzt Do'Urden

> ⚠️ **Stub — referenced, never encountered.** Created 2026-08-30 to resolve a broken link that [[Jorlan Duskryn]]'s page and the [[Campaign Dashboard]] both pointed at. **Everything here is sourced from the vault**; nothing is imported from wider Forgotten Realms canon, even though he is a well-known figure. Treat published lore as *out of character knowledge* until Addison brings it to the table.

## What the party actually knows

- A **legendary drow who overcame [[Lolth]]'s power** and lives among the dwarves, far to the west.
- **Still alive.** May be found near **Gauntlgrym**, with **King Bruenor Battlehammer**.
- ⭐ **[[Jorlan Duskryn]] named him to [[Kit Aluri]] as he died** in the northern watchpost at [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Velkynvelve]] (S14), alongside the [[Society of Brilliance]] and his faith in [[Artaxle]]. **Jorlan asked Kit to tell Drizzt about him.** That request is an open thread and Kit still carries it.
- **[[Jarlaxle]]** — "One-Eyed Jack," leader of [[Bregan D'aerthe]] — is confirmed to have worked **both with and against** Drizzt in the past (S19, from Kit and [[Binks Stonevein]]'s history rolls during the [[Artaxle]] flashback).

## Status in the campaign

- **Never met. No progress since S14.** The S21 note records the [[Society of Brilliance]] / Drizzt thread explicitly as *"not touched this session — no progress, no regression."*
- The party is currently ~10–15 days from [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Mantol-Derith]] with a demon prince loose behind them; Gauntlgrym is "very far" by Addison's own framing.

## Notes

- ⚠️ **[[Kit Aluri]] briefly mis-said "House Mizzrym" for his house** at ~01:47:21 in S14 — an in-character error, flagged in that note. **He is Do'Urden.**
- ⚠️ Addison's own [[Notes from Addison]] list him as "[Drizzt Do'Urdan], legendary Drow Ranger" — **spelling inconsistent in the source**; the vault uses **Do'Urden**.
- `DND_Sources/Characters Associated with the Underdark.md` carries a broader Do'Urden family and companion list for reference. **That file is background material, not established campaign fact.**

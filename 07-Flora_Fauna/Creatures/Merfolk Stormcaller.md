# Merfolk Stormcaller

> ✅ **This page establishes the vault spelling.** "Stormcaller" was capped at ≤60% confidence in the S22 spell-check **purely by the proper-noun rule**, while Addison spelled it correctly elsewhere in the same transcript. Approved by Taylor 2026-08-17. The confidence cap lifts for this term from S23 onward.

> 🛑 **Not enemies by choice.** Every Stormcaller encountered was a **charmed thrall of [[Nanny Plunk]]**, forced to cast her ritual against their own people. They are residents of [[The Mermaid Cove]].

## Description

Merfolk spellcasters who **channel a storm through a staff**. In [[The Mermaid Cove]] a Stormcaller's staff glowed with a light that **visibly connected to two other points**, marking the ritual's geometry — the party could see the network once [[Amanita de'Champignon]] stripped the fog away.

## Abilities / Behavior

- **The storm ritual.** Charmed and *forced* into it by [[Nanny Plunk]]. Addison stated the consequence plainly: *"If the storm goes off, it's going to immediately send you all into a dream state, all into a coma where your bodies will be slowly charmed and used and eventually turned into hags."* The party had **two rounds**.
- **Electrified fog.** A Stormcaller turned the fog "vicious and electrified," forcing **party-wide Dexterity saves against lightning** — 10 lightning + 10 cold on a failure, half on a success. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] burned **Absorb Elements**; [[Darby Stonefeather]] burned **Stone's Endurance** and rolled a 1 on the d12.
- **Concentration-based.** [[Turvy]] attacked one and **broke its concentration**, which unravelled more of the fog and revealed two more Skirmishers and a second Stormcaller. The ritual is interruptible by damage.
- **Recoverable.** Addison's standing **non-lethal damage** ruling exists specifically so these can be dropped without being killed.

## Encounters

- [[Session 22 — Blind Faith in a Hunter's Mark]] — **first appearance.** Hidden inside the fog for the whole of round one. The first was revealed when [[Amanita de'Champignon]] cast **3rd-level Dispel Magic** on the fog (cleared a 19) and the entire section collapsed, exposing the Stormcaller, three merfolk, and the second Green Hag. Its concentration was broken by [[Turvy]]; a second Stormcaller was then revealed. Both survived the session, **stunned and contained** by [[Indigo]] and [[Sloopidoop]]'s trap of ice shards and water bubbles once [[Nanny Plunk]] died.

## Open Questions

- ⚠️ **How many were there?** At least two are confirmed. The fog never fully cleared before the session ended. `[Unknown/Ambiguous]`
- ⚠️ **What breaks the charm.** Addison ruled the *converted hags* need **greater restoration** or careful tending. Whether the merely-charmed Stormcallers need the same is unstated.

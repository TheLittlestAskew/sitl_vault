# Giant Toad

> **Context:** Not a wild encounter — this is **[[Blarg]]'s new Wild Shape form**, first used in S20 ("I want to swallow some fish, people").

## Description

A giant toad — large enough for [[Stool]] to ride on its back ("we have toad and stool — toadstool," per the DM).

## Abilities / Behavior (as used by Blarg, S20)

- **Tongue-grapple + bite:** With advantage from Stool's Help-action spore flurry, bit and grappled/restrained a kuo-toa monitor (16 to hit; 6 piercing + 6 poison = 12 damage).
- Attacks against the restrained-toad-grappled target came at disadvantage; monitor C's counterattacks (10 damage) went into Blarg's temporary hit points.
- Blarg's token was accidentally removed on wild-shaping and re-added at his initiative 16.

## Encounters

- [[Session 20 — Not My Circus, Not My Demogorgon]]: First use — Blarg wild shaped mid-round-1 after casting Command on [[Bloppblippodd]]; the toad-and-Stool Help-action engine worked for the rest of the fight. Blarg was still in giant toad form at the session's hard stop.

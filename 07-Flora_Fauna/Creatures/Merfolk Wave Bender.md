# Merfolk Wave Bender

> ✅ **This page establishes the vault spelling.** "Wave Bender" was capped at ≤60% confidence in the S22 spell-check **purely by the proper-noun rule**, while Addison spelled it correctly elsewhere in the same transcript. Approved by Taylor 2026-08-17. The confidence cap lifts for this term from S23 onward. ⚠️ Two uncorrected instances of `wave. Bender` survive at L931/L935 of the corrected transcript — their line numbers were not in the approved table, so they were deliberately left for a future pass.

> 🛑 **Not enemies by choice.** Charmed thralls of [[Nanny Plunk]], and residents of [[The Mermaid Cove]]. **One was killed by [[Darby Stonefeather]] while she knew it was a charmed victim** — see below.

## Description

Merfolk who **hurl miniaturised storms of water and cold** at range. They fought entirely from inside the fog and were **never seen** by the party until [[Amanita de'Champignon]] dispelled it — the party could only track them by their attacks.

## Abilities / Behavior

- **Ranged water-and-cold attacks.** [[Aeolus]] took **20 cold damage** from one in round one; [[Amanita de'Champignon]] took **20 cold damage and was knocked prone** in round two. [[Blarg]] dodged one entirely.
- **Fought unseen.** For the whole of round one they were invisible inside the fog. [[Darby Stonefeather]] located one on a **Survival 21** — Addison rewarded the check with a private vision of a **horizontal whirlwind of ethereal axe beak feathers** ringing the target.
- Also landed a heavy hit on [[Glabbagool]].

## Encounters

- [[Session 22 — Blind Faith in a Hunter's Mark]] — **first appearance.** Two were attacking from the fog in round one. In round two [[Darby Stonefeather]] leapt a rock, reached the leftmost one, hit at advantage — and Addison **asked whether she wanted to put it down or keep it alive.**
  🛑 **She knew it was charmed and killed it anyway**, narrating that her rage took over: a grab to the throat and the mace coming down. *"Not by choice. This is not a 'I want to do this,' but I think this makes sense."* Addison awarded heroic inspiration for leaning into the character, then required an **Intelligence saving throw on her mace** — which she failed, taking **madness level +1**.
  ⚠️ **Addison's standing non-lethal ruling was issued after this kill.** The remaining Wave Benders survived the session and are contained.

## Open Questions

- ⚠️ **How many were there?** At least two, probably more. The fog never fully cleared. `[Unknown/Ambiguous]`
- ⚠️ **Whether the dead one could have been saved.** Addison confirmed the charmed merfolk were recoverable and issued the non-lethal ruling minutes later. This is the session's clearest unpaid cost.

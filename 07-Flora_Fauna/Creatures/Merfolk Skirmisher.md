# Merfolk Skirmisher

> ✅ **This page establishes the vault spelling.** "Skirmisher" was capped at ≤60% confidence in the S22 spell-check **purely by the proper-noun rule**, while Addison spelled it correctly elsewhere in the same transcript. Approved by Taylor 2026-08-17. The confidence cap lifts for this term from S23 onward. ⚠️ L891 was listed in the approved *Skirmisher* row but **contains no instance of the word** — a table error, logged not silently fixed.

> 🛑 **Not enemies by choice.** Charmed thralls of [[Nanny Plunk]], and residents of [[The Mermaid Cove]].

## Description

Melee merfolk who **leap from cover with ice-rimed spears**. The party's first physical contact with the cove's defenders.

## Abilities / Behavior

- **Ambush leap.** The first one **leapt out of the fog at [[Amanita de'Champignon]]** as [[Indigo]] shouted in dismay and darted away.
- **Ice-rimed spear:** 3 piercing + 2 cold on the opening hit. Later ones dealt 7 and 6 to [[Darby Stonefeather]] *after* her rage resistance.
- ⭐ **Visibly charmed.** [[Amanita de'Champignon]] answered the first one with a **Chill Touch** opportunity attack for 8 necrotic and, on a free **Insight 17**, **diagnosed the charm** — relaying it through the Rapport Spores as *"Dude, this guy's super high on something. I don't think they know what they're doing."* **That single read reframed the whole encounter** and is what Addison's non-lethal and "recoverable" rulings hang on.

## Encounters

- [[Session 22 — Blind Faith in a Hunter's Mark]] — **first appearance.**
  - One ambushed [[Amanita de'Champignon]] in round one and was **killed by [[Topsy]] and [[Turvy]] working together** in round two — Addison: "the twins work together to take down the first Skirmisher."
  - **At least two more** were revealed when [[Turvy]] broke a Stormcaller's concentration and the fog unravelled further; they speared [[Darby Stonefeather]] for 7 and 6.
  - In the wrap-up, [[Darby Stonefeather]] — still raging and hallucinating — hunted the nearest stunned Skirmisher. [[Blarg]] **threw his bear body between them**; Addison converted the attack into a flat check, and she grazed Blarg for **10** while still dropping the merfolk for 10 — **leaving it alive.**
  - Survivors are stunned and held in [[Indigo]] and [[Sloopidoop]]'s containment trap.

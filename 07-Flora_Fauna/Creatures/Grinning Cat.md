# Grinning Cat

> ✅ **This page establishes the vault spelling.** "Grinning Cat" was capped at ≤60% confidence in the S22 spell-check **purely by the proper-noun rule**, while Addison and [[Aeolus]]'s player both used it correctly elsewhere in the same transcript. Approved by Taylor 2026-08-17. The cap lifts for this term from S23 onward. ⚠️ A lowercase `Grinning cat` survives at L625 of the corrected transcript — its line number was not in the approved table, so it was deliberately left for a future pass.

> The fey spirit form [[Aeolus]] chose the first time he cast **Summon Fey**, and immediately became attached to. *"I'm going to keep the big cat forever."*

## Description

A **large ginger cat, grinning with sharp fangs and claws**, rendered in **bluish-violet**. [[Aeolus]] found the form on D&D Beyond and picked it because *"that one looks cute."*

Because **Summon Fey lets the caster choose the spirit's mood**, he set it to ***Fuming*** — so it arrives *"looking really, really angry right now."*

## Abilities / Behavior

- **Fey Blade** — the spirit's attack. **+8 to hit, 2d6+3 plus force damage.** ✅ Corroborated independently of the audio: [[Aeolus]] has **Summon Fey** on his D&D Beyond sheet, and Fey Blade is that spirit's attack.
- **One attack at 3rd level, two at 4th.** [[Aeolus]] is looking forward to the upgrade: *"When I get level 4, so 13 damage, it's gonna be funny."*
- **Shares [[Aeolus]]'s initiative** and acts immediately after him.
- **Responds to shouted commands.** Aeolus *"scream[ed] the command. Attack it. The hag"* and earned a **bonus damage die on a 25 Performance check** for the delivery.

## Encounters

- [[Session 22 — Blind Faith in a Hunter's Mark]] — **first appearance.** Summoned in round one at 3rd level; landed a **Fey Blade hit for 13** on [[Nanny Plunk]], and hit again in round two.
  ⚠️ Its round-two damage callout — *"Nine at three, 12 at 15"* (L1657) — is **unrecoverable garble**. Recorded as `[Unknown/Ambiguous]`.
  🛑 **It ends the session as [[Kit Aluri]]'s target.** Kit's short-term madness (`d100 = 55`) requires her to use her action each round to attack the **nearest creature** — and the nearest creature is the cat. Meanwhile [[Aeolus]], unaware, spent the cliffhanger **petting it**, and was awarded heroic inspiration for doing so.

## Notes

- [[Aeolus]] on why: *"You know, he literally loves Fey."* The attachment was established in a single session and is now a real character thread rather than a one-off summon.

# Violet Fungus

> **Type:** Hostile fungus
> **First Seen:** [[Session 16 — Zone of Truth|Session 16]] — first watch ([[Binks Stonevein]] + [[Topsy]]), Fungal Cavern night camp
> **Threat Level:** Moderate — ambush hazard within range; no threat at distance

## Description

Fungi with a **false appearance**: they can dim themselves to look like ordinary Underdark fungus. When revealed (or activating), they flash a purplish color. Per the DM, "within a range they can attack."

## Encounter Record

- **[[Session 16 — Zone of Truth|Session 16]] (first watch):** Off in the distance, a rumbling/groaning sound accompanied a mud-like flow trickling across the path through the foliage. As it passed, light-emitting fungus flashed and several ordinary-looking fungi turned purplish — identified (Binks, Perception 16) as violet fungi. "They're not posing a threat at you right now. You just notice that it's there." Not engaged.

## Abilities Observed

- **False Appearance:** Indistinguishable from ordinary fungus until they act or are revealed.
- **Melee threat within range:** Stated by DM; specifics not yet observed.

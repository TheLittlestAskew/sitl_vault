# Carrion Crawler

> **Type:** Insectoid scavenger
> **First Seen:** [[Session 16 — Zone of Truth|Session 16]] — second watch ([[Kit Aluri]] + [[Turvy]]), Fungal Cavern night camp
> **Threat Level:** Low — "they don't really pose much of a threat to people as long as they aren't threatened first"

## Description

Large insectoid creature, body length ~7–8 feet, capable of scurrying across walls (and presumably ceilings). Technically a Large creature despite reading as Medium-sized at distance.

## Behavior (per DM, Kit's Nature 15)

- Scavengers — "they're feeding on dead things." They scour areas of heavy decay (e.g., dead insects like the [[Fire Beetle|fire beetles]]).
- Generally non-aggressive unless threatened first.
- **Can be domesticated in the Underdark** — domesticated crawlers would bear a saddle or harness ("probably more of a harness… lots of straps to go around their many, many legs").

## Encounter Record

- **[[Session 16 — Zone of Truth|Session 16]] (second watch):** One wild specimen (no saddle/harness) scurried along a far wall, moving away from camp, feeding, paying the party no mind. Kit and [[Turvy]] stayed low and quiet (group stealth — Kit 24, Turvy 15); it passed without incident. Kit, an orc with a DM-acknowledged bug aversion: "How do you put a saddle on that?"

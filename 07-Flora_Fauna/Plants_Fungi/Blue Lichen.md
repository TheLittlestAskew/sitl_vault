# Blue Lichen

> ⚠️ **Stub — almost nothing is established.** Recorded because it was named on screen, not because it has been developed. Do not infer properties from [[Fire Lichen]] or from real-world lichen.

## Description

Blue lichen growing on the rock of [[The Mermaid Cove]], alongside outcrops of **bright blue azure crystal**. Both were **invisible until the fog died** — they are part of what the cove looks like once [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s Dispel Magic and [[Nanny Plunk]]'s death cleared the air, and the party saw the whole expanse for the first time.

[[Kit Aluri]]'s journal registers the reveal without enthusiasm: *"The whole cove opened up — blue crystal, blue lichen, water like glass — and it was beautiful, and I registered that the way you register a wall you might have to climb."*

## Properties

- **None established.** `[Unknown/Ambiguous]`
- Not identified, harvested, tested or discussed by any character. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] — the party's fungal specialist — was mid-combat throughout and never examined it.

## Notes

- ⚠️ The associated **azure crystal** is a mineral, not flora, and is recorded on [[The Mermaid Cove]] rather than here. It is load-bearing in one respect: [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s **Plant Growth** burst mycelial tendrils **out of the nearest azure crystal** to pin a fleeing hag, so the crystal supports fungal growth.

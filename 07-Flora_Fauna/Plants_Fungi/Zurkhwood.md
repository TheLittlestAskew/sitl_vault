# Zurkhwood

## Description
Giant mushroom wood native to the Underdark. Zurkhwood is used as a primary building material throughout the Underdark, serving the same role that timber does on the surface. It is edible.

## Properties
- **Structural material:** Used to build platforms, bridges, furniture, chests, and the spider statue at [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Velkynvelve]]
- **Edible:** Identified as edible by [[Blarg]] (Nature check 26, S01)
- **Appearance:** Woody mushroom material; strong enough for construction

## Campaign Appearances

### [[Session 01 — Prisoners of the Underdark]]
- Blarg identified the platform material at Velkynvelve as Zurkhwood with a Nature 26 check — "genuinely delighted" at the discovery
- The 10ft spider statue in the Shrine to Lolth is carved from Zurkhwood
- Bridges and platforms at Velkynvelve are made from Zurkhwood planks and silk cord
- Binks's foot broke through a Zurkhwood plank during stealth approach (S02)

### [[Session 02 — Come Get Your Shit]]
- Chests in the barracks and elite barracks were Zurkhwood
- Darby carried 1-2 Zurkhwood chests of equipment during the escape

### [[Session 03 — Into the Dark]]
- Zurkhwood and other fungi covered the mushroom cavern walls, floors, and ceilings in the passages north of Velkynvelve

### [[Session 16 — Zone of Truth]] / [[Session 18 — Trust the Whirlpool]]
- **[[Roxy]]'s shelter form** (S16) congealed a **zurkhwood tent-cabin** out of her dimensional saddlebags — the party's night camp at the [[Fungal Cavern (Day 4)]].
- **Roxy's twin rafts** (S18) were likewise zurkhwood, 20 HP each.

### [[Session 19 — We Are Split in Two]]
- [[Sloobludop]]'s netting gate is strung on **massive zurkhwood posts**; the settlement's bridges are zurkhwood too.

### [[Session 21 — The Shield and the Middle Finger]]
- **Boatbuilding material.** Zurkhwood was among the salvage [[Amanita de'Champignon]] recovered from [[Indigo]]'s cache at [[The Sheltered Cove]] (Investigation 14 with advantage), alongside food, rope, line, ties, floating O-rings and ship replacement parts.
- **Used to barricade the cave mouth** at the cove during the party's long rest.
- **Consumed building the fleet** — [[Roxy]]'s ship-form and the patching of the scavenged four-person rowboat. Its third distinct vehicle application in six sessions; zurkhwood is effectively this campaign's shipbuilding timber as well as its construction lumber.
- Also recovered at the cove: an unnamed **kelp-like fungus** and **lichen**. `[Unknown/Ambiguous]` — no species identified, no properties given.

## Spelling Note
DM Addison verbally spelled it "C-U-R-K-H wood" in S01. Canonical spelling per published D&D sources is **Zurkhwood**. Addison's written recaps use "zhirkwood" in one instance. Use "Zurkhwood" consistently.

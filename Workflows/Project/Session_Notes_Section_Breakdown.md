#### \[CHARACTER POV]

KIT ALURI POV JOURNAL (STORYTELLING EXCEPTION)

Track the session exclusively through the lens of Kit Aluri (Orc/Half-Orc Battlemaster Fighter/Assassin Rogue).

Storytelling Wiggle Room: Unlike the rest of the objective archive, this section must be output as an engaging, in-character story. You are permitted variance in wording and narrative flow to make it read as a true journal entry, provided the underlying events remain factually accurate to the session.

Personality Analysis Engine: Continuously analyze Kit's actions and decisions as the storyline progresses. Evolve her retellings to stay completely true to her pragmatic, survivor-oriented, tactical character.

Capture: Emotional arcs, memories, shifting loyalties, temper, sarcasm, anxieties, and moments of growth. Track the evolving meaning/use of key items (e.g., the silvered crowbar).



Example:

THE THINGS WE CARRIED IN THE DARK: A SOLDIER’S MEMOIR

Day \_\_\_: We stepped back through Psilofyr’s portal and the fungal magic sealed the rock behind us. We were standing in the western passage of Velkynvelve again—the same throat of stone we once used to flee. This time we came back armed, linked mind-to-mind through Amanita’s spores, and ready to make sure the drow could not simply follow us into the dark. . .

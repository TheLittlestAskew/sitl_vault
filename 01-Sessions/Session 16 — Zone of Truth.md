## Session Analysis

### Narrative Summary

The session (pt2) opens mid-ritual at the Day 4 night camp at the [[Fungal Cavern (Day 4)]]: [[Darby Stonefeather]], assisted by [[Aeolus]] (Animal Handling 23) and [[Blarg]] (Guidance + Bless), conducts a ~10-minute ritual focusing **the horn** *(origin: [Unknown/Ambiguous] — likely examined during lost pt1; see Archivist Notes)* on [[Roxanne|Roxy]]. Roxy partially dissolves into spores and is drawn inside the horn, but Darby's Animal Handling 12 (rolled [1,1] on the 2d4 support dice) falls short of the DC 25 needed for full success — Roxy reconstitutes. The attempt reveals: (1) storing Roxy in the horn **is possible**, (2) another attempt may keep her there, (3) "something else" within is controllable. DM attributes the difficulty to stacked dimensional magic (Roxy's dimensional saddlebags entering a dimensional space) plus ambient [Faerzress]. As Darby meditates, residual energy connects to Roxy: her saddlebags disgorge stored matter that congeals into a **[[Zurkhwood]] tent-cabin shelter** — a new Roxy form (immobile while assumed; sleeps the whole party "snugly"; explicitly *not* Tiny Hut-level wards yet; improves with practice/attunement/leveling; may eventually solve Roxy's narrow-passage problem). Darby: "It's kind of like a Pokémon if we can get it right."

**Night watches** ([[Amanita de'Champignon]] needs only 4 hours — trance):
1. **[[Binks Stonevein]] + [[Topsy]]** (Perception 16 / 8; flat d20 nat 20): distant rumbling/groaning, mud-like flow across the path, and ordinary-looking fungi flashing purple — **[[Violet Fungus|violet fungi]]** (false appearance; attack within range; too distant to threaten).
2. **[[Kit Aluri]] + [[Turvy]]** (flat d20 = 3; Perception 16 adv; Nature 15; stealth 24/15): a **[[Carrion Crawler]]** (~7–8 ft body) scurries along a wall, feeding, no harness or saddle (wild — though they *can* be domesticated in the Underdark). Party stays low; it passes.
3. **Amanita + Darby** (Darby Perception 17 adv): quiet — interrupted by Binks waking Darby (below).
4. **Amanita + Blarg** (Blarg Perception 11 adv): quiet until watch's end — **[[Derendil]] returns to camp on all fours**, collapses in a heap outside the shelter, unconscious, clothes torn **by his own claws**, no other injuries.

**Binks's dream (third watch):** Binks has had restless dreams since desecrating the Lolth sculpture at Velkynvelve — glades of trees, family with dead eyes, flying through Underdark passages. Tonight: he is *crawling, skittering* through tunnels toward "the one that created this disruption," entering through a hole in a stalactite above a shrine that is **molten, boiling, reshaping itself — "conflicting divine power"** — climbing his target's leg to the neck, preparing to strike… and the turning head shows **his own face**. He wakes with [[Clementine]] on his shoulder, steady, "as if waiting," and hears in his head: **"What stand will you take? Which stance will take you?"** (explicitly *not* a magical effect). WIS save 16 — avoids a madness point (count stays at 1; he has now dodged madness 3 times). Forced Religion check 19: a distant surge retreats; his connection to the Velkynvelve act **diminishes** — he was "protected from something that would have been very, very bad" — but it went **dormant, not away**, and he realizes he was seeing through **arachnid eyes**.

**Zone of Truth confrontation (morning):** Binks wakes Darby ("get your spider and keep her away from me"), then gathers everyone and casts **Zone of Truth** (15-ft radius, DC 14). Kit, Blarg, and Aeolus fail on purpose; Darby fails on accident (CHA save 9); **Binks fails his own spell** and is caught attempting "I am not possessed." **Derendil passes — and only Binks knows.** Revelations:
- Binks "might be possessed"; guesses the cause is "something that I did to a Lolth statue back in Velkynvelve."
- Asked "Am I talking to Binks?" he answers **"No"** — then clarifies: **his real name is not Binks** — "I'm not the guy that I named myself after. I've been the same person through and through." (First confirmation Binks is an assumed name.)
- The zone partially catches "she tried to bite me" re: Clementine — she was *prepared* to strike but not in the attempt.
- Blarg's Insight 21 vs Binks's persuasion 4, with DM exposition: **not true possession yet** — Binks is still Binks, but through Faerzress-saturated dreams he has "made a connection," and by desecrating Lolth's shrine, "faint as it may be, he's gained that attention."
- Kit Intimidation 22 on Derendil: he admits **"I dreamt that I was hunting… that I was a beast, that I was what I appear to be"** — woke in the fungus with self-torn clothes. Claims his curse — cast by **the evil wizard Terrestor**, who usurped his throne in the High Forest — drives the erratic behavior. He speaks no detectable lie, but Kit recalls he fought "like a quaggoth" at Velkynvelve — in *defense* of Topsy and Turvy.
- **Clementine, via Darby's Speak with Animals:** "I feel something familiar but wrong with them, like **The Master of Spiders** somehow with them, and that's not good. I have this master, but I don't like her very much. She's not as cool as you, but she's very powerful, **and she knows him now.**"

**Kit's trust speech:** No royal status in the group ("we're all equals here"); she will answer intra-party violence with force; "speak your truth… everybody get your shit together now. We have things we need to accomplish." [[Topsy]] pats her side: "Maybe we take it easy on the use of force." Binks defuses: "I think we've grown closer… team building exercise." Addison awards **Heroic Inspiration to everyone who lacks it**. The party retains its long rest.

**Travel close:** The party exits the fungal cavern; terrain slopes downward, water pooling at the cavern's far north end. Session ends **~1 day from [[Sloobludop]], officially in [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]] region**. Addison grants **level-up agency**: upon reaching Sloobludop the party may take its next level at any narratively-chosen moment, even mid-combat. Next session: **June 7, 2026**.

### Themes & Emotional Beats

- **The enemy within:** No combat — the session's entire tension comes from inside the camp: Binks's dormant connection to Lolth and Derendil's beast bleeding through. The Underdark corrupts through *dreams*, not blades.
- **Truth as ritual:** The Zone of Truth scene functions as the campaign's first formal trust ceremony — voluntary failure as a gesture of good faith. Note who chose to fail (Kit, Blarg, Aeolus) vs. who passed silently (Derendil).
- **Kit's escalating burden of enforcement:** Hand on hilt → hand on throat → sword drawn → speech. Topsy's "maybe we take it easy on the use of force" lands precisely because it comes from the person with the most reason to fear force.
- **Identity unmoored:** Binks isn't "Binks." Derendil may not be Derendil-the-elf. Even Roxy can be unmade into spores and remade. The session quietly asks what a "self" is down here.
- **Mirror imagery:** Binks hunting *himself* in the dream is the session's defining image — "What stand will you take? Which stance will take you?"

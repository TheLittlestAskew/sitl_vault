## POV Overview

### The Things We Carried in the Dark: A Soldier's Memoir

**Day — the same day the fish-town drowned. Then the first night in a long time that I did not spend counting exits.**

I came back into myself with a sword in my hand and no good reason to be holding it.

That is the honest version. The witch is dead and something she put in me was still walking around behind my eyes, and every living thing within reach looked like work that needed doing. [[Binks Stonevein]] was closest. I want that written down plainly because I am not going to pretty it up: my hand knew where he was standing.

He told me to drop it, and I did — not because I wanted to, because his voice went through me like a hook. Then [[Roxy]] took my sword off the sand and ran with it, which I would find funnier if it had been anyone else's sword.

So I pulled a dagger and I put it in [[Binks Stonevein]] anyway. Seven points of him. He ran. Good. Smart. I would have run.

Then the thing in my head turned me west and there was [[Glabbagool]], who has never done anything to anyone, sitting there being a very large friendly nothing, and I hit him too. I hit him with the greatsword. I had to pull to get it loose — it fights me now when I ask it to change, and I have started noticing how often I ask.

I apologized to the cube. He does not have a face to accept it with. I said it anyway.

Then [[Aeolus]] did something.

I do not know how to write this without sounding like I have gone soft in the skull. The air went bright. Not lit — *bright*, the way a room gets when someone opens a window in spring. Flowers. Grapes. The clink of glass. The taste of wine at the back of my throat when I had not swallowed anything. And the thing behind my eyes just — let go. Sat down. Left.

A vine came up out of the rock where he was standing, and it was poison, and I thought: that is about right for us. Something beautiful that will kill you if you do not learn it first.

[[Darby Stonefeather]] spent the whole fight in a field of mushrooms that was not there, laughing. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] walked out and joined her in it rather than pull her out of it, which is either the kindest thing I have seen this month or the most reckless, and with [[Amanita de'Champignon]] those are usually the same choice. Then she put that filthy corncob pipe in [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s hands and whatever came out of it took the madness with it.

We are all stool. That is what they decided. I am not going to argue with a working method.

---

Up the steps there is a temple.

I want to be careful here, because I walked in counting. Waterfall over the entrance. Steps and tiers, defensible, bad ground for anything heavy. Broken pillars, statues of fish-people with the faces knocked off. Blue crystal in the walls that pulses. High tide swallows most of it — that is a chokepoint and a drowning hazard and I marked it as both.

And then a tall man in wet robes came down the stairs to meet us and he was *ashamed*.

That is the part I keep turning over. He apologized to us. He said he thought he had time. He said [[Demogorgon]] tricked him into believing he had time. Every one of his people down there was tired the way you get tired after somebody else has been driving your body, and they still stood up straight for us and offered us hot water and food and a bed.

I have been on the other side of that. I have stood in the wreck of something I was supposed to protect and looked at strangers who came too late. There is no dignified way to do it. He managed it anyway.

[[Indigo]] stopped [[Amanita de'Champignon]] from patting him on the back. *Culture. Respect.* Then he offered his own back instead, because [[Indigo]] cannot let a moment stand without putting his thumb on it. I have never been happier to hear him do it.

[[Sloopidoop]] is alive. Unconscious, in their infirmary, but alive. The last time I saw that fish he was drowning his own city to buy us water to run on.

Then [[Amanita de'Champignon]] told the high priest, in that soft grinning way of hers, that she is a friend of the Carrion King, and the entire room changed temperature. The priest bowed. *Bowed*. Not the polite kind. [[Indigo]]'s mouth actually opened. I have watched people react to power for twenty years and I know the difference between fear and reverence and that was the second one, and it was pointed at the mushroom girl in the burlap who cannot keep her pipe out of her hair.

We are running with something much larger than we are. I have known that. It is different to see somebody else recognize it.

---

I slept.

I have to write it that way because I do not have a better word for what happened.

There was a door in it, and everyone was on the other side, and I went through, and there was an ocean made of light. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] was riding it. [[Darby Stonefeather]] came out of it flying, throwing orange feathers, and put wings on people. There was a black wave coming that wanted to eat all of it.

And I did nothing.

I want to be clear that I chose that. I was tired down past my arms, past the place where sleep fixes it. I looked at what was coming and I understood, the way you understand a thing you have no proof for, that this one was not mine to swing at. So I lay back and I let it happen, and I looked up.

I have not looked up in years. There is never anything up there but rock.

And then they were with me.

All of them. Every one. The brigades, the lines, the ones I marched into places they did not come out of. Floating on rafts in flat calm water, no war on them, no blood, none of them looking at me the way they look at me at three in the morning. They were at *peace*. They were part of whatever that water was. And my [[The Doll|Doll]] was sitting next to me and she was a person — real, quiet, buoyant, holding a drink she did not spill — and I was not surprised, and I have been carrying that thing out of a burned field for long enough that not being surprised is its own kind of ending.

I have spent my whole life being sure I killed them. I did not stop being sure. I just got to see them not blaming me for it.

And I heard my mother.

The songs. The ones I hum over my gear when I think nobody is listening, that I have hummed for so many years I stopped hearing the words. They were hymns. They were in two tongues I did not know I knew, and now I know them — I can understand them, I cannot say them back, and that is the most fitting curse I have ever been handed.

Something my family prayed to. Grounded, not cosmic. The dirt and the growing and the dying and the order of it. The **Wild Mother**. I did not know her name until somebody put it in the air in front of me and I recognized it in my chest before my head caught up.

I do not worship gods. I want that on the record. What I want is rest. If there is a heaven for me it is an afternoon with nothing coming.

I got one. It lasted a night.

---

I woke up first-ish, and I picked an island.

Small rock out in the shallows with a carved alcove and trickling water and a retractable walkway, near enough to see the mustering ground, far enough that nobody's breathing is in my ear. There are crabs. There are little bats. That is the correct number of neighbours.

[[Indigo]] walked me out to it before I bedded down. He said we have not had time together — not since our battle, not since the thing we do not talk about. I told him we do not need to talk about it. He agreed, and then said it seemed like I needed to anyway, and then let it go, which is the only reason I let him stay.

Tomorrow I want the hot spring. That is the whole plan. I got down to almost nothing in that fight and I have not had a bath since before the tunnels and my one ambition in this entire dark world is to sit in hot water until my hands stop being fists.

The priest is going to ask us what to call ourselves. So will the people at [[Mantol-Derith]], apparently. I do not have an answer. We have been six strangers falling downhill together for so long that giving it a name feels like admitting we meant to.

Ask me after the bath.

## Session Analysis

### Narrative Summary

The session opens with roughly nine minutes above the table. Addison opens by telling the table he is unwell and still recovering from an injury the previous weekend. ⚠️ **The details are deliberately not recorded here** (they are personal and this vault is public), **but the consequence is load-bearing:** a higher-than-usual share of this session's transcription errors are the DM dropping or swapping proper nouns mid-sentence rather than ordinary speech-to-text noise. Taylor mentions she has updated the campaign website; Addison pulls it up — *"Oh yeah. 22 sessions. There we go."* — and awards her inspiration on the spot. He also runs an unresolved riddle about a "Dr. Polymer" image he made for his science class, promising inspiration to whoever explains why the character is a secret villain; **nobody solved it and Addison never gave the answer.**

Addison then recaps [[Session 22 — Blind Faith in a Hunter's Mark]]: the Shoals, the whirlpool, [[Nanny Plunk]], the starlit cove choked in fog, [[Aeolus]] recalling hag lore, [[Kit Aluri]] recalling [[Mantol-Derith]] dealings, [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s Rapport Spores, [[Indigo]] vanishing into the fog, [[Darby Stonefeather]] killing a charmed [[Stormcaller]] and taking a point of madness, and [[Kit Aluri]] killing the hag wearing [[Binks Stonevein]]'s face and taking one of her own. He restates the **faerzress** rule plainly: it is *"permeating, absorbing this chaotic energy and then releasing it in random spouts,"* it is **not inherently bad** — *"It's just neutral. It's chaos"* — and the longer the party attunes to it, the more chaotic things will happen to them. He confirms that one converted hag is trapped and the other escaped carrying [[Nanny Plunk]]'s soul.

Because this is a **new combat**, Addison calls **fresh initiative**. He then rules the two madness conditions distinctly, and the distinction drives the whole first round: [[Darby Stonefeather]] has **vivid hallucinations and disadvantage on ability checks** but is not compelled; [[Kit Aluri]] has **no hallucinations at all** and must **use her action each round to attack the nearest creature**. [[Kit Aluri]] pushes back on the reading — *"So other than wanting to attack people, I'm in my right mind"* — and Addison confirms it: *"Everything that you see visually is real. But you are compelled."* Where multiple targets tie for nearest, she rolls a d4 for direction. Both players privately roll a **d10 for duration in minutes**; the archive shows **[[Darby Stonefeather]] 5 minutes, [[Kit Aluri]] 4 minutes** — numbers never spoken aloud at the table.

**Round one is the entire combat.** The [[Wave Bender|Wave Benders]] go first on a 24. One hits [[Kit Aluri]] at disadvantage on a 19 for **25 cold damage**; another shoots for bear-form [[Blarg]] and lands on exactly his AC of 18 for **24 cold damage**. [[Topsy]] and [[Turvy]], still stuck in wererat hybrid form and now inside the faerzress, roll a shared Wisdom save — [[Binks Stonevein]] rolls a **natural 20** on their sheet and both twins come fully back to themselves, break off from the [[Stormcaller|Stormcallers]] they were fighting, and use their actions to back away non-threateningly. It is the first unambiguously good thing to happen in the cove.

[[Binks Stonevein]] moves his Trickery duplicate beside himself, then casts **Command** on [[Kit Aluri]] — *"drop"* — and she fails the save on a 13. She drops her weapon; [[Roxy]] snatches it off the sand and runs. Addison rules [[Kit Aluri]] must take the opportunity attack; she is unarmed, and Addison grants a free **Sleight of Hand** check to draw a dagger mid-reaction. She rolls 15, draws, and swings at disadvantage — **natural 20** — for 7 damage. [[Binks Stonevein]] runs 30 feet anyway, prompting a rules exchange about whether a hit stops movement (it does not; that is **Sentinel**, which [[Darby Stonefeather]] has and [[Kit Aluri]] does not). [[Kit Aluri]]: *"Me sitting here going, note to self, get sentinel."*

[[Darby Stonefeather]]'s turn is the session's first comic-into-thematic pivot. Given free rein over her own hallucination, Austin declines the obvious: *"I've decided that I'm not going to try to be murderous, even though that would be the fun thing to do. I look out and instead of my comrades and fallen, fallen enemies, I just see thousands and thousands of stools."* Addison confirms there is almost no actual fungus on the beach — sand, rock, and phosphorescent crystal — and that [[Darby Stonefeather]] is seeing it anyway. She drops her rage, spends all 45 feet of movement, and goes *frolicking*.

The cove itself begins to stand down. The surviving Wave Benders come to on contact (a natural 1 on their attack plus a +6 Wisdom bonus); the [[Stormcaller|Stormcallers]] save at disadvantage on a 14 and **fall back rather than attack**, with [[Indigo]] shouting at them to stand down. Addison adds a **Wave Bender, a Stormcaller and a Scout** as reinforcements, and a *"more lavish mage of a merfolk"* comes up alongside [[Indigo]] — the party's first sight of the High Priest.

[[Kit Aluri]] rolls a **4 on her direction d4** — west — and attacks [[Glabbagool]], hitting for 9 with the greatsword after a Strength check (20) to wrench the blade free, then spends **Second Wind** to heal 8, announcing *"I have 16 HP, so take advantage of that. That's my job."* [[Blarg]], still a bear and down to 6 hit points in that form, uses **Cure Wounds** on a wounded [[Stormcaller]] for 18 — a pure goodwill heal on an enemy who was charmed minutes earlier, and the single act that most shapes the merfolk's later posture toward the party.

Then [[Aeolus]] ends the fight. He casts **Calm Emotions** — a spell he credits directly to his patron, *"thank you to my patron on that spell"* — centred to catch [[Kit Aluri]] and everyone but the frolicking [[Darby Stonefeather]]. [[Kit Aluri]] rolls a 15 against DC 16 and fails; the madness lifts. Addison narrates the 20-foot sphere brightening *"not with actual light"* — spring, budding, the scent of flowers and grapes, the clinking of wine glasses, wine at the back of every throat. A grape vine sprouts from the rock at [[Aeolus]]'s feet. Addison calls for a spellcasting roll (19) and then a **flat luck check against the faerzress** — 11+ good, 15+ very good, 10 or below bad. [[Aeolus]] lands *"very barely bad"*: the vine that grows is **poisonous or necrotic**, and Addison rules he could fix it given time.

[[Amanita de'Champignon]] closes the round by **Misty Step**ping 30 feet and then running another 35 toward [[Darby Stonefeather]]. Addison gives her a spore-borne read: [[Darby Stonefeather]] is hallucinating, the faerzress is acting on her mind, and — importantly — **[[Psilofyr]] is engaging with [[Darby Stonefeather]] through [[Roxy]] as a conduit.** [[Amanita de'Champignon]] does not dispel it. She joins it: *"whoa. They seem to be on a pretty good trip. I'll go join them."* [[Roxy]] had already succeeded on an advantage Wisdom check to reach [[Darby Stonefeather]], appearing inside the imaginary grove munching mushrooms beside her. [[Darby Stonefeather]] hears [[Amanita de'Champignon]] as the mushrooms talking. The exchange that follows becomes the session's running joke and its actual thesis:

> **[[Darby Stonefeather]]:** "There's so much. Did you know that there's thousands of stools out there?"
> **[[Amanita de'Champignon]]:** "Yeah, man. I mean, we're all, like, one huge being."
> **Addison:** "We're all stool."

Addison calls combat there — *"That was only one solid round, so good job there"* — flags *"We're all stool"* as a serious contender for the episode title, and takes a break, noting he is *"feeling a little weird"* and needs to eat.

The break runs long and is almost entirely out-of-character: gnome and dwarf lifespans, kobolds, a mathematically rigorous bit about killing **35,500 rats** to reach level 20 in sixteen years, and a *Fantasy High* reference. [[Kit Aluri]] states her position on the [[Drow]] plainly and out of character — *"the drow have been unjustly treated… we've been fighting them for no reason"* — and is challenged on it by Florian and Matt. Hannah steps away five minutes to collect her father.

On return, Addison lays out **the offer that reshapes the campaign.** The party is at the beach of a large complex: broad sand, tiers of steps and paths, a temple entrance carved into rock behind a waterfall, natural blue crystal formations pulsing toward purple and red, shrines, obelisks, and broken statues of ancient merfolk. There was clearly a recent struggle. Addison flags to Florian that [[Blarg]] senses **a draw of moon power in the crystals** and a tidal influence — at king high tides, most of the ground they are standing on submerges. Then he steps above the table: *"This is my opportunity that I'm laying out for you. **Establish a bastion on this side of the Darklake.**"* The merfolk have retreated here because [[Demogorgon]] surfaced in [[The Darklake]] and every chaotic and evil thing in the lake rallied to him and pushed out the lawful and the good. He introduces the head merfolk [[Stormcaller]] — and explicitly declines to name him: *"this head Stormcaller who I don't have a name for. I'll figure that out later. You can just call them priest or high priest or whatever."*

The audience with the **High Priest** is the session's exposition centrepiece. Addison establishes the [[Stormcaller|Stormcallers]] have **162 hit points each** — *"way stronger, way, like healthier than you"* — and that [[Blarg]]'s goodwill heal registered. The priest confirms the converted merfolk will make a full recovery under his own care over *"many hours and days"*, and that [[Nanny Plunk]]'s influence and [[Demogorgon]]'s presence are receding. He grieves publicly and specifically: [[Nanny Plunk]] was a long-standing trade partner (*"we have done business in the past many, many times with various iterations of her"*); she took two of the merfolk and began the conversion process; she used **their own fog magic against them** to mask the ambush; he alone resisted and got inside. He confirms the merfolk **knew about [[Demogorgon]] and did not warn the kuo-toa in time**, that they had spoken with [[Sloopidoop]] some time ago, and delivers the line that defines his character: *"I thought, to my everlasting shame, regrets, and never again. I thought we had time. Demogorgon tricked me, made us believe we had time."*

[[Amanita de'Champignon]] offers to give the two merfolk [[Darby Stonefeather]] killed a **spore burial** — returning them to the cycle. [[Darby Stonefeather]] asks whether they are okay with it, adding *"I just feel like I'm not worthy of their kindness,"* and rolls **Insight 19**: the priest and his people are patient and empathetic, genuinely shocked, and carrying **shame of their own** for having been possessed and turned against their sanctuary. A few are angry and are quieted by the priest. Addison connects merfolk death rites to the **kuo-toa scavenger goddess** — *"almost the same exact deity… she is their goddess of death and decay. Natural, natural, lawful, good death"* — and confirms open, decomposing underwater burial is the norm, which resolves [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s worry about *"the smashed in head of it all."*

[[Binks Stonevein]] runs the strategic conversation. [[Jimjar]] took [[Eldeth]] east and they are roughly **half a day to a full day behind the party** overland, sped somewhat by Duergar canals. Addison then confirms the party's existing network: **[[Eldeth]] reached the dwarven city and warned King Bruenor — but before she knew about [[Demogorgon]]** — and [[Jimjar]] has an established, indirect connection to **[[Bregan D'aerthe]]** in [[Menzoberranzan]] and vaguely to **One-Eyed Jax**. [[Binks Stonevein]]'s summary of the party's position is one of the session's best lines: *"We are just here by sheer happenstance at this point. Every time we go somewhere bad stuff happens and we're trying to stop it. That's where we're at."*

The priest answers by **opening the bastion**: *"I open the doors of our starlit grotto to you for as long as you require it."* Hot springs, an infirmary ward, food and drink. [[Sloopidoop]] is resting in the ward, **unconscious**, and the priest advises waiting until he wakes before approaching. He also warns this will be *"a long, long swim. A long triathlon. This is an iron Merman."* [[Amanita de'Champignon]] asks about casting **Plant Growth** in its long-cast enrichment mode to permanently improve the bastion's food supply, and the priest grants full access to the merfolk's algal and coral gardens, tidal kelp beds, coral reefs, and geothermal pockets, noting the site has been sacred for **millennia** but has never been a true bastion — *"although it was always prepared as such to potentially become one if or when necessary. It seems our ancestors knew."*

Then [[Amanita de'Champignon]] offers the enrichment *"just so we can be friends"* — as a friend of **[[Psilofyr]]**. The room changes. The priest names him: *"the Carrion King, the fae fungal… the archfey of all Mycelium,"* asks whether [[Amanita de'Champignon]] is a warlock and is told *"no man. Like, I'm just a druid, like doing what I can. Super groovy."* [[Binks Stonevein]] cheerfully adds that the whole party works for him — *"weird thing happened. We kind of got abducted. Weird fae stuff. It's fine. He's cool."* The priest reappraises them, seeing auras and spores with new eyes, and **bows** — putting *"my entire staff of druids and priestesses and my own humble fungal experts"* under [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s command. Addison stresses how far outside protocol this is: *"No high priest of the merfolk culture has ever bowed lower than someone who would by far, especially an outsider, be even level four, five, even considered."* [[Indigo]]'s *"jaw is on the floor."*

Addison does a time check, offers to keep roleplaying or fast-track, and the table votes for **the dream sequences**. The party bathes, eats — *"It's the Hilton"* — and takes a long rest.

**The dream sequences are the back three hours of the session.** Addison runs [[Amanita de'Champignon]] first because Hannah has to leave. Each PC rolls a **private madness Wisdom save** before dreaming, messaged directly to the DM.

[[Amanita de'Champignon]] dreams of surfing an endless glowing barrel on a sea of spores thrown off by the mycelial network, watching renewal and death move through every entity. Addison places her under a sky *"that reminds you perfectly of [[Psilofyr]]'s realm"* — not quite dawn, not quite dusk, very Pacific Northwest — and then sends a **black, purple, roiling tidal wave** at her. Her response is the character's turning point: she names her friends' convictions one by one — *"[[Aeolus]], he's got this goal and someone he cares about… [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s got her family… [[Blarg]]'s got these goblins that take care of him"* — and decides that unlike her usual *"the waves are the waves,"* she is going to **stop this one.** She rides a giant **Zurkhwood** board in half-mushroom form, bonding to it through her toes.

Addison then reveals the dream's frame to everyone. A stable crystal portal opens for each PC, and they all sense [[Amanita de'Champignon]] on the far side. Their dream-selves separate into **bioluminescent fungal soul-shadows**, leaving their bodies visible behind them. And he explains why: while they slept, the High Priest and his druids and clerics channelled protective magic over them, *"empowered by the faerzress itself"*, and with [[Demogorgon]] now emerged and visible to *"the entire material realm and the gods above and the abyss below,"* the celestial beings can act. **The party has the attention of the merfolk pantheon**, and each of them now owns *"your own soul realm, your own little pocket dimension."*

### Themes & Emotional Beats

- **Sanctuary as the reward.** After twenty-two sessions of running, this is the first session where the party stops and is *given* something. Every mechanical gift this session is a **home** — a mushroom cottage, a goblin cave, a steed-side cabin, an island alcove, a stolen plot of the Feywild. The campaign's emotional arithmetic flips from escape to establishment.
- **Shame handled with grace.** The High Priest's whole characterisation is a study in accountability without collapse. He apologises to strangers for a failure that got his own people mind-controlled, names the exact self-deception (*"I thought we had time"*), silences his angry subordinates by reminding them of their own ways, and then opens his doors anyway. It is deliberately contrasted with [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s *"I just feel like I'm not worthy of their kindness."*
- **Kit's ghosts, laid down.** The session's centre of gravity. Addison names the mechanism out loud — that Kit's guilt over her dead is what has kept her closed off from death, from [[Psilofyr]], and from [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s magic — and then gives her the one thing she has never asked for: an army at peace, a doll made real, her mother's songs decoded, and a sky. She defines her own religion as **rest**, and is granted it. The gift she receives is *understanding without speech* — she can comprehend Sylvan and Druidic but not speak them, which is a precise, almost cruel fit for a character whose defining trait is controlling what she gives away.
- **Consent and control inside other people's minds.** [[Amanita de'Champignon]] does not pull [[Darby Stonefeather]] out of the hallucination; she asks (*"You want to come down from that trip now, or are you, like, good?"*) and then joins her in it. [[Blarg]] is allowed to **dodge** [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s wings out of trauma, and Addison honours it. [[Kit Aluri]] passes her save and chooses to yield anyway. Every dream-magic effect this session is opt-in — a deliberate inversion of the charm, madness, and possession that has defined the last three sessions.
- **Dreams with teeth.** Addison's *Matrix* framing from S22 pays off literally: the soul realms are real, the wave was real, and every dream produced physical, permanent changes in the waking bastion. Nobody's dream was decorative.
- **Inheritance you didn't ask for.** [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s dream and [[Kit Aluri]]'s converge without either player planning it. Darby's greatest fear is a role handed down by generations of tradition; Kit's gift is a faith handed down by a mother she can barely remember. One inheritance is a weight, the other is a comfort, and both arrive as something the character never chose.
- **"We are all stool."** A drug joke that becomes the session's genuine thesis inside three hours. It opens as [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s benign hallucination, becomes [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s theology (*"we're all, like, one huge being"*), becomes literal when the party's souls are threaded into a shared mycelial dreamspace, and closes the session as Addison's sign-off.
- **A god's champion in burlap.** [[Amanita de'Champignon]] casually mentions [[Psilofyr]] and a high priest of a millennia-old culture bows to a level-5 outsider. Addison is explicit that this **never happens**. The party has been carrying divine weight since [[Session 11 — Gifts of the Carrion King]]; this is the first time an outside institution has weighed it for them.
- **Table culture.** Kobold lifespans; 35,500 rats; the DM's Google Assistant answering "what do rats eat" out loud mid-scene; *"I am the walrus"*; *"this is an iron Merman"*; [[Echo]] getting swatted out of the sky like a Hail Mary pass and complaining about it for two scenes.

### Encounter Summary

The combat lasted **one round** and ended without a single additional death on either side — the inverse of S22, where the party's own certainty was weaponised against them. What resolved it was not damage. It was three separate acts of restraint: [[Binks Stonevein]] rolling a natural 20 on the twins' Wisdom save and giving them their minds back; [[Blarg]], at 6 hit points in bear form, spending his action healing an enemy who had been shooting at him ten seconds earlier; and [[Aeolus]] spending a spell his patron gave him to make everyone calm instead of making anything dead. The material consequences run in the party's favour. [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s solo dream is the only one that cost anything, requiring two madness saves, the second passed by a single margin.

## Archivist Notes

### Patterns, Progress & Future Implications

- **The campaign changed shape this session.** Twenty-two sessions of forced movement — prison, escape, pursuit, flight across a lake — resolved into a fixed, defensible, allied base with a garrison of 162-HP casters, a food supply, an infirmary, hot springs, and a scouting network. Expect the next stretch to be operated *from* somewhere rather than *toward* somewhere.
- **Restraint outperformed force, and Addison rewarded it explicitly.** Every gain this session traces to a non-violent act: a heal on an enemy, an apology, a burial offer, a joined hallucination, a calm spell. The one round of combat produced zero enemy deaths and total victory. This is a legible design signal and the party should read it as one.
- **Madness is now an escalating, tracked resource with a rising DC.** Confirmed rules: points accumulate; **three points crosses into long-term madness**; the save DC started at **10** and is now **14**, *"and it's only going to get harder."* Current ledger — [[Amanita de'Champignon]] **1 point**; [[Darby Stonefeather]] cured; [[Kit Aluri]] cured; [[Aeolus]] passed; [[Blarg]] and [[Binks Stonevein]] untested this session.
- **The faerzress is a two-sided die, not a hazard.** Addison restated it plainly — *"It's just neutral. It's chaos"* — and then demonstrated both faces in the same session: [[Aeolus]]'s luck check produced a poisonous vine on a low roll, while the same energy channelled through the merfolk priests is what let the party's souls reach their own realms. Expect more flat luck checks with graduated outcomes.
- **The dream framework is now permanent infrastructure.** Six soul realms exist. [[Aeolus]] can reach the Feywild from his bastion space. [[Blarg]] can hold goblin form indefinitely in his. The dreams are not a one-off set piece; they are a new location layer with maintenance requirements (ritual upkeep) attached to each character's physical space.
- **[[Amanita de'Champignon]] has become the party's diplomatic centre of gravity.** She has now converted a hostile hag-ridden cove into an allied bastion and a millennia-old priesthood into subordinates, largely by being cheerfully honest about who she works for. Her leverage is [[Psilofyr]]'s name and she has not yet had to spend it on anything costly.
- **Two beacons were lit that cut both ways.** [[Blarg]]'s moon shrine draws goblinoids — *"the wary ones, the evil ones, will know where you are"* as well as the refugees. And the merfolk bastion is now a fixed, findable point in a lake [[Demogorgon]] controls. The party has traded mobility for a target.
- **The party's supporting network is finally legible.** [[Eldeth]] → King Bruenor (warned, but pre-[[Demogorgon]]); [[Jimjar]] → [[Bregan D'aerthe]] and One-Eyed Jax (indirect); the merfolk → scouting parties across the lake; [[Sloopidoop]] and the kuo-toa remnants (unrecovered). The obvious next move is closing the [[Eldeth]] information gap.
- **Backstory is being paid out in dream currency.** In one night the campaign established Kit's family faith and her mother's hymns, Darby's living father and her refused inheritance, Aeolus's noble decline and living warrior grandmother, and Blarg's goblin-mother/moon-goddess line. [[Binks Stonevein]] is the conspicuous exception and is owed the first slot next session.

- **A recurring motif worth tracking: gifts that are also debts.** Every single reward this session came with an obligation — the pipe requires downtime attunement; the grape requires a joint design with another player; the goblin form requires daily ritual; the hippogriff must return to the astral plane; the Feywild trance requires upkeep; the bastion requires everyone to file a written description. Nothing was given free.

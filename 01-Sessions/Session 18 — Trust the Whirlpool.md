## Character POV Overview

### Kit Aluri — POV Overview

*(Storytelling exception. In-character, in-world. See [[Kit Aluri Journal]] for the running memoir. POV Hard Limits applied — no dice, levels, spell-labels, player or table talk.)*

**Day 7.**

Seven days underground and I have decided the dark has a sense of humor, and it is a cruel one. We came back to the road armed and rested for once — the elf had spent her coin, the goliath had finally learned to call her beast back with the horn, I'd been wearing my tongue out on the under-tongue the others speak — and for a few hours it felt like we were people going somewhere instead of prey running from something. Then the tunnels filled with water, the water started to move, and the fish-priest cocked his head at the current like it had insulted his mother. *This is rougher than I remember.* I have learned to hate that sentence.

No boat. So the goliath did the impossible thing she does and talked the mount into *becoming* one — fed it the glowing wood until its bones spread out like wings and turned to rafts on either side of it, and then the whole mess of us was on the river whether we were ready or not. I have stormed a fortress. I have never been so certain I was about to die as I was on that water. We spun. We slammed. The dwarf went over the side and I put a loop in the rope and threw it true and the priest caught it and we hauled him back up coughing. One thing the army taught me that nothing has un-taught: when a body goes in the water, you do not think, you throw the line.

Then the rock. And the woman on the rock.

She was beautiful in the way that makes the hair on my arms stand up — too beautiful, glowing, reaching out with a kindness no one extends to strangers in this place. I did not trust her for a heartbeat. But the current took the elf under, and then the goliath — gods, the goliath, who I would have sworn could not swim — tied off a rope, handed me the end, and *dove in after her.* I held that rope like it was the only solid thing in the world, and for a while it was, and then I lost sight of her under the churn and the mount and the dark, and I felt the old cold come up my spine. Not anger. The other thing. The fear that I will hold the line and pull and there will be nothing on the end of it.

So I went in too. I am a soldier. I know how to swim, no matter what it looked like from up there. And under the water I saw the truth of the pretty woman — gray-green and long-armed and full of teeth, claws out, not saving anyone, *feeding.* The elf had already turned herself into something with bigger teeth and gotten her jaws around it, the spore-folk hanging on, everyone shouting which shape not to stab. And I climbed the mount's horns, and I came down out of the air with the heavy blade over my head, and as I fell — I cannot explain this and I am not sure I want to — the rock walls lit up, that chaos-glow that lives in the stone here pulsed like a held breath, and the sword *changed* in my hands. Went long and three-pronged, a spear meant for things that live in water and things that come from the other side of the world. Time slowed enough that I felt it choose to do it, and felt that it would do it again for any blade I've carried, the way the crowbar learned to hate the wolf-blooded. I put the new point through her and she came apart.

I'll keep this honest, the way I keep everything in here: in the moment before I landed I called out *Darby, other side* — not because I needed her to hear the words but because I needed her to live. I trust her now. I did not used to trust anyone who'd jump into black water on faith, and now I have one in the party I'd follow in. That's a change I don't have a name for yet.

We didn't get to breathe. Two more of the hags came around the bend, and the priest — the ridiculous, calm, useless-until-now priest — planted himself between them and us and threw walls of water and ice and *told us to trust the whirlpool.* Trust the spinning drain that is trying to drown us. Of course. We did. It chewed us up and spat us out — and when I came up choking, there were lights on the water. Little boats. A town. The town we've been walking toward for seven days, finally real and finally close.

I sat on the elf-turned-crocodile like a raft and looked at the lights and let myself feel it for exactly one breath: we made it. Then I put my back to the open water and started counting exits. Some habits keep you alive. I'm keeping mine.

## Session Analysis

### Narrative Summary

The session opens with a downtime recap. The party is **seven days out from [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Velkynvelve]]** on the road to [[Sloobludop]], guided by the kuo-toa archpriest [[Sloopidoop]]. Addison reconciles the off-screen week: [[Darby Stonefeather]] has finished **attuning to Roxy|Roxy's horn** (with help from [[Amanita de'Champignon]] and [[Aeolus]]), letting her summon and dismiss the mount; several party members have been **learning Undercommon** in their downtime; and the NPC **[[Artaxle]]** has split off from the group, **veering toward the drow city to act as a spy** for the party. The recovered **spell scroll** from the chuul's lair is discussed as **"Sanctuary"** (Amanita and Kit), and Kit experiments with the recovered **kuo-toa trident** (versatile, martial, with the thrown property), while the party recalls the **six shields** looted from the [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Velkynvelve\|Velkynvelve]] armory.

Pressing on, the tunnels turn to **canals and then to a fast-flowing underground river** as the party fully enters the [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake\|Darklake]]. The current is far stronger than Sloopidoop remembers; he warns they cannot proceed without a vessel. With no Zurkhwood to harvest, **Darby coaxes Roxy** — who once magically built a shelter from absorbed [[Zurkhwood]] — to transform: at the horn's call, fungal "wings" of bone and root spread from Roxy's saddlebags and harden into **two large rafts, one on either side of the mount** (Survival 17). The buoyant Roxy immediately starts drifting; the party scrambles aboard in a comedic low-stakes initiative (Kit lands a natural 20; Aeolus a natural 1, then **Misty Steps** aboard cleanly). [[Amanita de'Champignon]] lashes her **shield to her quarterstaff** as a makeshift oar/keel.

**The River Run (skill challenge).** What follows is a sustained whitewater sequence ("imagine Splash Mountain"): the party makes repeated Strength/Dexterity saves and steering checks as Roxy-the-raft bounces through the rapids. They bang a wall (4 bludgeoning to a raft; the DM rules **each raft has 20 HP**), spin counterclockwise (a DC 13 group Dexterity check rights them), and **[[Blarg]] is thrown overboard** (Addison rolls a natural 1 for him). [[Kit Aluri]] loops the rope and throws it true (Dex 21); Sloopidoop, swimming alongside "like a sea otter," gets it around Blarg and the party hauls him back aboard — **Blarg takes a point of exhaustion**. A brief calm stretch follows, then [[Binks Stonevein]] (Perception 18, steering from Roxy's horns) spots — too late — a **massive boulder mid-channel with a beautiful mermaid standing atop it.** Binks shouts to ask Sloopidoop if mermaids are friendly ("I do not think so") and then "Boulder!"; the raft strikes (Roxy takes 8 bludgeoning), and **[[Topsy]], [[Turvy]], Amanita, and [[Stool]] are thrown into the water.**

**The Hag Revealed.** Amanita (failing her swim from the **disadvantage of her new heavy non-metal armor**) is swept downstream and submerged, with **four rounds of air** (Con +4). She smears a handful of **glowing mushroom paste over her face** (Arcana 22) so the party can spot her and so she can see underwater — and watches the "mermaid" dive off the boulder and swim toward her. Insight reveals the mermaid's body shifting **gray-green, long-armed, teeth sharpening, hair turning oily black** — it is **not** saving her, it is reaching out to **attack.** Initiative is rolled.

**Combat — The Green Sea Hag.** The hag, hidden under **Disguise Self / illusory magic**, multiattacks the submerged Amanita (two claws, **15 slashing + 2 poison = 17**). The party's perception is split by the illusion: [[Darby Stonefeather]], having tied a rope to herself and handed the end to Kit, **dives in to "rescue the mermaid"** but is fooled (Insight 9) and believes the creature benevolent. [[Aeolus]] sees through it (Insight 17 → recognizes a **green [[Sea Hag]]**), and with **Echo** the raven flapping overhead reminding him the patron's fey nature, identifies the creature as fey (immune to *Sleep*). He casts **[[Phantasmal Force]]**, conjuring an illusory **ten-foot shark** in the hag's mind (she fails her Int save — Int 12 vs DC 15), dealing recurring psychic damage. **Amanita wild-shapes into a [[Crocodile]]** (a level-4 druid option, clarified mid-session), still glowing, with Stool clinging on; she **grapples the hag in her jaws** (8 piercing). **Stool** lands **pacifying spores** (hag poisoned → disadvantage on saves). [[Topsy]] misses with a drow hand crossbow; **[[Binks Stonevein]] casts [[Guiding Bolt]] with advantage** (grappled target), 16 damage — and **the illusion drops**, the hag now glowing for the next attacker.

**Kit's Birthday Strike.** With the hag glowing, grappled, and poisoned, **[[Kit Aluri]]** uses **Second Wind** to convert a failed Animal Handling check into a success, gets her footing on Roxy's horns, and **leaps off, greatsword raised**, rolling a **natural 20**. As she falls — and as the **[[faerzress]]** pulses through the rock walls — her **greatsword magically transforms into a trident** mid-strike. Addison rules this a new, permanent weapon property (improvised as a birthday gift, "rule of cool"): **Kit can now reshape her greatsword, with concentration, into any weapon she is proficient with and has previously carried**, and the resulting weapon **deals extra damage to fey and aquatic creatures** — explicitly paralleling her **silvered crowbar** (extra damage to lycanthropes). She plunges the trident through the hag (crit + Sneak Attack + Precision/Trip), and **kills it.** The kill pushes Kit over the **milestone threshold**; the DM lets her **level to 5** on the spot (she takes **Dual Wielder**). She emerges riding the popped-open corpse like a raft, the hag skewered over her shoulder "like a fishing pole."

**Trust the Whirlpool.** As the party drifts on, the muffled "singing" they'd been half-hearing turns to **screaming** — and rounding the bend they find **two more green hags** locked in combat with **Sloopidoop**, who is holding them off with water and ice. He calls out: ***"Let the whirlpool take you. It will take you straight to [Sloobludop]. Do not wait up for me. I will find you there. Go. Trust the whirlpool."*** The party agrees; Roxy dissolves back into spores and the horn; everyone is **flushed down the whirlpool** (7 bludgeoning each, plus Con saves vs. a level of exhaustion — Amanita fails). They erupt from the far end into open water: a **sea of faint lights, fishing boats, and docks** — they have finally reached **[[Sloobludop]]**. The session ends there, "a group of strangers just exploded out of the water." Out-of-game, the next session is set for **July 5**.

### Themes & Emotional Beats

- **Found family / earned trust:** The session's emotional core is **Kit and Darby**. Darby — established as barely able to swim — dives into black water on faith to save a stranger; Kit holds the rope, then goes in after her, and consciously narrates *"I trust Darby; there's no reason she'd get in the water if she couldn't swim."* Kit's reluctant-loner armor cracks: she follows someone into danger on trust, and says so.
- **Nature reclaimed as a tool / Roxy's evolution:** The mount that once built a shelter now *becomes the vehicle.* Darby's bond with Roxy (speak-with-animals coaxing, the horn) is the literal thing that carries the party through. The party's reliance on fungal/natural magic deepens.
- **Illusion vs. truth (the hag):** A recurring Underdark motif — nothing is what it appears. The "beautiful mermaid reaching out to help" is a predator; only those who *look past the glamour* (Aeolus's insight, Kit's distrust) survive it, while the trusting (Darby) are nearly drawn in. Mirrors the campaign's larger "everyone outside the party is evil at some point" running joke (Binks: "we trust the mushrooms").
- **The chaos (faerzress) as living force:** Kit's weapon transformation is tied explicitly to the **faerzress pulsing in the walls** — the same worsening chaotic energy the Society of Brilliance studies is now **empowering** a PC's gear, not just driving madness. A new, generative face of the campaign's central mystery.
- **"Traumatic level-up":** The table articulates a theme directly — Kit's growth is framed in-fiction as a fear-and-adrenaline response to watching a friend in danger ("like when moms lift cars off their babies"). Power earned through crisis and care, not grinding.
- **Comedy under pressure:** Darby's "stuck between a rock, sea, and a hard place," the absent-minded-academic theory of Sloopidoop, "Dragon drop," and Kit riding the dead hag like a raft keep a near-drowning combat buoyant.

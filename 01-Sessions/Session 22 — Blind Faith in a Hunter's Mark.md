## POV Overview

### The Things We Carried in the Dark: A Soldier's Memoir

**Day — the same day we lost the fish-town. Two hours of water and we were already somewhere worse.**

The cove was beautiful and I hated it on sight.

I count exits. That's the first thing I do. There weren't any. One way in around the bend, rock walls, fog thick enough to lean on, and a witch standing fifteen feet above us on an outcropping with her hand out over the water like she was warming it. Bioluminescent crystal in the sand. Plankton glowing in the shallows. Very pretty. Nowhere to go.

She said fate delivered us to her. She said she'd have her revenge and turn us into her own sisters. I've heard grander speeches from worse people, but she meant it, and that's the part that counts.

[[Indigo]] knew her name. Of course he did. He talked to her the way you talk to a rival you've been undercutting for years, all flourish and courtesy, and I stood there dripping and thought about the last time I traded with people like her. You don't purge hags. You deal with them and you watch your hands afterward.

Then he mentioned — after the banter, after the accent, after the whole performance — that this cove belongs to his people, that there are merfolk here, that they're innocents. He buries the lead every single time. I've told him. I'll tell him again.

[[Amanita de'Champignon]] put the spores through us before anyone could argue about it, and six heads shared one thought at once, and I still haven't decided how I feel about that.

We fought.

[[Stool]] went first, and I will never get used to that little mushroom being the fastest thing on the field. He hopped off the boat, blinked across the water with the waterskin over his head, landed on the witch's skull and shook [[Glabbagool]] out onto her like a man emptying a bucket. She never saw it coming. Green light poured down her face. I would have applauded if I hadn't been busy.

[[Aeolus]] pulled a cat out of nowhere. A large one, ginger, grinning with too many teeth, furious about it. He screamed at it to attack and it did. That's a fine trade — scream, and something with claws goes where you point.

Then the fog started throwing things at us. Cold, mostly. Snowballs the size of a man that hit [[Aeolus]] hard enough to frost him over. [[Blarg]] pulled moonlight out of his hands and put him back together, and I noted where the throwers were and could not see a single one of them, which I did not enjoy.

[[Topsy]] and [[Turvy]] set foot on the sand and lost themselves.

I want to be careful about this part. They went over into the rat shape, both of them, and they were in pain, and they were scared, and they couldn't hold onto their own heads. [[Blarg]] got the moon into them and steadied them. But I stood there in the shallows and watched [[Topsy]] struggle against her own body, and I have a crowbar on my belt that used to be an ordinary crowbar, and there's nothing useful to say about that so I'm not going to say it.

[[Indigo]] vanished into the fog. Went to pull his people out before the witch could finish them. I reached for him — not the way I reached that other time, just enough to feel whether he was dying — and I felt him. A pulse buried in stone and sand, deep, and not dying. In danger, but not the kind that pulls at you.

I put my mark on the witch and hit her. The sword's still short, still shaped the way I left it in the last fight, and it fought me a little when I asked it to hold that shape. It gave. I hooked her leg out from under her and she caught herself. Fine. There'd be time.

The dark rumbled while we were catching our breath. The whole cave lit up in that sick faerzress glow, all of us feeling something enormous roll over in the water somewhere behind the world. We held together anyway.

And then we learned what she was actually doing.

The other hags in the fog were not hags. They were merfolk. Charmed, hollowed, rebuilt, and the storm she was raising would have put all of us in a sleep we don't wake from and made us into the next set of sisters. That's the cycle. That's what [[Blarg]] saw in her posture at the start and called grief. She loses her sisters and she makes new ones out of whoever the water brings her.

I'd almost respect it if she'd picked anyone else.

[[Amanita de'Champignon]] tore the fog open. Whole stretch of it just gone, and there they were — the singers with the staffs, the spear-throwers, the two hags. [[Darby Stonefeather]] got to one of the throwers and killed it, and it was a charmed thing that didn't know what it was doing, and she knew that, and the rage had her and she did it anyway. That mace of hers wants something. She's not going to like finding out what.

Then the two hags smiled at the same time and everyone's eyes stopped working.

I looked at where the witch had been standing and there was [[Binks Stonevein]] standing there instead. And [[Binks Stonevein]] was also behind me, where he'd been the whole time. Two of him. I asked him through the spores how many of him there were and both of them answered at once and I felt my own certainty slide sideways — because I *knew*, absolutely, in my chest, that the one in front of me was him. That's what she did. She didn't hide. She put my own conviction in my mouth and made me swallow it.

The only thing that didn't lie was my mark. I put it on her myself, hours ago, before any of this. It sat on her and it did not care what my eyes were doing.

So I stood there in front of my friend's face with my sword out and every part of me screaming, and I made a joke, because that's what I do when I'm about to do something unforgivable. I told him if I was stabbing him, he needed to yell *sunglasses*. He said *what the hell are you talking about*. He would have. He didn't say the word.

I stabbed him.

The first swing went through nothing — she'd already gone invisible and slipped a step, and my blade passed through a picture of a gnome I'd die for. Then she came apart back into herself, and I put her down.

I turned around to the real one and said *your Binks*. He flipped me off. I've never been happier to see that hand.

The fog died with her. The link between her and the thing in the lake snapped. The whole cove opened up — blue crystal, blue lichen, water like glass — and it was beautiful, and I registered that the way you register a wall you might have to climb. The merfolk collapsed where they stood. The other two hags screamed and ran north. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] caught one in a fist of fungus. The other got away carrying something of the witch inside it.

[[Indigo]] came out of the water at the top of the cove holding up a half-dead kuo-toa priest who I last saw drowning a town to save us.

And then it got me too.

I can feel it sitting behind my eyes. The nearest living thing keeps looking like a target. Right now the nearest living thing is that cat.

[[Binks Stonevein]] came over. He always comes over. He looked at my face and knew — he's the only one who'd know, he's seen this face on me exactly once before, the day I watched [[Topsy]] breathe again and came apart on the moss. He made a joke about it. Then he did something to me and I couldn't stop laughing.

I'm still laughing. It's echoing off the walls of a cove full of people we half-saved.

I don't know what I am right now. I know what I'm holding.

## Session Analysis

### Narrative Summary

The session opens above table with [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s in-character "previously on" recap of S21 — the [[Demogorgon]] rising, the tidal wave, [[Indigo]] leading the party out by boat, and the introduction of a new figure "who also seems groovy but maybe a little bit scary." Addison awards a point of heroic inspiration for it. [[Binks Stonevein]] reports he has updated the party inventory on D&D Beyond: **1,800 gold** total including the platinum, a string of pearls and healing potions, all left undivided — "it's a communist party at this point. We're all sharing."

Addison then does two pieces of framing. First, he confirms the party was pulled into the Shoals shortly after leaving [[Sloobludop]], got around the whirlpool, and ran into a **Green Hag** on the far side — not the one they fought before, but **[[Nanny Plunk]]**, faced in her true form because "[[Aeolus]] at the end of last session dictated the Fates." Second, he flags that [[Demogorgon]] — *the* Demogorgon, a demon prince, "not Stranger Things" — has left an impact, and that **dream sequences** are coming: he asks every player to spend five minutes designing a dream for their character, warns that dreams will involve rolls, and invokes *The Matrix* — "if your mind thinks it's real, then it'll have real effects on your character's bodies." He also asks for an above-table status round on level and subclass. ⚠️ **Neither the dream sequences nor the status round happened this session** — the encounter consumed the entire runtime.

The party arrives in a **starlit water cove** choked in unnatural magical fog, drifting out of the bone teeth into swift shallow water with bioluminescent crystal in the sand and plankton in the water. [[Nanny Plunk]] stands on a rock outcropping roughly 15 feet above them, hand held out over the dark lake in a meditative ritual, and greets them with a mixture of surprise and expectation: *"fate has delivered you unto me. I shall have my revenge and turn you into my own sisters."* [[Indigo]] recognises her, names her aloud for the first time, and bickers with her in a "very suave Spanish accent" — Addison performs the whole exchange as an [[Indigo|Inigo Montoya]] bit, complete with *"I do not think it means what you think it means."*

A round of knowledge checks establishes the picture. [[Aeolus]] (Insight 18) recalls hag lore from his patron: they are fiendish rather than brutal, dealing in words, illusions, pacts and accords — "my scratches are very long and jagged" — and [[Nanny Plunk]] is a seasoned hag long resident in this dark lake realm, in an odd business-competitor relationship with [[Indigo]]. [[Kit Aluri]] (History 19) recalls the same from her dealings in [[Mantol-Derith]]: green hags in the region get *dealt with* rather than purged. [[Blarg]] (Insight 21) reads her posture as long-held grief — an endless cycle of ritual reincarnation in which the surviving hag converts others into green hags. [[Binks Stonevein]] (Insight 25) reads her threat level as very high and notes she is **alone**, which hags are not, and that her hurt and anger are a deliberate affect. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] (Nature 26) reads the fog as magical and saturated with the hag's conflicting emotions, with *something powerful* happening behind it that they cannot yet pierce. [[Darby Stonefeather]] (Nature 8) is "focused on keeping [[Roxy]] steady." Addison adds that she is drawing energy from the **faerzress** and from the chaotic aura [[Demogorgon]] released.

[[Amanita de'Champignon]] puffs their pipe and casts **Rapport Spores** — "super groovy edition" — linking the party telepathically. The party debates: [[Amanita de'Champignon]] finds the sea witch fascinating and wants to see what she is doing in the fog; [[Binks Stonevein]] floats leaving her be or talking; [[Darby Stonefeather]] asks what their experience with hags is and gets the honest answer that they killed the last one. [[Kit Aluri]]: *"there's no way I killed the shit out of that hag."* [[Indigo]] cuts it off telepathically — the hag has taken over the cove of his people and allies, **there are merfolk here, innocents, and other Tritons** — and advises against parley. [[Binks Stonevein]]'s response is the session's first big laugh: *"you really buried the lead there."* The witch notices the spores and the party's suspicious silence during her monologue, and initiative is called.

**Round one** runs long and establishes the shape of the fight. [[Stool]] — who has levelled up and gained speed — wades off [[Roxy]], **Misty Step**s onto [[Nanny Plunk]]'s head and empties the fungal waterskin over her; she fails her Dexterity save (a 3) and is covered in glowing green ooze. Addison confirms the waterskin has become, through [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s slow attunement, a dimensional container for [[Glabbagool]] — "a genie's lamp," or as [[Binks Stonevein]] puts it, "a fungal pokeball." [[Roxy]] stays in ship form as a shield and escape route but fails her checks and is pinned by the current. [[Aeolus]] casts **Summon Fey** at 3rd level and chooses the **Grinning Cat** form — a large, ginger, fanged fey cat in bluish-violet, mood set to *Fuming* — screams the command *"Attack it. The hag,"* earns a bonus damage die on a 25 performance check, and lands a **Fey Blade** hit for 13. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] walks into the fog and, on a 22 Arcana, traces a mycelial line straight to a hidden point — Addison rules that the fog is **not** [[Nanny Plunk]]'s creation, though her energy permeates it — then activates **Symbiotic Entity**, mushrooms popping out along their flexed arms. [[Topsy]] and [[Turvy]] set foot on sand and lose control, dropping fully into **wererat hybrid form**, in pain and effectively suffering short-term madness. [[Binks Stonevein]] moves behind the hag and summons his **Spiritual Weapon** — not a pick, an **actual lute**, played like an electric guitar; it misses and bangs off the rock with a "BANGING electrical riff."

The cove answers. A **merfolk Skirmisher** leaps from the fog at [[Amanita de'Champignon]] as [[Indigo]] shouts in dismay in a language nobody understands and darts off into the fog — out of the party's hands but still active. The Skirmisher hits for 3 piercing and 2 cold with an ice-rimed spear; [[Amanita de'Champignon]] answers with a **Chill Touch** opportunity attack for 8 necrotic and, on a free 17 Insight, learns the merfolk is **charmed** — relaying it through the spores as *"this guy's super high on something. I don't think they know what they're doing."* Two unseen **Wave Benders** hurl miniaturised storms of water and cold; [[Blarg]] dodges, [[Aeolus]] takes **20 cold damage**. [[Darby Stonefeather]] triangulates one on a 21 Survival — Addison gives her a private vision of a horizontal whirlwind of ethereal ax beak feathers ringing the Wave Bender — and rages. Addison then narrates the fog thickening into a storm cloud, ozone and blue arcs building, a ritual nearing completion. [[Nanny Plunk]] shrieks and claws [[Glabbagool]] twice. [[Blarg]] heals [[Aeolus]] for 24 with a 2nd-level **Cure Wounds** described as moonlight melting the frost off him, and because he explicitly channeled the moon, Addison grants him moon-intuitive vision of the wavelength around [[Topsy]] and [[Turvy]] — he steadies them, removing their disadvantage. [[Turvy]] bows to him and swings at the Skirmisher, missing twice.

[[Kit Aluri]] closes the round. She asks after [[Indigo]] and gets his telepathic answer — *"I needed to save the leaders before they became infected by the witch's magic… Work to slow the surge and keep their attention. I will work on the storm"* — then spends her **reaction** reaching for him through the faerzress and feels a pulse hidden in stone and deep in the sand: in danger, but not mortal danger. She closes to reach, **Hunter's Mark**s [[Nanny Plunk]], and attacks with her shape-shifting greatsword still in short-sword form from the last fight; Addison requires a **Strength saving throw** for the sustained shape (19, passed), then a 21 to hit for 7 + 5 = **12 damage**. Her **Trip Attack** fails on a DC 15 Strength save the hag makes by exactly 15.

At the break, Addison offers the party a choice: pool their energies to hold their initiative against the chaotic faerzress, or reroll. They debate through the break — [[Amanita de'Champignon]] and [[Aeolus]] want to keep an order that puts most of the party ahead of every enemy — and on return Addison runs it as a **group madness check**: flat Wisdom saves, all passed. They feel an echoing deep rumble from [[Demogorgon]] and see the faerzress light up around them, and they hold.

**Round two** is where the encounter's real stakes land. [[Stool]] shakes [[Glabbagool]] out for the last time and spends the rest of his turn assisting [[Kit Aluri]]; [[Glabbagool]] manifests in full and attempts to **engulf** [[Nanny Plunk]], who makes the Dexterity save and escapes. Addison then delivers [[Indigo]]'s telepathic briefing, the session's central exposition: [[Indigo]] has found the **High Priest of the merfolk** and **[[Sloopidoop]]**, both alive but weak; there must always be a **triad** of hags; and the storm is being cast and summoned by **ritual merfolk who are charmed and forced into it**. If the storm completes, the whole party goes into a dream state and coma, their bodies charmed, used, and eventually turned into hags. They have this round and one more. [[Kit Aluri]]: *"Sweet. We're all getting hag powers, you guys."* [[Binks Stonevein]]: *"I don't want to be a hag."*

[[Darby Stonefeather]] gets a "loving nudge" from [[Roxy]] on a 13 Animal Handling — advantage plus an extra 1d4 of magical bludgeoning from fungal wood engraved into her fists. [[Aeolus]] moves ("it's not unusual for him to use people as meat shields"), **Misty Step**s and takes **Refreshing Step** for a maximum 10 temporary hit points, then lands both beams of **Eldritch Blast** for 24 combined; his cat hits again. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] casts **Dispel Magic** at 3rd level on the fog itself, clears a 19 on the check, and **the entire section of fog collapses** — revealing the first **Stormcaller** channelling a staff whose glow connects to two other points, three merfolk, and the **Green Hag**. Only **two** green hags are visible, confirming the party's guess that the third is being made. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] casts **Shillelagh**, moves on the hag, dodges an opportunity attack, and misses with the **mithral war pick** even after burning heroic inspiration on the reroll. Addison offers a standing ruling here: **non-lethal damage is available any time — just declare it.** [[Topsy]] finishes the first Skirmisher; Addison notes "the twins work together to take down the first Skirmisher." [[Binks Stonevein]] states his goal plainly — *"My goal is to kill [[Nanny Plunk]]"* — casts a 2nd-level **Guiding Bolt** and follows with the lute for 8.

The enemies escalate hard. A **Wave Bender** lands a massive hit on [[Glabbagool]] and another shoots across the field at [[Amanita de'Champignon]] for **20 cold damage and prone**. [[Darby Stonefeather]] leaps a rock and reaches the leftmost Wave Bender, hits at advantage, and is asked whether she wants to put it down or keep it alive — **she knows it is charmed and kills it anyway**, narrating that her rage takes over and she "will not be able to control myself": a grab to the throat and the mace coming down. Addison awards heroic inspiration for leaning into the character, then requires an **Intelligence saving throw** on the mace itself. [[Darby Stonefeather]] rolls a 7 and fails: she feels a connection to the weapon "not one that entirely puts you at ease. There seems to be some kind of will to it. Not a sentience per se" — **madness level +1**, and a d100 of 70 gives her **vivid hallucinations and disadvantage on ability checks**.

The **Stormcaller** turns the fog vicious and electrified and forces party-wide Dexterity saves against lightning: 10 lightning + 10 cold for failures, half for successes. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] burns **Absorb Elements**; [[Darby Stonefeather]] burns **Stone's Endurance** and rolls a 1 on the d12.

Then [[Nanny Plunk]] acts. Addison calls for insight from [[Amanita de'Champignon]] and a small group insight from [[Kit Aluri]], [[Binks Stonevein]] and [[Aeolus]]. The result: the green hag in front of [[Amanita de'Champignon]] is **certainly a converted merfolk**, recoverable with careful healing and tending; **[[Nanny Plunk]] is the focal point**, the one true hag, and dropping her will not un-make the others but will significantly dampen the fog, the storm and the ritual. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] broadcasts it: *"guys, we got to take out Nanny. Like, this one's not even a real hag. Just get the big guy."*

The two hags smile in unison and shimmer. **[[Amanita de'Champignon]] sees [[Binks Stonevein]]. [[Kit Aluri]] sees [[Binks Stonevein]]. [[Binks Stonevein]] sees [[Kit Aluri]].** Two Kits, two Binkses — strike at disadvantage or find another way. Two more Skirmishers spear [[Darby Stonefeather]] for 7 and 6 after her resistance. [[Blarg]], watching [[Darby Stonefeather]] kill a charmed victim and hallucinate, decides she is a danger to herself and everyone, and casts **Hold Person on his own party member**; [[Darby Stonefeather]] rolls a **natural 20** and rages straight through it. Addison offers [[Blarg]] heroic inspiration to reroll and **[[Blarg]] refuses it** — "I think it's fine that she breaks through it in her rage. That makes sense to me" — then closes the distance screaming *"Darby, you gotta stop. Calm down. Please. Please,"* and wild shapes into a **bear** to absorb whatever comes: *"I'm gonna give you the worst trip of your life right now."* [[Turvy]] attacks the Stormcaller and breaks its concentration, unravelling more of the fog and revealing two more Skirmishers and another Stormcaller.

[[Kit Aluri]]'s turn is the centrepiece of the session and the source of its title. She is looking at [[Binks Stonevein]] standing exactly where [[Nanny Plunk]] stood, and [[Binks Stonevein]] standing behind her where he has been all along. Her first insight check leaves her memory of the hag's position "blackened out." Her second, at a lower DC because both Binkses answer her telepathically at once, actively convinces her that **the Binks in front of her is the true Binks** — precisely the wrong conclusion. [[Blarg]] points out that the hag was carrying her **Hunter's Mark**, and Addison rules the mark **overrides** the insight results at the cost of restricting her to an action *or* a bonus action. [[Kit Aluri]] narrates the whiplash — confident, then not, then confident about the other one — shakes her head, and attacks. Disadvantage from her own screaming instincts cancels against [[Stool]]'s advantage into a flat roll: **21 to hit**, 10 + 3 Hunter's Mark + 4 sneak attack = **17 damage**. Her line as she does it: *"Binks. If I'm stabbing you, I need you to yell sunglasses right now"* — the party's established code word for darkness. Addison: *"Weekend at Bernie's."* She **Action Surge**s and spends a superiority die on **Trip Attack**; this time the hag fails the DC 15 Strength save, and the second attack lands at 20. Addison reveals that the first swing passed **through an illusory [[Binks Stonevein]]** — [[Nanny Plunk]] had gone invisible and moved but had not gotten clear — and her form coalesces for the killing blow. [[Kit Aluri]] asks for a point of madness for the turn and gets it, plus heroic inspiration. She turns to the real [[Binks Stonevein]] and says *"your Binks."* He flips her off.

The aftermath resolves the encounter without ending the fight. **The psychic faerzress connection between [[Nanny Plunk]] and [[Demogorgon]] is cut.** The storm dies, the fog thins to mist, and the whole expanse of the cove is visible for the first time — bright blue azure crystal, blue lichen, the surviving merfolk suddenly stunned and in agonising pain. The two remaining hags' invisibility drops, they screech, and they flee north up the coast. Ice shards and glowing water bubbles descend to trap the merfolk: **[[Indigo]]** and a **very, very weakened [[Sloopidoop]]** are holding the trap together, Sloopidoop concentrating through his staff, with **another merfolk priest** emerging at the top of the map.

In the wrap-up round, [[Amanita de'Champignon]] chases the nearer green hag — the one they know was a merfolk — trying to talk it down (*"You're not a hag. Come on, come back to me"*) and casts **Plant Growth**, mycelial tendrils bursting from the nearest azure crystal to lock it in place. **The second hag escapes**; Addison says the essence of [[Nanny Plunk]]'s soul went into it on her death and empowered its flight, and that it was likely another converted merfolk being made into a new Nanny Plunk. [[Darby Stonefeather]], still raging and hallucinating, hunts the nearest stunned Skirmisher; [[Blarg]] throws his bear body between them, and Addison converts the attack into a flat check — [[Darby Stonefeather]] grazes [[Blarg]] for **10 damage** and still drops the merfolk for 10, leaving it alive. **[[Indigo]] shouts "Stop" in Aquan**, [[Darby Stonefeather]] fails the Wisdom save on a 5, and is magically held; her rage drops but the hallucinations remain. [[Kit Aluri]] rolls her madness d100: **55 — must use her action each round to attack the nearest creature.** The nearest creature is the Grinning Cat. [[Aeolus]], meanwhile, is petting the cat, and earns heroic inspiration for it.

[[Binks Stonevein]] walks up to check on [[Kit Aluri]] because she has been asking a lot of questions. She describes what he sees: confusion turning into utter exhaustion, *"the Kit who just saw [[Topsy]]… come back to life. She's back. She's in a real bad place."* He intuits the short-term madness, makes a joke of it — *"well, at least you got her"* — and casts **Tasha's Hideous Laughter** on her. Addison closes the session on Kit wanting to attack her friend, who was also not her friend, having just killed the hag that was her friend, erupting into hysterics that echo through **the Mermaid Cove**. Initiative resumes next session.

### Themes & Emotional Beats

- **Wearing your friend's face.** The session's spine. [[Nanny Plunk]] does not hide from the party; she makes the party's own certainty into the weapon. [[Kit Aluri]]'s two insight checks make her *more* wrong, not less, and the only thing that survives is the mark she placed herself, hours earlier, with her own hand. The campaign has repeatedly asked Kit to trust something outside herself — a god in S21, a patron, a prophecy. Here the answer is that the only trustworthy thing in the cove was her own prior work.
- **Victims, not enemies.** Every merfolk in the fight is a charmed captive. Addison builds the entire encounter so the party has to notice — [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s free insight, the standing non-lethal ruling, the recoverable green hags — and then hands [[Darby Stonefeather]] a rage that makes noticing irrelevant. She knows, and kills anyway, and narrates it as loss of control rather than choice: *"Not by choice. This is not a 'I want to do this,' but I think this makes sense."*
- **Madness as the new currency.** Two PCs end the session mad, and neither by enemy action — [[Darby Stonefeather]] from her own weapon, [[Kit Aluri]] by asking for it. Kit explicitly requests the madness point as the cost of what she did: *"I fully deserve a point of madness for that turn."* The [[Demogorgon]]-poisoned faerzress is no longer background lore; it is a per-round tax.
- **Grief that reproduces itself.** [[Blarg]]'s insight names it early: an endless cycle of ritual reincarnation, a hag who keeps losing her sisters and keeps making new ones out of whoever the water brings her. The reveal that the "hags" are converted merfolk turns the whole encounter from a monster fight into an interrupted assembly line — and the escaping hag carrying [[Nanny Plunk]]'s essence means the line is already restarting.
- **Party as each other's hazard.** [[Blarg]] casts a control spell on [[Darby Stonefeather]]; [[Darby Stonefeather]] hits [[Blarg]] for 10; [[Kit Aluri]] stabs [[Binks Stonevein]]'s face; [[Binks Stonevein]] ends the session by incapacitating [[Kit Aluri]] with laughter. Every one of those is an act of care, and all four are attacks.
- **Telepathy as intimacy and exposure.** [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s Rapport Spores carry the tactical picture, the charm diagnosis, and [[Indigo]]'s briefing — and then get weaponised, when both Binkses answer [[Kit Aluri]] through the same link at the same time. The link that makes the party fast is also what makes the illusion convincing.
- **Table culture.** The fungal pokeball; the lute that is an electric guitar; "we're all getting hag powers, you guys"; "Weekend at Bernie's"; "I'm gonna give you the worst trip of your life right now"; [[Aeolus]] petting a summoned cat during a cliffhanger and being rewarded for it.

But the win came at a cost the party is still paying at the session's end: two of the three hags escaped, one carrying [[Nanny Plunk]]'s essence and likely becoming her successor; a charmed, innocent merfolk is dead by [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s hand; [[Blarg]] took 10 damage from his own ally; and **both [[Darby Stonefeather]] and [[Kit Aluri]] end the session under short-term madness with combat still live.** [[Topsy]] and [[Turvy]] are stuck in wererat hybrid form. The surviving merfolk are contained rather than freed, and the party has inherited responsibility for a cove full of victims, two priests, one restrained hag, and a manhunt up the north coast.

Tactically the encounter was decided by three plays: [[Stool]]'s round-one Misty Step delivering [[Glabbagool]] onto the hag's head before she could act, [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s 3rd-level **Dispel Magic** on the fog turning an invisible fight into a visible one, and [[Kit Aluri]]'s refusal to trust her own senses over her own Hunter's Mark. The party's decision at the break to *hold* their initiative rather than reroll — motivated by having most of the party act before every enemy — is what made a two-round deadline survivable.

### Patterns, Progress & Future Implications

- **The party's tactical signature is now "act before the enemy, all together."** They chose at the break to hold an initiative order that put five of six PCs plus [[Stool]] and [[Roxy]] ahead of every enemy, explicitly reasoning it out ([[Amanita de'Champignon]]: "I think it's good that a lot of us have initiative before any of the merfolk or hags can go"). That decision is what made a two-round deadline survivable. Expect them to defend that ordering again.

Addison is running all five as full initiative entries.

Addison rewarded both with heroic inspiration. That loop — do the emotionally costly thing, take the madness, get inspiration — is now an established table incentive.
- **Addison is building the encounter around a moral out and then testing whether the party takes it.** The free charm-diagnosis insight, the standing non-lethal ruling, the "recoverable with careful healing" ruling on the converted hags: all three exist so the party *can* save the merfolk. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] took every one of them; [[Darby Stonefeather]] took none, and was penalised in-fiction rather than mechanically punished.

The lore round predicted the boss mechanic. That is a pattern worth watching: this table's knowledge checks are load-bearing foreshadowing.
- **[[Demogorgon]] is now an environmental condition, not an encounter.** He is not present, and he still caused: the faerzress corruption powering [[Nanny Plunk]], the group madness check, the rumble during the interlude, and (via the severed link) the ritual's collapse. The party's exposure to him is measured in madness points.
- **Two threads point directly at next session.** [[Kit Aluri]] must attack the nearest creature — currently [[Aeolus]]'s Grinning Cat — and [[Darby Stonefeather]] is held, hallucinating, with an unresolved relationship to her mace. Combat resumes mid-initiative.

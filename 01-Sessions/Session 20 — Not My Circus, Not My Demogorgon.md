## Character POV Overview

### Kit Aluri — POV Overview

**Day unknown — the day the water stood up.**

We watched it happen from a fog that was half his and half the lake's. Indigo pulled steam off the canal with a turn of that trident like it was nothing, and we stood in it, unseen, while the fish-priests marched my people to a butcher's altar. I have stood prisoner in front of an altar before. Watching my friends do it while I stayed hidden was worse. Every soldier's instinct I have said *break cover*, and every one of them was wrong, so I held.

The altar was a stretched hide and dead things pinned up like trophies, with a gold basin under it drinking blood that never stopped coming. Her — the daughter, the one this whole rotten schism belonged to — she came out of a squat little hovel like a queen coming out of a palace, and you could see it on her, that perfect certainty. Nothing in the world is more dangerous than somebody who is completely sure.

Then the old priest hit his own daughter with his scepter, and the whole plan happened at once. Lightning and spores and screaming, half the town's fish-folk dead in a single breath from the old man's wave of thunder, my crew carving room for themselves in the crowd. I said something to Indigo about his stealth being a skill issue. He said something about his trident throw. I showed him mine — told him I'd picked it up and thought of him, which is truer than I usually let things be — and then I stepped. Not ran. Stepped, the fey way, one foot up here and down thirty feet away. No frills. Just efficiency.

The sword cost me, though. I asked it to be smaller — it does that now, if I feed it — and it obliged, and then the balance was all wrong and my first cut went through Indigo's net instead of the thing inside it. Weeks of his work, parted on my blade. I finished the netted one properly on the next pass, both blades, clean. And then I did something that wasn't clean. I put my dagger into a thing that was already dead, and I felt the water *approve*. Something out in that lake wanted me frenzied, wanted the red in my eyes to stay. I've been owned before. I shook it off. The big duergar we'd been cutting loose looked at me like he was reconsidering his threats. Good.

Then the rays came out of the water in droves, eating everything, fleeing the water they live in — and things that flee their own home know something you don't. The daughter called her god's name one last time and her father killed her for it, and I heard myself ask if he had any other children. He does. One. I don't know what I wanted the answer to be.

It didn't matter that she died. The pulse still went through the shrine like a heartbeat, and the fog opened, and the lake stood up. Two heads. Taller than the towers of any city I've served in. A roar that reached in through my ears and pulled — I slipped, caught myself, held on with everything I keep behind my teeth. The fish-folk who'd bled for it dropped and groveled. The ones who hadn't just stood there and watched their town start to end.

Indigo says water. Jimjar says land. The little cleric says we don't split, and he's right, and I already know which way my feet are pointing. If Indigo says run, I run. I trust him — implicitly, completely, in a way I don't examine too closely because examining it costs. Two roads out of a drowning city, and a god that isn't a god wading in behind us. We move next time the world does.

## Session Analysis

### Narrative Summary

The session opens with Addison recapping the end of [[Session 19 — We Are Split in Two|Session 19]]: the party's farewell to [[Artaxle]] (his departure toward [[Menzoberranzan]] hoping to join [[Bregan D'aerthe]], the drow-operated mercenary network led by [[Jarlaxle]], "One-Eyed Jax," with [[Kit Aluri]] having supplied a contact useful on the [[Mantol-Derith]] route), the party's capture and surrender at [[Sloobludop]], Archpriest [[Ploop]]'s plan to hand the party over as false sacrifices to his daughter's Deep Father cult while springing an ambush at the ritual site, and Kit and [[Indigo]]'s tavern reunion with [[Shuushar]] and [[Jimjar]] — cut short by Jimjar's warning that a kuo-toa contingent was heading to arrest Ploop. *(Continuity note: this recap says [[Sloopidoop]] held off "Sea Hags" during the S18 escape; S18/S19 records say green hags. Preserved as spoken — see Continuity Flags.)*

Play begins at that cliffhanger. A kuo-toa monitor and twelve kuo-toa collect Ploop, his whip, and the "prisoners" from Ploop's quarters and escort them toward the **Altar of the Deep Father** as an uncommon fog rolls in off [[The Darklake|the Darklake]]. Kit and Indigo, hurrying back from the tavern, do not rejoin the group: Indigo pulls water and steam off a canal to thicken the fog around them, and Kit — aided by her cloak — stays hidden (Stealth 23 with advantage) as the two of them dart into an alleyway to watch. [[Jimjar]] slinks off and disappears, working "behind the scenes" for the rest of the session.

At the altar — a stretched manta-ray hide idol with a dead splayed manta pinned to it, two dead octopuses arrayed across the top, a reeking golden chalice-basin collecting blood, and a blood-stained depression with a grate 20 feet from the altar — the party is herded together with a kuo-toa guard on each character. **[[Bloppblippodd]]** ("Blop"), the female kuo-toa Archpriestess of the Deep Father, emerges from a low hovel flanked by her whip **[[Clib]]** *(name uncertain — the DM gave "Clip or Clib" on air)*. A group insight check (Darby highest) reads her instantly: a bloodthirsty sadist with full, perfect confidence in her faith that her divine vision will raise her to rule her people. Bound and gagged against a post is a **duergar prisoner** — [[Binks Stonevein]]'s insight (23) suggests a smuggler-trader who walked into the wrong town after the Deep Father faction rose, now bruised, beaten, and queued for sacrifice.

As the chanting peaks, Blop gestures toward the altar — **and her father [[Ploop]] strikes her with his scepter, springing the planned betrayal.** The kuo-toa loyal to each archpriest surge into each other; the guards herding the party are caught flat (surprised — no move, action, or reactions on their first turn). [[Amanita de'Champignon]] activates Symbiotic Entity as the strike lands, and initiative is rolled.

**Round 1.** Blop shields herself (Shield of Faith) while chanting [[Limu Gugun]]'s name. Binks channels divinity to create an illusory duplicate of himself atop the shrine, making "the most obscene gestures" as a distraction (combined Deception/Performance reported as 39 — garbled numbers, but ruled a success: whips and monitors turn toward the double), then slips behind [[Derendil]]. [[Stool]] holds, then bursts Pacifying Spores at an adjacent Deep Father kuo-toa — a miss, but it draws attention. [[Aeolus]] opens with an Eldritch Blast into **Clib** (22 to hit; 14 damage plus a DM-granted bonus 10 for beating double the target's AC — 24 total), hurling him prone into the altar, then uses his fey-touched Misty Step to reach a rooftop — fails the DC 20 Dexterity save (19) and crashes through the rickety roof to the second floor (5 bludgeoning), granting [[Blarg]] 7 temporary hit points on the way (his Misty Step rider). **Ploop unleashes Destructive Wave** — Addison rules the mass of Deep Father kuo-toa commoners in the 30-foot burst simply die (no damage rolled; they have ~18–20 HP against 5d6+5d6), while the archpriestess, monitors, and whips save. Blarg casts Command ("grovel") on Blop — natural 2, failed — sending the Archpriestess groveling prone in the middle of her own ritual, then wild shapes into a **giant toad** for the first time. Indigo nets two kuo-toa and pins one (K) with a thrown trident (9 damage) that mists back into his hand. The kuo-toa monitors attack: one bites the illusory duplicate (which persists), one scrapes off [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s stone-hard skin (15 vs. AC 16), one climbs to Aeolus's rooftop and misses (4). Darby Misty Steps ("a poof of wind and feathers") to the duergar and heaves at his bindings (Strength 11 — loosened, not freed). Amanita charges Blop, casts Shillelagh on her mithril war pick, and hits through Shield of Faith (24 to hit; 19 total damage), cracking the altar's stone base with the follow-through as Earth Tremor releases — Blop screams in rage. Kit banters with Indigo, blink-steps beside the netted kuo-toa B, burns a superiority die to convert her greatsword into a **shortsword** (her homebrew weapon feature, wording held in Taylor–Addison DMs), hits — but fails the feature's DC 16 Strength save (11) for the unfamiliar balance and slices a hole in Indigo's net instead ("That took me weeks to make"). Roxy, spooked (Darby's Animal Handling 8), stampedes in a random direction and demolishes a building. [[Topsy]] and [[Turvy]] shift into wererat hybrid form and vanish under at-will Nondetection. A frenzied [[Derendil]], unable to distinguish friend from foe, slashes toad-Blarg (6), then a monitor. Clib misses Ploop twice.

**Round 2.** Blop loses her turn groveling. Binks casts **Bless on Darby, Amanita, and Aeolus** through a window. Stool rides the giant toad and readies a Help action ("toad and stool — toadstool," per the DM). Aeolus blasts the rooftop monitor (25 to hit, 11 force) and layers **Armor of Agathys at 2nd level** — which promptly punishes the monitor's two hits (8 piercing taken) with 20 total cold damage, walked through by Matt's rules coaching ("Good warlock security measure"). Ploop crits his own daughter with a scepter blow (~30 lightning). Toad-Blarg, aided by Stool's spore-flurry Help, tongue-grapples and bites a monitor (12 damage, restrained). Indigo finishes kuo-toa K with two rapier strikes (Vex granting advantage on the second). Amanita's Halo of Spores chips a monitor (7 necrotic). Then the lake changes: **[[Ixitxachitl|ixitxachitls]]** — small, evil, manta-ray-like aberrations — boil out of the water **in droves**, attacking everything, dragging chanting kuo-toa under, and visibly trying to flee the water they live in as the fog thickens and a deep rumbling rises from the depths (Kit's Nature/History 15 identifies them from her Darklake time). Darby locks in Rage ("rage of the ax beak" — ethereal feathers and a pulse of wind), rips one strap off the duergar with radiant-heated hands (Strength 10 with advantage), and ungags him: he is **[[Hemeth]]**, screaming third-person threats in an Undercommon-Dwarvish hybrid ("When Hemeth gets his weapons back, you're all going to die") — then **enlarges to Large size** mid-struggle, duergar-fashion, while still failing to break his last strap. Amanita batters Blop again (25 with Bless; 24 damage) — the Archpriestess is now badly wounded. Kit kills the netted kuo-toa B exactly to the hit point (9 + 9 across greatsword and Dual Wielder dagger), then plunges her dagger into the corpse — and feels a **pervasive manic energy from the water** trying to push her into frenzy: she shrugs off the point of madness (Intimidation 23, Wisdom save 20), unnerving kuo-toa and duergar alike. Topsy gnaws through Hemeth's last cord (natural 18) — the enlarged duergar is free; Turvy's attacks on Clib miss, and Clib's claw-scepter grapples Turvy (7 piercing). Derendil, bloodied and frenzied, carves through two more kuo-toa.

**Round 3 — the rising.** Kuo-toa are pulled under en masse; the water foams red. Blop makes one final call of "[[Limu Gugun]]!" **as her father's final blow kills her** — and the ritual **still achieves its terrible success**. A pulse rolls off the shrine. Tentacles burst from the Darklake, then two monstrous baboon-like heads on a single 30-plus-foot torso: red eyes, curved tusks, madness. Only [[Blarg]]'s Religion 26 places it (Kit 15, Darby 18, Amanita 8, Aeolus insufficient): **this is [[Demogorgon]]** — Prince of Demons, the sibilant beast, master of the spiraling depths, his two heads named [[Aameul and Hethradiah]]; "Limu Gugun" was the kuo-toa's name for him all along. Every party member makes the DC 13 Charisma save against his gaze — Kit (7, rerolled 17 on Heroic Inspiration) and Amanita (12, rerolled with Heroic Inspiration) burn resources to pass; **nobody fails**, and Addison shelves the madness table. The Deep Father kuo-toa grovel and chant; the Sea Mother kuo-toa stand in shock; Demogorgon begins wading toward Sloobludop, heedless of everything.

**The hard stop.** With Austin's 11:30 limit reached, Addison compresses to planning. Binks — down to one spell slot — floats desecrating the ritual and a gloriously cheeky Ceremony gambit (turn the lake around Demogorgon to holy water: "That'd be like a DC30... Religion check"); Blarg's plan "was running away." **[[Indigo]] offers escape by water** (Water Walk across the Darklake); **[[Jimjar]] emerges having cleared an escape trail by land** ("This is bad, you guys. We cannot take this thing."). Kit will follow Indigo — "she trusts him implicitly"; Darby: "Not my circus, not my Demogorgon"; Binks: "I ain't splitting the party." Amanita is allowed to recast Rapport Spores as one of the session's last acts. Combat is "effectively ended" unresolved; the land-vs-water choice is deferred to Session 21 (August 2). Addison confirms contact with the demon prince clears the **Level 5** threshold — level-ups to be narrated at S21's open.

### Themes & Emotional Beats

- **Filicide and faith:** The session's emotional core is a father killing his own daughter to stop her god — and it not being enough. Kit's quiet "Does he have any other kids?" (answer: one son, [[Glug]], asserted present but never shown on the map) lands the cost. Blop dies mid-prayer; the ritual succeeds because of everything that came before the ambush, not despite it.
- **The plan that worked and failed at once:** Ploop's ambush went essentially perfectly — surprise achieved, the enemy archpriestess neutralized nearly the whole fight (prone, groveling, battered), her faction's commoners massacred. And it changed nothing. The Deep Father was already fed.
- **Chaos leaks upward:** The fog, the manic energy pushing Kit toward frenzy over a corpse, water-dwelling ixitxachitls desperately fleeing their own element, kuo-toa dragged under mid-chant — the battlefield itself signals Demogorgon's approach for a full round before he surfaces. Madness as weather.
- **Trust as compass:** Kit follows Indigo "implicitly" — the campaign's most guarded character naming total trust out loud. Meanwhile Binks's "I ain't splitting the party" holds the found family together against a literal two-headed god of schism — a direct echo (and inversion) of S19's title, *We Are Split in Two*.
- **Comedy under the guillotine:** Binks's obscene stunt double, "toad and stool — toadstool," Roxy demolishing real estate, the holy-water-lake gambit, "Not my circus, not my Demogorgon" — the table's humor runs at full speed precisely while the fiction turns apocalyptic.
- **Scale:** A party that just clawed to the level 5 threshold watches a 30-foot demon lord rise. For the first time in the campaign, the correct answer is unambiguous: run.

## Character POV Overview

### Kit Aluri — POV Overview

*(Storytelling exception. In-character, in-world. See [[Kit Aluri Journal]] for the running memoir.)*

I am beginning to think the water down here hates me as much as everything else does.

We came out of the mushrooms into open air — open for the Underdark, anyway — and a still black pool with steam coming off it. I should have read that for what it was. Still water that quiet is water with something underneath it. But everyone was tired and clean-starved, so the elf with the fishing pole went fishing, and the others went swimming, and I climbed up on a mushroom cap to keep my feet dry and my eyes high, and for one whole half-hour I let myself believe nothing wanted to kill us.

Then the line went tight, and the pool stood up.

It came out of the deep end like a nightmare wearing armor — claws the size of my torso, a mouth full of poison whips. It went for the soft ones first, the way they always do. We were on it before it could drag anyone under. I marked it, opened it up, and for once the dice were on the right side of the table. Roxy came down on its belly like a falling wall. The little eye in the water skin crawled into its gills and started drowning it from the inside. And then — gods — the thing that thinks it's a prince leapt the whole pool and ate it. Just ate it. Tore it to pieces and kept tearing. We've seen a lot of ugly things. I still stepped back.

That's when the fish-priest walked out of the dark.

I'll say this plainly, the way I'd say it if anyone asked: I'm tired of standing next to the one who wants to kill us all. Last time I swore I wouldn't take it again. So when the beast set its eyes on the stranger and the red came up behind them, I moved in to put it down before it could start. It turned on me instead. Two strikes, and the second one should have finished me. I felt the floor of myself drop away — and then the old blood pulled me back up by the collar, the way it always does, one breath left, one heartbeat, *not today.* I came up out of the water with my own blood in my mouth and I kept stabbing. I didn't want to. I wanted it to be anyone else's turn to be the one nobody could save. But it was mine, so I did the work.

The warlock put it to sleep before I had to make it permanent. I looked at the kid and thanked him, and I meant it. Then I let my head fall on the dwarf's shoulder while he stitched me, because I was done pretending I wasn't hurt.

The priest. The fish with the ridiculous name. I laughed — let me have that, I've nearly died fourteen times and a man telling me his name with a straight face is the funniest thing that's happened in weeks. He didn't flinch when we laughed. Big calm eyes, a staff, a way of moving water around like it owed him. He talked like a scholar and asked nothing he couldn't pay for. He gave us a bead that opens his village, took back his dead from the monster's hoard, and led us down through a hole in the water to pick over the bones. A pristine lock that won't open for anyone, a mending rope, a scroll, gemstones, a fish-bone spear. Useful things from a useful death.

He says the chaos that's been making everyone mad is getting *worse.* He says the thing wearing the elf's face will keep rotting back into the beast, and there's no cure but the peace of death. He has never heard of the kingdom the prince keeps naming. I didn't say what I was thinking, which is that I've known that for a while.

We're a day out from a real city now. Walls, people who can pull curses out of a man, maybe a quiet bed. I'll believe it when I'm inside it. Until then I'm keeping my back to a wall and my eye on the prince, and I'm not going swimming again.

## Session Analysis

### Narrative Summary

After a recap of the prior camp (Roxy's mushroom-cavern "evolution," the horn ritual, [[Derendil]]'s bloodied nocturnal disappearance, [[Binks Stonevein]]'s [[Zone of Truth]] confrontation and his half-converted shrine to [[Garl Glittergold]] desecrating [[Lolth]]'s, plus a [[Carrion Crawler]] sighting on watch), the party emerges from canal-like tunnels into a high-ceilinged clearing lit by bioluminescence. A still underground **pool** sits in the open, shallow at the rim and dropping off sharply in the middle. The DM reconciles bookkeeping: **only Binks still carries a point of madness** (Kit/Aeolus/Blarg cleared in "session 10," Amanita in "session 5," Darby in "session 10"); **Binks and Derendil each take 1 level of exhaustion** — Binks from sleepless nights, Derendil from his episodes. Addison restates the milestone rule: **leveling to 5 is unlocked the moment the party reaches [[Sloobludop]]**, at each player's chosen narrative moment.

[[Aeolus]] decides to **go fishing** with an actual fishing pole, baiting the hook with [[Glabbagool]] (the awakened gelatinous cube, still carried in a waterskin). After scouting the depths through Glabbagool's eyes (bioluminescent plankton, deep fish, scattered bones, >15 ft drop), Aeolus lands **two large fish** (Animal Handling 22). [[Blarg]] and others bathe; [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s line catches on something heavy — and a **[[Chuul]]** (a lobster-scorpion aberration, an ambush predator) erupts from the water and attacks.

**Combat 1 — The Chuul Ambush.** The chuul surprises the party but whiffs its opening strike (the DM rolls a Nat 1 then a Nat 3; Amanita makes a Constitution save and dodges the paralytic tentacles). In initiative, the party dismantles it fast: [[Binks Stonevein]]'s **Guiding Bolt** (level 2) for 20; [[Blarg]] **Bless**es Kit/Aeolus/Amanita; [[Kit Aluri]] opens with **Hunter's Mark** + short sword (15) and a failed Trip Attack; Aeolus **Eldritch Blast** for 12 force. The chuul grapples Kit (9 damage) and tries to drag her into the depths to drown her — but Amanita, using **War Caster**, snaps off an opportunity **Thorn Whip**, hauling the chuul bodily out of the pool ("I've landed a fish"). [[Darby Stonefeather]] arrives mid-fight; **Roxy** gores it for 17, Glabbagool engulfs its gills (acid + suffocation), [[Stool]] chips in a point of poison, and as the creature drops to 6 HP, **Derendil emerges from his exhausted stupor, leaps the entire pool, and devours the chuul** in a gruesome frenzy.

**First contact — Sloopidoop.** A second, thinner kuo-toa walks out of an eastern passage: a **priest** in net-robes and a beaded necklace, immediately recognizable as the same species as [[Shuushar the Awakened]]. He warns that **Derendil is no elf** but a **[[Chuul|quaggoth]]** afflicted by chaotic magic that has given it a false memory, and that "there is no cure besides the **peace of death**." This panics the party.

**Combat 2 — Derendil's Frenzy.** As Derendil fixes on the priest, the party tries to restrain him; he turns on Kit, dropping her to 0 — she survives via **Relentless / Orcish Endurance at 1 HP.** Blarg's held **Hold Person** fails (Derendil is a monstrosity, not a humanoid). Kit, refusing to fall, unloads sneak attack + Action Surge for **31 damage in a round**, and **Aeolus's Sleep spell** (Derendil rolls a Nat 4 save) finally drops the quaggoth onto the chuul's corpse. [[Topsy]] and [[Turvy]] — who'd gone to explore the left-hand cave and missed the whole fight — return and **bind Derendil** for safety. Blarg Cure Wounds Kit for 15; Binks tops her up for 9.

**The priest's offer.** The priest introduces himself — **Sloopidoop**, a mononym he delivers without embarrassment despite the party's open laughter — a **scholar-priest of the [[Society of Brilliance]]**, colleague of the **mind flayer [[Wazalax]]** *(name unresolved — also heard "Grazilax"/"Rasa Lax")* and acquainted with Shuushar. He worships not [[Lolth]] (he reacts to the suggestion with genuine anger) but, he says, "knowledge" and **the Sea Mother** *(deity-name delivered as deliberate untranslatable kuo-toa gurgle — [Unknown/Ambiguous]; see Archivist Notes)*. The chuul had killed his acolyte and taken his belongings; he grants the party **magical gills** and leads them down a whirlpool into the creature's **submerged lair** to recover the body and loot. Roxy, too buoyant to dive, is finally **stored in the horn** on a second ritual attempt (Aeolus Arcana 22 → Darby succeeds), and the party descends.

**The lair & the bargain.** In the lair they recover **5 gems** (~50 gp each), a pristine **deadbolt lock** (later identified as a **Lock of Trickery**), a magically enhanced **rope** (**Rope of Mending**), a **spell scroll** (**Spell Scroll of Sand**, 1st level), plus a non-magical **kuo-toa trident** (bone + [[Zurkhwood]]) and **kuo-toa scale armor** (teeth/shell) and a **fishing net**. Sloopidoop preserves and shrinks his acolyte's corpse with water-magic. Pressed on Derendil's story, he says he knows the **High Forest** kingdoms and has **never heard of "Nel Rinden Vain"** (Derendil's claimed kingdom) nor of the wizard **"Pterostor"** *(also heard "terroristor")* — confirming, to him, that the quaggoth is simply mad. He confirms the **chaotic energies are worsening** and that studying their origin is the Society of Brilliance's primary goal (he suspects demonic origin; the party hints, telepathically and cautiously, at a **fey** dimension — tied to the archfey patron **[[Silofirst]]** *(spelling unresolved; also "Silo Fear")* — and an imbalance toward death/undeath). He proposes a **mutual-aid bargain**: escort him a short day's walk to his home village of **[[Sloobludop]]**, help the village against the chaos, and he will trade knowledge and aid (including identifying their loot). The session ends as the party climbs out of the lair to make the final leg to Sloobludop. Next session is scheduled for **June 21 (Juneteenth weekend)**, "a couple of weeks" out.

### Themes & Emotional Beats

- **The food chain / survival in the dark:** Sloopidoop's proverb — *"the big fish eat a little fish"* — is the session's spine. The party goes fishing and becomes the hunted; the chuul ambushes and is itself devoured; even the priest "was merely waiting… for a group like you." Predation cuts every direction down here.
- **Kit's recurring burden:** For the second straight session Kit ends up at melee range with the party's most dangerous member and nearly dies for it. "I'm just always going to be standing right next to the person in our party who wants to kill us." Her Orcish Endurance and a non-lethal Sleep are the only reasons Derendil lives and so does she.
- **Identity & delusion:** Sloopidoop's blunt "that is no elf" reframes [[Derendil]] entirely — a quaggoth gaslit by chaos into believing itself royalty. The kingdom "Nel Rinden Vain" cannot be verified; the curse-wizard "Pterostor" is unknown. The party's hope of a curable noble curdles toward "no cure but death."
- **First true ally of the Society of Brilliance:** After the pacifist monk Shuushar, the party meets a *brilliant, well-spoken, transactional* kuo-toa. The relationship is built on a quid pro quo, not charity — and the party (via Amanita) is consciously learning to bargain rather than give everything away.
- **Comedy as relief:** "Sloopidoop," the Sea Mother gurgle, "every pond is a random pond," and Darby's open crush on the priest puncture two near-death fights. The table mocks the name even as the NPC stays serenely unbothered.
- **The worsening chaos (faerzress):** The "Ferris whis" / chaotic radiation "making everyone a bit nuts" is now explicitly *intensifying* — the Society's primary study — tying the madness mechanic to a campaign-level mystery the party is feeding fey/death-imbalance theories into.

## POV Overview

### The Things We Carried in the Dark: A Soldier's Memoir

**Day — third tenday out of [[Velkynvelve]].**

The fog came in ahead of the thing. That's the part I keep coming back to. Not the twin heads, not the tentacles — the fog. It rolled up the canal like it had been sent ahead to soften us.

I counted heads. That's what I do. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] and [[Blarg]] and [[Aeolus]] stacked on top of each other by the shrine. [[Turvy]] with them. [[Topsy]] and [[Darby Stonefeather]] with me. [[Binks Stonevein]] somewhere in the middle, and then two of him, which took a second to sort. [[Derendil]] the farthest out, and [[Roxy]] beside him, and both of them ringed by fish.

My back was to the water. I didn't like that. I still don't.

I told them to move toward the canal. I told [[Aeolus]] to get ready for the dark. I didn't shout it — we've got the spore-speech now, [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s gift, and it's strange to feel a plan land in six heads at once without opening your mouth. I've spent my whole life controlling what people know. Now they get it whether I hand it over or not. I haven't decided how I feel about that.

[[Binks Stonevein]] prayed. Out loud, in front of everybody, before he did anything else — asked for wit, asked for cleverness, and then his voice came out of three mouths at once, big as a bell, telling a whole dying town which way to run. Some of them ran. That's more than I'd have managed.

[[Derendil]] didn't.

[[Amanita de'Champignon]] got close enough to look at him properly and came back with the thing I think we'd all been walking around for weeks. There was never a prince. There was a quaggoth with somebody else's memories stuffed into him, and when the demon came up out of the lake it shook them loose. He stood in the middle of the dead and swung at anything that moved. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] said goodbye to him. Said *be at peace in the mycelial network*, said *it was awesome knowing you*, and he turned and looked at her for about a heartbeat with something almost like a person behind his eyes, and then he was gone.

I said he'd survive. I said he'd turn up in a few years and either save all of us or kill all of us. I said it fast, the way you say a thing so you don't have to sit in the other thing. We left him raging on a dock in a town that was about to stop existing.

That's two now — him and the town. I'm not counting [[Jimjar]] and the dwarf, because they chose the tunnels and they'll be fine. Probably. [[Jimjar]] said *I guess I'll see you when I see you* and bobbed off into the fog like a man who has never once been where a disaster was.

[[Indigo]] told us to run *into* the wave.

Every instinct I have said no. You don't charge water. But he was standing in a spinning ring of it he'd pulled up out of the canal with his hands, and he was completely certain, and I have fought beside that certainty before. So I told everybody to come, and we came, and the water held us up like a floor, and the wave went under us instead of over us.

Mostly. It picked up [[Amanita de'Champignon]] and [[Turvy]] and drove them under. It threw [[Binks Stonevein]] into a dock hard enough that I heard it from a hundred feet out. Then the second head turned — through the fog, through [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s spore-cloak, straight at us — and reached, and I have never in my life felt something that big decide to notice me.

We got clear. And then I looked back, because I always look back, and I was counting again, and [[Binks Stonevein]] wasn't with us.

He was at the edge of the mist saying a prayer for a town full of fish-people who'd been kind to him. That's the whole of it. Nobody made him. There was no advantage in it. He just stopped and did it.

So I did something I don't do.

I closed my eyes and I hummed my parents' song and I reached out. Not to anyone in particular — I don't have a god and I don't want one — but I know he's attached to somebody, he's said so a hundred times and I've listened to maybe four of them. So I reached for whoever had their eyes on [[Binks Stonevein]] right then, and I held one picture in my head: a shield in front of him.

Something answered. I went grey around the edges and there was a flash I felt more than saw, and when I came back the others were staring at a shield of light standing between [[Binks Stonevein]] and the demon, humming in a low voice that they said sounded like me.

And then [[Binks Stonevein]] — because he is who he is — made the thing raise its other hand and put up one finger at a demon lord.

The shrine went gold. Whatever else sinks into that lake, that stays.

I don't know what I did. I'm not sure I want it explained. I've been on the outside of every miracle this party's been handed — everybody else got a vision, a gift, a voice in the dark, and I got a nightmare and a doll. This time something reached back, and it wasn't even for me. That feels about right.

We ran an hour up the coast on water that held. Found a cove [[Indigo]] knew, dry and sheltered, one way in. I counted the exits out of habit and there was only the one, and I decided that was fine.

He introduced himself properly then. *Indigo Montoya de Charmed.* Mercenary, from the ocean, hunting a six-fingered not-quite-man who killed his father. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] asked who he was to me and I didn't have words for it, so I let the spores carry the picture instead — him and me in the line together, and the same reach I'd just made for [[Binks Stonevein]], made for him once before. She saw it. She understood it faster than I could have said it.

[[Darby Stonefeather]] doesn't like him. Won't say so out loud, which I respect, and hid it well enough that he bowed and complimented her mount instead of calling her on it. I told her she doesn't want that beef. I meant it kindly. I don't think it landed kindly.

Then it was rope and salvage and mushrooms. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] and [[Darby Stonefeather]] coaxed [[Roxy]] into a boat — an actual boat, hull and all, shields lining the sides like a raiding ship. [[Binks Stonevein]] and [[Blarg]] and [[Indigo]] and I went out for a second hull and patched what we found. [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s mace runs hotter now. She'll figure out what it wants eventually.

We're going to [[Mantol-Derith]]. Ten to fifteen days on the water. [[Binks Stonevein]] wants to warn people, and he's right — a thing like that in the lake is everyone's problem now, not ours. I tried to remember the politics of the place and came up with nothing useful. I was too hollowed out to hold it. [[Binks Stonevein]] remembered everything, because of course he did.

Last thing, before the light: I asked to hold [[Indigo]]'s trident. Then [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s weapon. Then [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s mace. My sword learns what I hold. Nobody asked me why I wanted three at once. I think that was generous of them.

The bone teeth pulled us in before we were ready for them. Current, then a whirlpool, then rock. [[Binks Stonevein]] went over the side and [[Indigo]] came up out of the water and put him back. We got around the corner.

And there was a hag on a rock, older than the one we saw before, and she looked at me like she'd been waiting.

*It is you. It was fates that brought us back together.*

[[Indigo]] knew her name.

I'm holding three weapons that don't belong to me and I have no idea which one my hands will want in the morning.

## Session Analysis

### Narrative Summary

The session opens with no recap gap: the party is standing in the wreckage of the [[Sloobludop]] canal district exactly where S20 left them, with [[Demogorgon]] risen from [[The Darklake]], rolling fog coming in, dead kuo-toa and ixitxachitls everywhere, [[Indigo]] heading for a gap along the canal and [[Jimjar]] trying to lead the party out the north gate. Addison opens by calling for perception checks and, in the same breath, invites every player to narrate their level-5 surge — the whole party crosses to level 5 in-fiction, in the middle of a demon lord's arrival.

The level-up round doubles as a character round. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] rolls a 26 and pulses out through every spore, fungus and algae mat in the water, getting a full spherical read of the drop-off, the thermal vents, the growing whirlpools, and — critically — the tidal wave already building. They describe rooting down and "becoming more themselves and more fungal," and flex a fresh crop of mushrooms on their arms. [[Darby Stonefeather]] rolls a 2, still exhausted, and narrates a Barbarian coming into her own who nonetheless left [[Roxy]] behind against her better judgment and is "really stressed out" about it. [[Blarg]] rolls a 26 religion, is openly frightened of [[Demogorgon]], and reaches for the moon and [[Selûne]] for guidance; Addison rounds his darkvision from 120 up to 200 feet and describes the world in black-and-white moonlight. [[Kit Aluri]] rolls 15 and spends her turn purely on triage — counting heads, identifying that only [[Derendil]] and [[Roxy]] are unreachable, and confirming the party still has [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s telepathic spores. [[Binks Stonevein]] takes a fourth Cleric level and the Skill Expert feat (expertise in Insight, proficiency in Deception), then prays out loud — "Please, give me wit. Give me cleverness, [[Garl Glittergold|Garl]]" — and rolls a 31 on a combined Religion+Insight check; Addison triples his stunt doubles, makes them semi-corporeal, golden, and able to speak through him. [[Aeolus]], hoarse and struggling, takes a Warlock level, picks **Repelling Blast** and **One with Shadows**, and Addison grants the second one free on the spot with an evocative scene: clean air, grapes and wine, home and his patron, and then smoke and shadow as he turns invisible in front of a kuo-toa monitor that should be able to see through it.

Addison then puts the party back into the initiative order carried over from S20 (the Black Pudding token is dead and removed). [[Binks Stonevein]] goes first and uses Thaumaturgy through all his duplicates to boom an evacuation order in Undercommon across the whole town — "Sloobludop is lost. The demon is coming to destroy this place… Your lives are more important. We have to leave" — then follows it with a 26 Persuasion. The town starts running. [[Ploop]] reinforces it in Undercommon, refuses to flee himself, and lingers over his fallen daughter while his son tries to pull him along.

The session's first real loss lands mid-combat. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] makes an Insight check on [[Derendil]] with advantage and Addison rules definitively: there was never a prince. [[Derendil]] "is and always was a quaggoth" — a chaotic brain-damage situation that produced false memories, "like a reverse amnesia" — and [[Demogorgon]]'s arrival shook it all loose. He is in slaughter mode, will not follow, and Addison states flatly, "you'd be lucky to get him to follow you again ever." [[Amanita de'Champignon]] rolls again for a farewell and gets a moment of clarity out of him: a sad, haunted look, a howl, and then he lopes off and gives up the fight. [[Kit Aluri]] deflects with a prediction that he'll survive and turn up in a few years to either save or kill them all.

[[Jimjar]] and [[Hemeth]] split off. [[Binks Stonevein]] fails the persuasion to bring them along, and both take the overland tunnels northeast while the party commits to water. [[Indigo]] releases the Water Walk he has been holding, catching ten of them, and the flight north begins.

The escape itself is a running set of group checks. [[Amanita de'Champignon]] holds Pass Without Trace until everyone is inside the radius; the group stealth lands between 14 and 28. It doesn't matter. The tidal wave hits: everyone rolls Dexterity against DC 10, [[Amanita de'Champignon]] and [[Binks Stonevein]] fail, [[Amanita de'Champignon]] catches [[Roxy]] on a reroll and takes 1 point, [[Binks Stonevein]] is flung into a dock for 10 bludgeoning. Then, through the fog and through the magic, one of [[Demogorgon]]'s heads turns and looks straight at them, and a tentacle reaches out "inordinately long." Strength saves at DC 10 send [[Amanita de'Champignon]] and [[Turvy]] 30 feet and ten feet under, for 7 bludgeoning each. [[Stool]] survives by latching onto [[Roxy]]'s horns on a natural 20.

After the break Addison escalates with Wisdom saves against madness (DC 12), [[Binks Stonevein]] at disadvantage. Everyone passes, several only barely — [[Kit Aluri]] rolls a 10 and burns Indomitable/Tactical Mind for a d10 of 9 to reach 19; [[Blarg]] rolls 10 and spends heroic inspiration to hit 24. Addison then hands [[Darby Stonefeather]] a scene: she spends her own heroic inspiration on a Religion+Animal Handling check to calm [[Roxy]], describes her family's multigenerational axe beak farming and extends a celestial feather. The result gives the whole party advantage for an hour on checks made against or to avoid fiendish presences, permanently raises her Religion checks by +2, warms her mace, and lights up the fungal filaments woven through [[Roxy]]'s body.

The session's centrepiece is next. [[Binks Stonevein]] hangs back at the mist line to watch [[Sloobludop]] die — "the second town he held in his heart that has been destroyed" — and says a prayer for the kuo-toa. He rolls an 8. Addison had built the moment so the party could help him, and [[Kit Aluri]] passes the high-DC perception (19) needed to notice. Asked what [[Kit Aluri]] believes, Taylor gives one of the session's best character statements: Kit isn't an atheist, she's seen too much to want to be involved, but she reflexively reaches for prayer in trouble the way a lapsed believer does. She won't name a god. She hums her parents' song and reaches for "whoever would have its eyes on [[Binks Stonevein]] at that moment," holding a single image — a shield protecting him. Addison grants advantage (the roll is against a fiend) and then imports a **Daggerheart**-style Hope/Fear mechanic: two d12s, declare which is which before reading them, take the higher. [[Kit Aluri]] rolls 12 and 10 and assigns Hope the 12, reasoning that she's putting her trust somewhere she normally wouldn't. The final religion check clears the DC 30 by a wide margin. [[Kit Aluri]] blacks out at the edges; everyone else sees a blinding light burst from [[Binks Stonevein]], and his remaining duplicate solidifies into a shrine to [[Garl Glittergold]] wearing [[Binks Stonevein]]'s own face, posture and robes, holding a shield out toward [[Demogorgon]] — humming in a low sing-song voice that reminds [[Binks Stonevein]] of [[Kit Aluri]]. [[Binks Stonevein]] immediately adds a raised middle finger to the statue's other hand. Addison notes there may be consequences later, but a wave of protection rolls out from the city, [[Demogorgon]] is visibly paused, and the shrine — now golden — will emanate [[Garl Glittergold]]'s power wherever it ends up, buried or drowned.

The back third is downtime and planning. The party runs an hour up the coast, past shipwrecks, crags and stalagmites, and shelters in a small dry cove [[Indigo]] knows. [[Indigo]] finally introduces himself — "My name is Indigo Montoya de Charmed" — and delivers a full *Princess Bride* homage, hunting a six-fingered "not really a man" who killed his father, and asking whether anyone present has six fingers on their left hand. He compliments [[Roxy]], earns [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s quiet dislike (hidden behind a natural 20 Deception), calls [[Demogorgon]] a beluga and then apologises to belugas, and lectures [[Blarg]] on goblinoid tribes after [[Blarg]] declares himself "a goblin in spirit." [[Amanita de'Champignon]] investigates the flotsam, recovers zurkhwood, food and ship parts, and takes a long rest with [[Indigo]] on first watch.

Addison then retcons in the [[Sloobludop]] treasure the party took before leaving, and explicitly puts it on the record: 500 cp, 2,000 sp, 150 gp, 27 pp, a strand of pearls worth ~1,000 gp, two potions of healing, a potion of water breathing, and a spell scroll of *light* — the archpriest's near-suicidal peace offering. He also states plainly that [[Shuushar]]'s fate is unknown.

The destination debate is the session's other major beat. Addison lays out the map: [[Menzoberranzan]] ~26 days, [[Blingdenstone]] ~20, [[Gracklstugh]] ~20 southerly, [[Neverlight Grove]] farther than [[Blingdenstone]] but nearer than [[Gracklstugh]], and [[Mantol-Derith]] 10–15 days across the water. [[Binks Stonevein]] rolls a dirty 20 on History and gets a full briefing on [[Mantol-Derith]]: a centuries-old neutral outpost carved into a pillar running from ceiling to waterline with waterfalls, ~140 people, lit by continual flame and reflecting crystal, governed by an informal council of four chief negotiators — drow, duergar, human and [[svirfneblin]] — with surface trade dominated by the [[Zhentarim]] and the Black Network beneath them. [[Kit Aluri]] rolls a 7 and gets nothing; Addison rules that the politics of the place were never her cup of tea. The party settles on [[Mantol-Derith]], primarily to warn people: "a huge ass Demogorgon is out in the Darklake. We need to warn people."

[[Amanita de'Champignon]] and [[Darby Stonefeather]] then convert [[Roxy]] into a vessel — a dirty 20 on Animal Handling with advantage produces a wide, Viking-ish longboat with the party's shields forming the gunwales, 50 temporary hull hit points (25 per side) and AC 14. [[Binks Stonevein]] (with [[Blarg]]'s help and guidance) and [[Kit Aluri]] (with [[Indigo]]'s, via a Shape Water-granted swim speed and air pocket) scavenge a second hull: a four-person rowboat, patched over an hour, 25 HP and AC 12. Addison openly apologises twice for skipping [[Aeolus]], who is too sick to speak much, and rules him onto the main boat.

The party sets out and immediately rolls a terrain encounter — a whirlpool and the bone teeth. A group Athletics check (DC 15+, [[Darby Stonefeather]] raging and gripping [[Roxy]], [[Binks Stonevein]] casting a second-level Bless, [[Aeolus]] invoking [[Echo]] through a second Hope/Fear roll for +10) carries them around. A group Dexterity check at DC 13 mostly passes, but [[Binks Stonevein]] rolls a natural 1 — "Don't worry, I'm kind of an anchor" — and goes over the side. Damage lands on both hulls. [[Indigo]] surfaces, hauls him back aboard on an advantage Athletics check, and a final DC 12 group Dexterity check clears the stone teeth two-thirds passed, with [[Kit Aluri]] pulling everyone together.

The session ends on a hard cut. Rounding into calmer water, the party sees a stalactite out of the water and, on it, unmistakably and with no illusion, a green hag — older than the one they saw before. She turns, registers surprise, then smiles, and says: *"It is you. It was fates that brought us back together."* [[Kit Aluri]] pulls out a shotgun. [[Indigo]] says, *"Nanny Plunk, we do not have time for your games."* End of session.

Table flavour ran high throughout: Addison's own on-air "Pass gas without trace" (immediately confirmed by Austin, "That's what I heard"); the long break-time tangent where Taylor and Matt dissect Path of the Totem Warrior and Matt reads Aspect of the Elk and Eagle aloud in disbelief; [[Blarg]] insisting he is "a goblin in spirit" and could disguise himself as one "maybe not right now"; [[Darby Stonefeather]] "grasping Roxy respectfully and consensually and then locking into a rage"; and the closing round-table on which PC is actually the most reckless, which lands on [[Binks Stonevein]] ("challenge two gods and desecrate their shrines. That's nothing") over [[Kit Aluri]]'s protest.

### Session Setting

The session begins in the open air of [[Sloobludop]]'s canal district, in fog, standing among the dead from the previous session's altar fight, with [[Demogorgon]] risen from [[The Darklake]] and a tidal wave seconds away. Addison places the party's elapsed time at the end of the session: "your third or tenday since leaving [[Velkynvelve]]," roughly twenty days, and "probably close to your first month" since the campaign began. In-game elapsed time is therefore approximately **Day 21–30 since the escape from Velkynvelve** — Addison's own math is partly garbled and the note flags it rather than fixing a number. Addison also observes out of character that the party has only levelled twice in a real-world year and that level 6 will come "in the next two to three months at least."

### Scene / Timeline Breakdown

### Themes & Emotional Beats

- **Ascension mid-catastrophe.** The party's level-5 milestone is deliberately narrated *inside* the arrival of a demon lord. Everyone gets a surge of power in the same breath they get the worst thing they've ever seen. Addison stacks free gifts on top — [[Blarg]]'s 200 ft. moonlight vision, [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s passive Perception 20, [[Binks Stonevein]]'s tripled semi-corporeal doubles, [[Aeolus]]'s free invocation, and a party-wide exhaustion clear — so power and terror arrive together.
- **Running toward the thing that will kill you.** [[Indigo]]'s "We need to run into this wave. Not away from it" is the session's tactical and thematic spine. The party survives by inverting the flight instinct.
- **Identity as something that can be taken.** [[Derendil]]'s reveal is not a betrayal or a transformation — it is the erasure of a person who was never there. "A chaotic brain damage kind of situation… It's like a reverse amnesia." [[Amanita de'Champignon]] responds not with grief but with a blessing into the mycelial network. [[Kit Aluri]] responds by refusing to close the file.
- **Faith as reflex, not belief.** [[Binks Stonevein]] prays out loud and gets answered twice. [[Blarg]] reaches for [[Selûne]] and the moon. [[Darby Stonefeather]] finds hers in a family trade and a bird. [[Kit Aluri]], who wants nothing to do with gods, reaches anyway — blind, wordless, humming her parents' song, and for someone else. The session's most powerful magic comes from the party member least invested in it.
- **Protection as the shape of Kit's love.** [[Kit Aluri]] does not pray for victory, escape, or herself. She holds one image: a shield in front of [[Binks Stonevein]]. The same reach, she reveals through the spores, that she once made for [[Indigo]].
- **Irreverence as survival.** The golden shrine holds a shield in one hand and a middle finger in the other. That is the party in one image: genuine devotion and complete refusal to be solemn about it.
- **Grief for places, not just people.** [[Binks Stonevein]] frames [[Sloobludop]] as "the second town he held in his heart that has been destroyed," and turns it into resolve and revenge rather than despair. [[Kit Aluri]]'s counterpoint — "maybe we're the problem" from [[Binks Stonevein]], "I'm a perfect baby angel" from Taylor — is the table refusing to hold the weight for too long.
- **Found family, tallied.** The party literally counts itself twice this session ("What's our current party number? We're at an 11" — "we lost Derendil, but we gained Indigo"). The closing conversation about who is most reckless is a group affirmation dressed as an argument.

### Encounter Summary

The escape from [[Sloobludop]] is a clean tactical success with real costs. Nobody drops, nobody takes madness, and every party member reaches open water — but the town is destroyed, [[Derendil]] is permanently lost, and [[Jimjar]] and [[Hemeth]] are separated from the party with no reunion plan. The decisive factors were [[Amanita de'Champignon]]'s 26 Perception (early warning of the tidal wave), [[Binks Stonevein]]'s tripled speaking duplicates (mass evacuation without a second check), and [[Indigo]]'s held Water Walk, which converted the lake from a wall into a road. [[Indigo]]'s counterintuitive instruction to run *into* the wave is what actually saved the party from being crushed against the shore.

The madness gauntlet was the closest call. Addison set DC 12 and several party members cleared it only by spending resources — [[Kit Aluri]]'s Indomitable/Tactical Mind die, [[Blarg]]'s heroic inspiration — meaning a slightly worse round would have started distributing long-term madness effects across the party. Addison confirmed afterwards: "because of those boosts, you barely all passed."

The [[Garl Glittergold]] shrine is the session's largest strategic gain and its largest open question. It bought a visible pause from a demon lord, granted the party a permanent monument that emanates divine power regardless of where it ends up, and — because [[Binks Stonevein]] added the raised finger — carries an explicitly flagged future consequence.

- **[[Aeolus]] rolled almost nothing.** Illness and connection trouble meant Addison twice apologised for skipping him. His only meaningful contributions were a Strength check (16), a d12 encounter roll and the Hope/Fear invocation of [[Echo]].

### Patterns, Progress & Future Implications

- **Addison's escalation pattern is now explicit and consistent: reward, then tax.** Every gift this session came with a bill attached — the level-5 narration round is immediately followed by a tidal wave; the free invocation for [[Aeolus]] is followed by [[Demogorgon]]'s gaze; the party-wide exhaustion clear is followed by madness saves; the successful shrine miracle is followed by "there may be consequences for that later."

## How To Rebuild This

Per session, in order. Roughly one pass.

| Field | Source | Trust |
|---|---|---|
| **HP** | DDB sheet max, minus every damage event in the Full Roll Log, plus healing | ✓ **only valid from a known baseline.** A long rest resets it; without one, carry the previous card's numbers forward |
| **AC, class, level, inventory** | `03-Characters/PCs/Party Character Sheets/*(DDB).md` | ✓ authoritative, auto-synced |
| **Zone** | Party Structure & Subgroups table + the final scene | ~ **zones only.** Position lives on the VTT, not in the audio |
| **Condition** | [[Exhaustion & Madness Tracker]] + the session's NPC table | ✓ already maintained, link rather than copy |
| **Concentration** | inferred from spell names in the roll log | ~ **never stated at the table.** The word appears twice in a 188-minute session. Safe for unambiguous spells, never confirmable at session end |
| **Spent resources** | declared casts in the roll log | ✓ for spells, ⚠️ for defensive reactions. Addison tells the table *"you don't need to tell me your stuff, just minimize it"* — so HP and passive resources are private by convention |
| **Disposition** | Reputation & Relationships | ✓ the single best-supported field. People say what they feel out loud |
| **Enemy damage** | roll log, tallied per body | ✓ **damage dealt only.** Never assert enemy HP |

> [!tip] 📌 Calibrate the archive clock, then mine it for unstated rolls
> The D&D Beyond archive stores wall-clock ET; the transcript stores elapsed audio time. **Anchor on the initiative rolls**, which are unambiguous in both, and you get the offset for the whole session. S22: transcript `00:34` = archive `12:37 ET`, so audio began ~`12:03 ET`.
>
> With the offset, any moment in the transcript maps to a window in the archive. That recovers rolls the table never said out loud, including saves, damage totals, and `1d100` madness results, and it independently confirms things like advantage (`2d20`) and reductions.
>
> ⚠️ It only works for players who roll in D&D Beyond. **[[Aeolus]] and [[Blarg]] roll physical dice and have zero archive rows in any session.** Their numbers are recoverable only from the transcript, and often not at all. Do not read their absence as a sync gap (standing S18 correction).

## NPC Companions (Current Party)

- [[Indigo]] — Triton mercenary (Eldritch Knight), from the surface ocean; [[Kit Aluri]]'s prior contact from [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Mantol-Derith]]. **S21: gave his full name for the first time — "Indigo Montoya de Charmed"** *(surname unverifiable — see his page)* — and revealed he is hunting a six-fingered "not really a man" who killed his father. His held **Water Walk** (once per day, caught ten creatures) and his ruling to run *into* the tidal wave are what got the party out of [[Sloobludop]] alive. Guided them to his cove cache, took first watch, hauled [[Binks Stonevein]] out of the whirlpool, and **knows [[Nanny Plunk]] by name.** Passive Insight 16, Survival +6, swim speed 30. Traveling with the party; **counted as party member 11.** **S22: [[The Mermaid Cove]] is his people's home.** He named [[Nanny Plunk]] aloud, advised against parley, then **went off-map alone into the fog** and extracted the **High Priest of the merfolk** and [[Sloopidoop]] before the witch could convert them. Relayed the ritual's stakes telepathically (the triad, the two-round deadline). Held the containment trap on the surviving charmed merfolk with Sloopidoop at the end. 🛑 **He also used force on a party member** — a shouted Aquan *"Stop"* that magically held [[Darby Stonefeather]], who already dislikes him and said so twice. Addison plays him as Inigo Montoya throughout. (S19–S22)
- [[Topsy]] — Deep gnome, wererat. Resurrected by the Carrion King via mycelial fibers (S11). Leveled up between S12-S13 (Fey-Touched spell pending Matt's choice). **S21: aboard [[Roxanne|Roxy]]'s ship-form; passed every save (Dex 15, Str 16, Wis 14).** 🛑 **S22: setting foot on [[The Mermaid Cove]]'s sand forced her fully into wererat hybrid form**, in pain and effectively under short-term madness. [[Blarg]] steadied her with moonlight (disadvantage removed, form unchanged). **Killed the first merfolk Skirmisher.** Still in hybrid form. Traveling with party.
- [[Turvy]] — Deep gnome, wererat. Leveled up between S12-S13; assigned Silver Barbs (Fey-Touched). **S21: natural 20 on the tidal-wave Dex save, then failed the tentacle Str save — flung 30 ft, 7 bludgeoning, driven 10 ft underwater. Recovered; aboard Roxy's ship-form.** 🛑 **S22: forced into wererat hybrid form on the cove's sand, as [[Topsy]].** Steadied by [[Blarg]] and **bowed to him** for it. **Broke the first Stormcaller's concentration**, unravelling more of the fog. Still in hybrid form. Traveling with party.
- [[Stool]] — Myconid sprout, bonded with Amanita via rapport spores. Stabilized by Amanita (Spare the Dying, S14). Healed by Blarg (Cure Wounds, 9 HP restored → near full). **S21: survived [[Demogorgon]]'s tentacle on a natural 20 Strength save by latching onto [[Roxanne|Roxy]]'s horns; passed the madness save at 20.** ⭐ **S22: LEVELLED UP — increased movement speed**, which Addison used to let him **Misty Step onto [[Nanny Plunk]]'s head in round one** and empty the fungal waterskin over her (she failed the Dex save on a 3). Arguably the play that decided the encounter. Spent round two assisting [[Kit Aluri]]. Traveling with party.
- [[Glabbagool]] — Awakened gelatinous cube; befriended by Amanita (S06). Compressed into Amanita's waterskin (S09). Long rest achieved in Feydark (S10) — should be fully reconstituted. ⭐ **S22: the waterskin is identified.** It is a **dimensional container** built by [[Amanita de'Champignon]] channeling her own energy into it over the extended rest — Addison: "like a genie's lamp… a fungal pokeball, just like [[Roxanne|Roxy]]'s horn." [[Stool]] emptied it over [[Nanny Plunk]]'s head in round one; Glabbagool **manifested in full form** in round two and attempted to **engulf** her (she made the Dex save). Took claw damage from Plunk ×2 and a heavy Wave Bender hit. **Emptied for the last time this session.** Alive, damaged.
- [[Roxanne]] — Rothe/fungal hybrid. Killed [[Ilvara Mizzrym]] with a 27-damage gore/trample attack (S13). Decisive blow of the Battle of Velkynvelve. **S16:** gained a Zurkhwood tent-cabin **shelter form** from her dimensional saddlebags. **S17: now fully stored in the horn** — Darby attuned; summon by calling "Roxanne." Inventory inaccessible while she's inside. **S21: reshaped into a seaworthy longboat — 50 temporary hull HP (25/side, port and starboard tracked separately), AC 14, party shields forming the gunwales. Fungal filaments now visibly glowing after [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s celestial-feather scene. Took 2 piercing at the bone teeth; port hull down 5.** **S22: held ship form the entire session** as the party's shield and escape route; failed her checks early and was **pinned by the current**. Nudged [[Darby Stonefeather]] affectionately (Animal Handling 13) for advantage plus **1d4 magical bludgeoning from fungal wood engraved into her fists**. ⚠️ **Confirmed on the record as an *ancient rothe run on the oryx stat block*** — Addison: "There's also no rothe stat block." This is the source of the campaign's contextual oryx/rothe convention.
- ~~[[Derendil]]~~ — **WRITTEN OUT (S21) — see Former / Departed Companions.** Quaggoth (formerly believed a polymorphed elf prince). Rescued (S13). **S16:** Sleep-"hunted" on all fours, returned with self-torn clothes and no memory; named his curse-wizard "Terrestor." **S17: identity debunked** by [[Sloopidoop]] — "no elf" but a chaos-deluded quaggoth, "no cure but the peace of death." Devoured the chuul, then **frenzied and dropped Kit to 0**; Slept by Aeolus and **bound** by Topsy/Turvy. Was **Captured/Restrained** (bound, asleep), +1 exhaustion. **S19:** woke mid-scene but flailing/non-functional, no memory of the past several hours; carried/supported by the party throughout. **S20: frenzied through the entire altar battle** — unable to tell friend from foe, slashed toad-Blarg (6) and a kuo-toa monitor, carved through multiple kuo-toa (killed C and I), ended the session **bloodied** amid the chaos. Active, unresolved intra-party danger.
- [[Sloopidoop]] — Kuo-toa scholar-priest of the [[Society of Brilliance]]; worships "knowledge"/the Sea Mother (not Lolth). **New transactional ally (S17):** struck a mutual-aid bargain (escort him to [[Sloobludop]] + help the village vs. the chaos, for knowledge and aid), identified the party's loot, gave a village-access bead. **S18:** stayed behind to hold off two more green hags so the party could escape via whirlpool — fate unconfirmed. **S19:** not present; his successor [[Ploop]] (current Archpriest) dispatches scouts to check on him after Kit mentions the hag encounter. ⭐ **S22: RETURNS ALIVE.** Found and extracted by [[Indigo]] from inside the fog, **"very, very weakened"** — he had evidently been taken by [[Nanny Plunk]] along with the merfolk leadership. Immediately spent himself again, **concentrating through his staff to hold the containment trap** of ice shards and glowing water bubbles on the surviving charmed merfolk. ⚠️ **He is kuo-toa, not merfolk** — [[Indigo]]'s relay groups him with them ("the High Priest of the merfolk as well as Sloopidoop"), but he is simply present alongside them. Do not record him as merfolk-affiliated. The S18 cliffhanger (he stayed behind to hold off two green hags) is now **resolved: he survived.**
- [[Clementine]] — [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s pet spider. Grew in size in the Feydark. Size/stats TBD by Addison. **S16:** Found "waiting" on Binks's shoulder after his arachnid dream. Via Speak with Animals: senses "The Master of Spiders" with Binks — "she knows him now."

In slaughter mode and unreachable. Parting line: *"All right, I guess I'll see you when I see you."* Likely heading to [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Blingdenstone]]. **No reunion plan** — Addison: "you don't know when or if you'll reconnect unless you… actively circle back."
- [[Hemeth]] — **Freed by the party (S20), separated (S21).** A duergar smuggler-trader who walked into the wrong town after the Deep Father faction rose ([[Binks Stonevein]], Insight 23); found **bound, gagged, bruised and queued for sacrifice** at the [[Altar of the Deep Father]]. **[[Darby Stonefeather]] Misty Stepped to him and worked the bindings across two turns** (Strength 11, then a strap ripped free with radiant-heated hands at Strength 10 with advantage) and removed the gag; **[[Topsy]] gnawed through the last cord on a natural 18.** He immediately began screaming third-person threats in an Undercommon-Dwarvish hybrid — *"When Hemeth gets his weapons back, you're all going to die"* — and **enlarged to Large size** duergar-fashion. ⚠️ **His confiscated weapons were never recovered.** Impressed by exactly one party member: [[Kit Aluri]], for stabbing a corpse. **S21:** ripped free of fresh bonds and ran overland northeast with [[Jimjar]]. Still carries a point of madness; froze and failed his save during the escape.
- [[Eldeth]] — Shield dwarf scout from Gauntlgrym. Departed through the Gauntlgrym Nexus Pool portal (S11) to warn the dwarven kings and bring reinforcements. Promised to return.
- [[Sarith Kzekarit]] — Drow prisoner. Escaped into pool during S02; told Kit to find him at [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Neverlight Grove]]

## Key Antagonists

- ~~[[Ilvara Mizzrym]]~~ — **DEAD (S13).** Drow priestess, Velkynvelve commander, primary antagonist. Shot by [[Jorlan Duskryn]]'s crossbow. Tackled from stalactite by [[Derendil]]. Killed by [[Roxanne]]'s gore attack (27 damage trample). Her own Insect Plague killed [[Shoor Vandree]]. Primary antagonist arc from S01 is closed.
- ~~[[Shoor Vandree]]~~ — **DEAD (S13).** Drow warrior, Ilvara's lieutenant/lover. Killed by Ilvara's own Insect Plague spell after she lost concentration control.
- ~~[[Asha Vandree]]~~ — **DEAD (S14).** Junior drow priestess. Attempted to cast Darkness but was stopped by Binks's Command (Halt). Killed by [[Darby Stonefeather]]'s Fire Mace attack of opportunity (20 damage, mace spike embedded in skull) when she tried to flee into magical darkness.
- ~~[[Jorlan Duskryn]]~~ — **DEAD (S14).** Scarred drow elite warrior, former Ilvara lieutenant. Turncoat confirmed across S01, S02, S13, and S14. Died in Kit's presence in the northern watchpost. Passed on lore: [[Drizzt Do'Urden]], [[Society of Brilliance]], and faith in young drow [[Artaxle]]. Gave Kit his bracers, swords, and lieutenant cloak.
- ~~Drow Elite (unnamed)~~ — **Surrendered (S14).** Persuaded by Kit's nat 20 Persuasion speech (with Binks translating). Dropped weapons and knelt. Ordered Garruk to stand down.
- [[Garruk]] — Drow crossbowman. Escaped Velkynvelve under cover of his own Darkness spell. Took [[Ilvara Mizzrym]]'s body and Shoor's riding lizard. Currently fleeing.
- [[Artaxle]] — Young drow from Velkynvelve. Jorlan's protégé. Eyes flash purple. Declined to join party; heading to [[Menzoberranzan]] with false intel to buy the party time. First appearance S14.
- The Corrupted Entity — Female, from the Abyss. A twisted reflection of [[Psilofyr]]. Seeks to control death and rot. Naos attempted to infiltrate her and is now in danger. Name unknown — Naos "dare not speak" it. Likely Zuggtmoy (do not metagame). (S11)
- **Lolth / "The Master of Spiders"** — Following Binks's shrine desecration (S14), her attention on him is confirmed (S16): dream-connection through arachnid eyes, "dormant, not that it went away." Per Clementine: "she knows him now." Not yet a direct appearance.
- ~~[[Bloppblippodd]]~~ ("Blop") — **DEAD (S20), killed by her own father.** Kuo-toa Archpriestess of the "Deep Father," [[Ploop]]'s daughter. A vision of a new god weeks before the party arrived drew away a large and growing faction of her father's Sea Mother worshippers, on **human and humanoid blood sacrifice** and **"chumming" the Darklake** for something unknown. She never appeared on screen in S19 — she sent the contingent that arrested her father as the session ended. **S20: she is the session.** She emerged from a hovel at the [[Altar of the Deep Father]] flanked by her whip [[Clib]]; a group Insight read her instantly as a **bloodthirsty sadist with full, perfect confidence** her vision would raise her to rule her people. [[Ploop]] struck her with his scepter mid-ritual to spring the party's ambush; she shielded herself, **lost a whole round groveling** to [[Blarg]]'s Command (natural 2), was crit by her father for ~30 lightning, and was battered twice by [[Amanita de'Champignon]] (19, then 24). 🛑 **She died in round 3 making one final call of "[[Limu Gugun]]!" — and the ritual succeeded anyway.** Addison was explicit that it worked because of everything that came *before* the ambush. Her death is the campaign's filicide beat: [[Kit Aluri]]'s quiet *"Does he have any other kids?"* is what lands the cost. (S19, **S20 — died**)
- ⚔️ **[[Limu Gugun]], "the Deep Father" — REVEALED AS [[Demogorgon]] (S20).** The name the cult gave the god they were calling out of the lake. For two sessions it was a distinct entity to the party; **[[Blarg]]'s Religion 26** made the connection as the thing surfaced (Kit 15, Darby 18, Amanita 8, Aeolus insufficient). ⚠️ Nothing establishes that [[Bloppblippodd]] ever knew what she was actually calling. ⚠️ Spelling ruling still pending — see that page.
- [[Clib]] — Kuo-toa whip, [[Bloppblippodd]]'s enforcer. **S20:** flanked her at the altar; **knocked prone into the altar** by [[Aeolus]]'s opening Eldritch Blast (22 to hit, 14 damage **plus a DM-granted bonus 10** for beating double his AC), **missed [[Ploop]] twice**, then **grappled [[Turvy]]** with his claw-scepter for 7 piercing. ⚠️ **His true name is unresolved** — Addison gave "Clip or Clib" on air, plus "clip, clip, clip, Dulegate." **Fate after S20 unrecorded**; [[Sloobludop]] was destroyed in S21. (S20)
- [[Glug]] — Kuo-toa "whip" (enforcer), [[Ploop]]'s son. Objected loudly to outsiders' involvement in Sloobludop's civil war and was overruled by his father (S19). ⚠️ **S20: asserted present but never shown.** Addison stated he was "engaged this whole time. Though I don't have him on the map." **S21:** last seen trying to pull his father away from [[Bloppblippodd]]'s body as the town fell. **Fate unrecorded.** (S19, S20, S21)
- **[[Demogorgon]] — Demon lord, the Prince of Demons. Campaign-scale threat.** ⭐ **He is [[Limu Gugun]], "the Deep Father"** — the kuo-toa cult's name for him all along, revealed only when [[Blarg]] rolled Religion 26 as he surfaced. Two heads named [[Aameul and Hethradiah]]. Summoned to the Material Plane and **risen from [[04-World-Lore/Locations/The Darklake]]** at the close of S20 — **the ritual succeeded even though [[Bloppblippodd]] died mid-invocation**; **S21: destroyed [[Sloobludop]].** Two heads, taller than a city tower; rolls fog ahead of itself; raised a tidal wave that nearly ended the party. One head turned on the party *through* fog and Pass Without Trace and reached with an "inordinately long" tentacle (Str DC 10 — [[Amanita de'Champignon]] and [[Turvy]] flung 30 ft, 10 ft under, 7 bludgeoning each). Forced party-wide **DC 12 Wisdom saves vs. madness** — everyone passed, several only by spending resources. **Visibly paused** by the golden shrine [[Kit Aluri]]'s prayer and [[Binks Stonevein]]'s duplicate raised against it. Still at large in the Darklake; warning the Underdark about it is now the party's stated goal number one. **S22: not present, but his released chaotic aura was powering [[Nanny Plunk]]** — the party felt "an echoing deep rumble" from him during the group madness check, and **the psychic faerzress link between him and Plunk was severed at her death.** Addison also framed him plainly for the table: *"This is not Stranger Things. Demogorgons are not beasts of the Upside Down, but a demon prince."* His lingering impact is the stated reason for the **dream sequences** that were set up and never run. (S20, S21, S22)
- ~~[[Nanny Plunk]]~~ — **DEAD (S22).** Green hag. Named by [[Ploop]] in S19, appeared S21 on the bone teeth, **killed by [[Kit Aluri]] in [[The Mermaid Cove]]** in S22. She had taken the cove from [[Indigo]]'s merfolk and was **converting charmed merfolk into new green hags**, needing a third to complete a coven; the storm ritual her thralls were raising would have put the whole party into a coma and turned them too. She disguised herself as [[Binks Stonevein]], went invisible and moved — and Kit killed her through the illusion on the strength of her own **Hunter's Mark**, overriding a **natural 1** Insight. Her death **severed the psychic faerzress link between her and [[Demogorgon]]** and collapsed the storm. ⚠️ **Not a closed problem:** the essence of her soul passed into a fleeing hag, and her S21 line — *"It is you"* — was **never explained.** [[Indigo]]'s "weird business relationship" with her also died with her, unexplained. (S19, S21, **S22**)
- **The Escaped Green Hag — ACTIVE, NEW (S22).** One of [[Nanny Plunk]]'s two converted-merfolk "sisters." When Plunk died, Addison ruled **the essence of her soul went into this hag and empowered it to escape**, fleeing **north up the coast**. Likely "another converted merfolk being made into a new Nanny Plunk." **The successor is already in motion.**
- **Merfolk Stormcallers, Wave Benders and Skirmishers — charmed thralls, contained (S22).** Not antagonists by choice: all confirmed **charmed victims** of [[Nanny Plunk]], recoverable, and the reason Addison issued his standing **non-lethal damage** ruling. One Wave Bender killed by [[Darby Stonefeather]] under madness; one Skirmisher killed by [[Topsy]] and [[Turvy]]. The survivors are stunned and held by [[Indigo]] and [[Sloopidoop]] at the top of the cove.

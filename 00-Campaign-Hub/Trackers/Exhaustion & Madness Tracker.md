## Rules Reference

**Exhaustion (2024 PHB — per Addison's S09 clarification)**
- Levels 1–6, cumulative. Each level: **−2 × level** to all d20 Tests (attacks, ability checks, saves — including death saving throws) and **−5 ft × level** Speed.
- **Level 6 = death.**
- A long rest (with food and water) removes **1 level**. This is NOT disadvantage (that was the 2014 rule).

**Madness (Out of the Abyss / DMG)**
- Three types: **short-term** (1d10 × 10 minutes), **long-term** (1d10 × 10 hours), **indefinite** (a lasting flaw until cured).
- Corrupted **faerzress** can trigger short-term madness (e.g., Amanita, S05).
- Addison tracks cumulative **madness points** per character (first applied S14). Track across sessions — points persist even after the triggering effect ends.
